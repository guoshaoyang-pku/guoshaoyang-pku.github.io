# Low-Budget XOR: When Last-LN Plain MLPs Beat Residual Rivals

## Answer and Scope
At the measured AdamW setting beta=(.9,.95), weight_decay=0, lr=3e-5, T=256, batch=32, last-LN d4 plain MLPs beat otherwise identical residual twins in 18/18 dim2/4 comparisons on two datasets, with 10 seeds per cell. They also win 6/6 at dim8. This supports a scoped exception, not "never deep plain," a universal residual bonus, or every condition with Delta<.05. At lr=3e-4 the corresponding counts fall to 2/18 and 1/6. The threshold itself was not located.
The fewer-LN C>B contrast transports to two allowed dim4 splits, but jointly matching B to C's width256/GELU reduces the large raw CE advantage to a near tie. Width and activation account for the transported gap; the original split's causal explanation remains underidentified. Final LN alone cannot distinguish C from B because both have it. C>A evidence must not be promoted into a complete ranking.

## Delta Calibration
K1001's Delta is a nominal cumulative-update proxy, not a measured displacement: Adam/AdamW/RMSprop approximately lr*T; Adagrad approximately 2*lr*sqrt(T); SGD approximately .01*lr*T/(1-momentum). SGD assumes raw gradient magnitude .01; Adagrad assumes accumulator growth with constant-magnitude gradients. Net parameter RMS displacement and cumulative path length are different quantities; neither is visible.
Under constant same-sign gradients, no decay and zero initial momentum, SGD's finite-step expression is .01*lr*[T/(1-m)-m*(1-m**T)/(1-m)**2]. At T256,m=.9,lr3e-4 this is .00741 rather than .00768. Ignoring epsilon, Adagrad's conditional exact sum is lr*sum(t**(-.5),t=1..T); at T256 its multiplier is 30.570885, not 32. At lr=.00024 this gives .007337, rather than .00768. Neither correction changes any audited .05 classification. These are exact conditional algebra, NOT exact empirical calibration; evolving/sign-changing gradients, normalization, decay and optimizer states remain unmeasured.

## C Versus B and A
At low lr, the two dim4 transports give C/B CE=.12403/.18337 and .11310/.17443: C-B=-.05933/-.06133, ratios .6764/.6484. Seed SDs for C/B are .00652/.00970 and .00698/.00847. Matching B to width256/GELU gives .12215/.11650: C-B becomes +.00188/-.00341, mixed-sign near ties.
Changing only B's width reduces CE by .04298/.03709; changing only ReLU to GELU reduces it by .02307/.02438; changing both reduces it by .06121/.05793. Thus neither factor alone accounts for the full transported gap, while their joint intervention does. At matched width192/GELU or width256/ReLU, C also loses narrowly to the corresponding B. This is stronger evidence than the activation-unmatched historical fewer-LN analogues, but does not prove the original split's causal attribution.
Turning only B's residual path off lowers CE by .03061/.02165 on the two low-lr splits, demonstrating an adverse residual interaction even at d5,count4. Extending C to d5 with three late LNs gives CE .11451/.10456, not deterioration in mean; this changes depth and layout jointly, so it does not isolate depth. Binary final LN, LN count alone, or a shallower-is-always-better explanation cannot account for all these controls.
C>A differences are -.06403/-.06611. Reducing C to width192 retains differences -.01928/-.02298, so width alone does not account for C>A. Moving A's two LNs from00110 to00011 actually worsens residual CE by .01884/.01737. This confirms that later LN is topology-dependent, not an automatic benefit.

## Optimizer and Schedule Controls
At matched nominal low/high proxies .00768/.0768, d4w192GELU LN0111 plain-minus-residual CE differences are SGD m0:-.00642/+.02977; SGD m.9:+.00123/+.01665; Adagrad:-.04179/+.00614. SGD low means are close relative to SD; they do not establish either winner confidently. The clearly negative Adagrad low contrast and tiny SGD contrasts do not validate an optimizer-invariant Delta calibration or the universal AdamW exception.
C/B schedule contrasts on the first dim4 split: T256,b32,lr3e-5 gives-.05933;T256,b16 gives-.06870;T512,b32,lr1.5e-5 (same nominal low proxy) gives-.06237;T512,b16,lr3e-5 (same8,192 samples as T256b32) gives-.04051. High proxy atT256,lr3e-4 gives-.00326 versusT512,lr1.5e-4 gives-.03888, with substantial SD. Equal low proxy retains a similar C/B gap, but proxy or sample total alone does not reproduce absolute CE. Shared budget is not a within-question architecture difference; schedule interactions remain possible and these coupled interventions do not separate every budget/noise effect.

## Predictions, Explanations and Boundaries
Preregistered P1 expected placement/count-dependent rather than universal plain advantage: supported for placement across early/late layouts, but its strongest non-universality prediction within the tested last-LN AdamW grid was not observed (24/24 low-lr mean wins). P2 predicted fewer-LN C>B would not reliably survive width/activation matching: supported by near ties on two splits. P3 predicted higher proxy would reduce placement advantage: supported by the mean contrasts, without locating a .05 transition. The additional forecast that dim8 would weaken the low-budget edge was not supported: its absolute mean gap was larger, not smaller.
Only means/SDs, not paired seed losses, are visible. A conservative approximate t-style halfwidth is 2.262*(SD_plain+SD_rival)/sqrt(10), allowing worst-case unknown seed covariance. It is not a multiplicity-adjusted significance test or a between-dataset interval. Raw C/B gaps have halfwidths .01161/.01106; matched gaps .01093/.01035. Last-LN low twin differences clear this bound in 4/6 dim2,12/12 dim4,5/6 dim8 cells; higher-lr dim2/4 cells clear neither direction, while three dim8 cells favor residual. Reported mean wins must not be read as universal statistically established wins.
Competing explanations: placement/topology interactions are directly intervened on; joint width/activation explains transported raw C/B gaps; residual/depth/layout effects also exist. Feature-scale attenuation, gradient flow, distractor overfitting and measured displacement are not distinguishable with only held-out CE. No training losses, gradients, confidence curves or representations were exposed. Original C>B remains underidentified despite useful transport evidence.
Unmeasured predictions for continuation: on a new allowed dim4 split at the same low AdamW setting, expect width/activation-matched C/B absolute mean difference <.02 and a larger raw gap; test these as separate preregistered contrasts. Predict weakened last-LN plain advantage at intermediate lr1e-4,T256 versus3e-5, but the sign is unresolved. Dim6 requires a newly allowed dataset; broader seeds, depths, optimizer settings, compute matching and a dense lr/T grid are required before claiming a boundary or mechanism.
