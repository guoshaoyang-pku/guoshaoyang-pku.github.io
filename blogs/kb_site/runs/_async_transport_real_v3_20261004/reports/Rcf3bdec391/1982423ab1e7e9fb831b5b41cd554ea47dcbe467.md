## Scope and unresolved questions
K1015's classification 'never deep plain' is refuted; K1006's residual+1 is not calibrated here. K1061's last-LN qualifier helps describe the interaction but does not prove universality across optimizers or dimensions. K1018 is restricted to>=8 features and its supplied usefulness-.06,n52 is not causal evidence. C>B remains unexplained pending factorial and target-adjacent measurements; both have final LN, so a binary final-LN explanation alone cannot distinguish them. Width, activation, depth, count/layout and sample budget confound the historical contrast. Only held-out CE is visible: train-test gaps, gradient norms, representations and measured movement are unavailable. New evidence and counterexamples will update this ongoing report.
