# When Sparse Normalization Invalidates Regression Depth Preference

## Finding
K1074’s displacement gate is not sufficient: matched normalized deep models can lose to shallow models. The supported exception depends on LN position, target and training regime, not simply “one LN + low-rate Adagrad + SiLU.” A universal last-LN rescue is contradicted. These experiments do not causally explain the width-confounded q_b57ff5 result.

## Sources and Definitions
Evidence comprises frozen training comments, K1074/K1006/K1001/K1019, allowed lab rows and controlled training. Answer fields are excluded from analysis and persisted history; an initial unrestricted history response included them, but they were not used as evidence. The motivating configuration and shallow-win observation come from the supplied research question and training comment. Its 8/16 screen across 15 sets and two historical SGD wins are observational, not matched causal cells. Refresh pinned `J4c2bf77e4459_research_e0002`; no later completed epoch was available.

Depth denotes lab hidden blocks after input projection. E/M/L/D mean early-only, middle-only, last-only and all-block LN. Depth4 patterns are `TFFF/FFTF/FFFT/TTTT`; depth5 patterns are `TFFFF/FFTFF/FFFFT/TTTTT`. Tail length is blocks after the last LN: depth5 E/M/L have 4/2/0. R is deep mean test MSE divided by the corresponding depth1 LN[T] mean, holding optimizer, activation and residual setting fixed. R>1 favors shallow. These are ratios of means, not means of seed ratios.

## Design and Coverage
Comparisons fix dataset, width96, default initialization policy, MSE, 256 steps, batch16 and wd1e-4. The lab protocol requests 10 seeds per condition. Paired losses, actual initial tensors and valid-seed counts are not exposed. Initialization policy is fixed, not identical tensors across unequal-depth models; parameter counts are not matched.

The full factorial contains 3 optimizers at lr3e-4 (Adagrad/Adam/plain SGD momentum0), 2 activations (SiLU/ReLU), 2 residual settings and 9 architectures: depth1 plus depth4/5 E/M/L/D. This gives 108 conditions per dataset. Two multivariate datasets were selected before replication outcomes; the univariate factorial was adaptively completed after its preregistered core revealed an early-LN reversal.

There are 360 primary conditions on 6 measured datasets: full factorials on `mvar_000e80`, `mvar_00ccf0`, `sym_018114`; 12-cell Adagrad-SiLU cores on `mvar_00d128`, `sym_00bebe`, `sym_02e07a`. Cores include plain depth1/depth4&5 E/M/L/D and residual depth1/depth5 M/L. Selection used sorted lab IDs; unavailable `sym_01a8a6` was retained as an unsuccessful attempt and replaced by the next ID. Seven datasets were attempted, six produced measurements, and four provide finite plain Adagrad-SiLU shallow denominators. Of 360 primary conditions, 351 have finite mean and SD; 287/318 deep comparisons have finite numerator and denominator.

An input-dimension mismatch produced 108 error conditions before uniform correction to input_dim4 on `mvar_00ccf0`. Three cross-family requests were rejected and rerun with a univariate base. These are not training evidence. A native-base diagnostic reproduced the second dataset’s shallow Infinity and approximately its depth5 M mean (1.485881 versus 1.485039). Non-finite aggregates are retained, never converted into zero ratios. Non-finite and zero-SD aggregates require per-seed diagnostics before strong inference.

## Prediction Tests
1. **Universal normalized-depth preference fails.** Early-only plain SiLU loses at both depths on `sym_018114` and the independently selected replacement `sym_02e07a`. Middle-only wins on all four datasets with finite plain Adagrad denominators, although the nearly tied `mvar_000e80` cell is not a robust advantage. A general low-Adagrad middle-LN exception matching q_b57ff5 is not demonstrated.
2. **Monotone last-LN rescue fails.** Depth5 L/M mean-loss ratios are 1.002, 1.058, 1.096, 1.309, 4.523 and 1.873 respectively for the six table datasets. Moving LN later can worsen loss. This changes both prefix and tail, so it does not isolate trailing length alone.
3. **Density helps the tested low-rate Adagrad-SiLU cells.** Depth5 dense/last ratios are .528, .640, .666, .691, .0187 and .202 respectively. This is not universal across optimizers: primary Adam plain SiLU dense is worse than middle (.71791 versus .66744).
4. **Residual protection is conditional.** On `sym_018114`, Adagrad-SiLU early depth5 R changes from 2.270 plain to .196 residual. On `mvar_000e80`, Adam-SiLU last changes from 1.105 to .466. Conversely, `sym_018114` SGD-SiLU middle still loses with residual (R1.238), and `mvar_00ccf0` SGD-SiLU residual last has R1.049.
5. **Activation moderates position effects.** On `sym_018114`, Adagrad plain ReLU depth5 E/M/L/D ratios are 1.339/.158/.0660/.00359, versus SiLU 2.270/.0881/.398/.00745. Early-only failure is therefore not exclusive to smooth activations. On `mvar_000e80` Adam, ReLU last R.590 contrasts with SiLU1.105.

## Optimizer Versus Exposure
Equal learning rates imply different proxy displacement: Adagrad .0096, Adam .0768, SGD .000768. A preregistered follow-up used Adagrad3e-4, Adam3.75e-5 and SGD3.75e-3 to equalize the K1001/K1019 proxy at .0096, not actual movement. Thirty-six follow-up conditions cover three datasets; repeated Adagrad anchors are not independent replications.

On `mvar_000e80`, calibrated depth5 M/L/D ratios are Adagrad(.995,.997,.527), Adam(.994,.996,.538), SGD(.979,.994,.518). Reducing Adam’s rate shrinks its last-only gap from Adagrad, as predicted; higher-rate Adam had (.458,1.105,.493).

On `mvar_00d128`, calibrated M/L/D ratios are Adagrad(.820,.898,.599), Adam(.821,.916,.610), SGD(1.156,1.614,1.025). SGD still loses, so matching the proxy is insufficient. On adaptively tested `sym_018114`, calibrated E/M/L ratios are Adagrad(2.270,.0881,.398), Adam(2.140,.0863,.397), SGD(4.507,4.158,3.944). Higher-rate Adam’s early ratio was .0241. The early-only reversal is reproducible under low-rate Adam, not Adagrad-specific; SGD dynamics and inaccurate gradient calibration remain competing explanations.

## Uncertainty and Historical Support
Marginal SD cannot recover paired difference variance. A delta-method SE upper bound under arbitrary seed covariance is `(SDdeep + R*SDshallow)/(sqrt(10)*meanshallow)`. Illustrative R±2.262×bound ranges are [1.812,2.729] for `sym_018114` Adagrad early depth5 and [1.027,1.183] for `mvar_000e80` Adam last depth5. These are approximations, not exact or multiple-comparison-adjusted confidence intervals. Sparse primary Adagrad differences around one are comparable to seed variation. Sorted synthetic targets do not support population-frequency estimates.

The strict historical audit excludes controlled `exp:` rows, excluded candidates and any failed seeds; compares only within set; requires MSE, deep≥4 with ≥1LN, deep LN count≥shallow count, and shallow≤2. It yields 1,262 observational comparisons and **zero** pairs jointly matched on width, activation, residual, initialization, optimizer dictionary, budget and loss. Above the displacement-ratio gate, deep wins uni328/396 across75 datasets and multi297/394 across91; below it, deep wins55/272 and56/200. These nuisance-confounded counts do not reproduce K1074’s quoted380/461 and323/435. Including partially failed, non-excluded candidates gives331/399 and317/427 above the gate, still different.

The exact unpooled support domain is `support_cells.json`: 790 above-gate comparisons with dataset/set/candidate IDs and both full configurations, displacement estimates, ratios and SDs. `observational_pairs.csv` retains all1,262; `descriptive_strata.json` is explicitly pooled description only. No matched historical low-displacement smooth single-LN cell supports the motivating exception.

## Interpretation and Remaining Tests
Loss measurements support position×target×training-regime interactions and useful dense normalization in the tested low-exposure cells. They do not establish attenuation: activation RMS, gradient norms, Jacobians and actual parameter displacement were not measured. Conditioning, representation, effective learning rate, initialization and target scale remain alternatives.

Scope is width96/default-init/noiseless symbolic regression/256×16/MSE/wd1e-4 and the listed rates. Width192, the exact q_b57ff5 dataset and longer training remain untested. There are three measured datasets per regression family, but only two multivariate and one adaptively completed univariate full factorials. No generic shallow preference or mandatory last-LN rescue is warranted.

Future falsifiable tests should record paired losses and actual movement; attenuation predicts stronger activation/gradient decay in losing early-only plain cells and reduced decay with dense LN or residuals. Holding a normalized prefix fixed while adding trailing plain blocks would isolate tail length: a pure tail-attenuation account predicts increasing loss with longer tails. Width192 and T2048 tests should assess whether extra exposure removes the early-only deficit. Failed predictions should narrow or reject the mechanism.
