# Sparse normalization and regression depth

Status: initial preregistration; no new controlled results yet. Source question q_b57ff5 motivates a width-confounded observation (plain SiLU depth5 width96 LN FFTFF versus depth1 width192 LN T, Adagrad 0.0003, 256 steps, batch16, wd0.0001). Its training comment reports shallow wins in 8/16 screened one-LN comparisons across 15 sets, none matching the low-displacement smooth-activation regime; the two historical shallow wins used SGD. These are observations, not matched causal cells. Evaluation answers are not research evidence.

Primary test: hold lab dataset, width96, budget256/batch16, initialization defaults, MSE and wd0.0001 fixed; cross depth1/4/5 with early/middle/last single LN and dense LN, residual false/true, SiLU/ReLU, and Adagrad/Adam/SGD at lr0.0003. Each condition uses the lab's 10-seed protocol. Report ratio of deep mean test MSE to shallow mean test MSE, marginal seed SD, and dataset counts. Optimizers at equal learning rate are not equal measured displacement.

Predictions recorded before experiments: a tail attenuation explanation predicts moving the only LN from middle to last improves plain deep SiLU at fixed LN count, especially at depth5, and residual connections reduce the tail penalty. A density explanation predicts dense LN improves even over last-only LN. An optimizer-specific explanation predicts the depth reversal and LN-position effect differ across optimizers. A universal depth preference predicts ratios below one for every normalized deep cell; any reliably adverse cell falsifies that universal version. No activation/gradient attenuation measurements are currently available, so attenuation remains a hypothesis even if these loss predictions hold.

Audit plan: enumerate K1074-eligible pairs within set only, stratify family, optimizer, activation, residual, LN count and tail, and separately enumerate exact nuisance-matched depth comparisons. Preserve row identifiers and scripts. Do not pool these counts into causal support. Dataset replication will use outcome-blind lab IDs. Uncertainty is seed variation rather than dataset-population uncertainty; paired seed differences cannot be recovered from marginal SD alone.

Unresolved: K1074 displacement calibration, initialization matching across unequal parameter shapes, target-specific simplicity, and whether LN alters optimization or representation. Findings will be scoped to the measured lab cells; no general exception is justified yet.
