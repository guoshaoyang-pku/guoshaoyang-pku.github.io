# When Sparse Normalization Invalidates Regression Depth Preference
## Finding
K1074's displacement gate is not sufficient: matched normalized deep models can lose to shallow models. The supported exception is conditional on LN position, target and training regime, not simply “one LN + low-rate Adagrad + SiLU.” A universal last-LN rescue is contradicted. The particular width-confounded q_b57ff5 result is not causally explained by these experiments.

## Prediction Tests and Factorial Interactions
1. Universal normalized-depth preference fails: early-only plain SiLU loses at both depths on sym_018114 and the independently selected replacement sym_02e07a. Middle-only wins on all4 datasets with finite plain Adagrad denominators; the nearly tied mvar_000e80 cell is not a robust advantage. There is no demonstrated general low-Adagrad middle-LN exception matching q_b57ff5.
2. Monotone last-LN/tail rescue fails: at depth5, L/M mean ratios are1.002,1.058,1.096,1.309,4.523,1.873 respectively for the six table datasets. Shortening the tail by moving LN later can worsen loss. This intervention also changes the unnormalized prefix; it does not isolate tail length alone.
3. Density benefit holds strongly for low-rate Adagrad-SiLU in these matched cells: depth5 dense/last mean ratios are0.528,0.640,0.666,0.691,0.0187,0.202 respectively. It is not universal across optimizers: primary Adam plain SiLU dense is worse than middle (.71791 vs.66744). LN count is not a sufficient explanation everywhere.
4. Residual protection is conditional. On sym_018114 Adagrad-SiLU early depth5 R changes2.270 plain to0.196 residual; on mvar_000e80 Adam-SiLU last R changes1.105 to0.466. Conversely sym_018114 SGD-SiLU middle still loses with residual (R1.238 vs2.026 plain), and mvar_00ccf0 SGD-SiLU residual last still has R1.049. No guaranteed residual rescue follows.
5. Activation moderates the position effect. At sym_018114 Adagrad, plain ReLU depth5 E/M/L/D ratios are1.339/.158/.0660/.00359 versus SiLU2.270/.0881/.398/.00745: early-only failure is not exclusive to smooth activations. On mvar_000e80 Adam, ReLU last R.590 contrasts with SiLU1.105. Full dataset-specific factorial cells are preserved; these are not pooled win counts.

## Optimizer Versus Training Exposure
At equal lr, core proxy displacement is Adagrad.0096,Adam.0768,SGD.000768; optimizer identity is confounded with exposure. A preregistered follow-up used Adagrad3e-4,Adam3.75e-5,SGD3.75e-3 to equalize the K1001/K1019 proxy at.0096, not actual movement.36 follow-up conditions on3 datasets include repeated Adagrad anchors; they are not independent replications.
On mvar_000e80, depth5 M/L/D ratios become Adagrad(.995,.997,.527),Adam(.994,.996,.538),SGD(.979,.994,.518). Reducing Adam's rate shrinks its last-only gap from Adagrad, as predicted; higher-rate Adam had M/L/D(.458,1.105,.493).
On mvar_00d128, calibrated M/L/D ratios are Adagrad(.820,.898,.599),Adam(.821,.916,.610),SGD(1.156,1.614,1.025). SGD still loses, so matched proxy exposure is insufficient. On adaptively tested sym_018114, calibrated E/M/L are Adagrad(2.270,.0881,.398),Adam(2.140,.0863,.397),SGD(4.507,4.158,3.944). Higher-rate Adam's E ratio was.0241. Thus the early-only reversal is reproducible under low-rate Adam, not Adagrad-specific; SGD dynamics or inaccurate gradient calibration remain competing explanations.

## Uncertainty and Historical Support
Marginal SD cannot recover paired difference variance. A delta-method SE upper bound under arbitrary seed covariance is (SDdeep+R*SDshallow)/(sqrt(10)*meanshallow). Illustrative R±2.262*bound ranges are[1.812,2.729] for sym_018114 Adagrad early depth5 and[1.027,1.183] for mvar_000e80 Adam last depth5. These are Gaussian/delta approximations, not exact or multiple-comparison-adjusted confidence intervals; failed-seed counts and pairing are unavailable. Sparse primary Adagrad differences around1 are comparable to seed variation. Sorted synthetic targets do not support population-frequency estimates.
Strict historical audit excludes exp: rows, excluded candidates and any failed seeds; compares only within set; requires MSE,deep>=4,>=1LN,deep LN count>=shallow count,shallow<=2. It yields1262 observational comparisons and ZERO pairs jointly matched on width,activation,residual,initialization,optimizer dictionary,budget and loss. With proxy displacement ratio>=1/3, deep wins uni328/396 over75 datasets and multi297/394 over91; below the gate deep wins55/272 and56/200. These correlated, nuisance-confounded counts are descriptive only and do not reproduce K1074's quoted380/461 and323/435. Including partially failed but non-excluded candidates gives331/399 and317/427 above the gate; still not the quoted tallies. SGD calibration, particularly against unnormalized shallow rivals, is an assumption.
The exact unpooled support domain is support_cells.json:790 above-gate comparisons with dataset/set/candidate IDs and both full model,optimizer,budget,loss/init configurations, displacement estimates, ratios and SDs. observational_pairs.csv retains all1262; descriptive_strata.json is explicitly only a pooled description. No low-displacement smooth single-LN matched historical cell supports the motivating exception.

## Interpretation, Boundaries and Remaining Tests
Measured loss supports position×target×training-regime interactions and useful dense normalization in the tested low-exposure cells. It does not identify attenuation: no activation RMS, gradient norms, Jacobians or actual parameter displacement were measured. Conditioning, feature representation, effective learning rate, initialization and target scale can produce these outcomes. LN placement changes both prefix and tail; residual and activation alter both optimization and representation.
Scope is width96/default-init/noiseless symbolic regression/256×16/MSE/wd1e-4 and the listed optimizers/rates. Width192, the exact q_b57ff5 dataset, longer training and other datasets remain untested here. Independent dataset counts are3 multivariate and3 univariate measured, but only2 multivariate and1 adaptively completed univariate full factorials. No generic shallow preference, mandatory last-LN rescue or established signal-attenuation mechanism is warranted.
Future falsifiable tests: record paired losses and actual parameter motion; measure pre/post-block activation and gradient scales, predicting stronger decay in the losing early-only plain cells and its reduction with dense LN/residuals if attenuation is causal. Separately hold a normalized prefix fixed while adding/removing trailing plain blocks: a pure tail-attenuation account predicts increasing tail length worsens loss. Test width192 and T2048 on the specified early-loss cells: an optimization-speed account predicts exposure reduces the deficit. Failure of these predictions should narrow or reject the mechanism, not be absorbed into an explanation.
