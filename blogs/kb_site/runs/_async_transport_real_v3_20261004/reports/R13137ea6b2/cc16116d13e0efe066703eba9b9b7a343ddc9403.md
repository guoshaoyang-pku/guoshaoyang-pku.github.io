# Conditional long-budget high-rate GRU reversal

## Checkpoint 1: frozen evidence and preregistration
Research question: when does GRU Adam/AdamW lr=.003 lose to genuinely weak transformer rivals, rather than merely to a better-tuned GRU?

Source q_7dbb0e (history epoch 1, ranking_v2) has one fixed stochastic bigram law, V=24, L=24, alpha=.8, 800 train and 200 held-out windows, sequence seed 9001847266482 and table seed 9001847276482. All choices use T=1024, batch=32 (32768 sampled windows), 10 initialization/training seeds. Observed ranking D<E<A<B<C: D is GRU64x2 AdamW .0001, beta2=.95, wd=.001; C same GRU at .003; A transformer64x1 plain SGD .003; B transformer32x3 Adagrad .00003; E transformer64x2 AdamW .0003. This is ordinal evidence only, not a measured CE difference or proof of divergence.

The frozen lab contains 1880 bigram candidates across 407 sets: 1862 at T=256 and 18 at T=512, none at T=1024/2048. Prior comments report high-rate wins in 18/18 weak-rival comparisons over three T=256 sets, but opposite same-GRU 1024-step outcomes at alpha=1.0 and 1.4. Those comparisons must be reconstructed rather than interpreted as an alpha threshold.

Predictions recorded before new experiments: (1) GRU64x2 AdamW beta2=.95 wd=.001 at .003 should often beat weak rivals at T=256, but its CE should worsen relative to .0001-.001 at T=1024 on at least some materialized 800-window datasets. (2) A same-GRU rate reversal need not cross the much worse weak-rival CE. (3) Alpha alone will not identify a reliable reversal boundary. (4) Finite held-out CE and zero failed seeds would argue against catastrophic numerical divergence, but would not distinguish overconfidence, irrelevant-history fitting, or optimization jitter.

Planned controlled sweep: rates 3e-5,1e-4,3e-4,.001,.003 at T=256/1024, batch32 and seeds0..9 matched within each allowed materialized lab dataset; transformer64x1 SGD .003 momentum0 and transformer32x3 Adagrad3e-5, with q_7dbb0e architecture/decay controls. Follow-up beta2, decay, width/depth and T=2048 contrasts will be conditional on lab support. No synthetic unapproved datasets or hidden training results will be used.

Uncertainty: seed variability is conditional on one fixed train/test split and transition law; it is not uncertainty over laws. Aggregate mean/std cannot yield paired-seed confidence intervals without covariance. Budget comparisons at fixed batch change samples seen and reuse; context and vocabulary comparisons across different materializations are confounded by transition/sequence seeds. Mechanism remains unresolved until calibration, train CE, history perturbation or trajectory evidence is visible.
