## Definitions and methods

Loss is held-out 10-seed mean test CE or MSE, lower better. Experiments use seeds 0–9. Standard deviations describe seed variability, not uncertainty of a paired difference. Aggregate measurements lack seed covariance, and pairs share candidates and sets; pair counts are not independent trials.

Comparisons are restricted to one `set_id`. The displacement proxy Δ is `lr*T` for Adam/AdamW/RMSprop, `2*lr*sqrt(T)` for Adagrad, and `lr*T*g/(1-m)` for SGD. Following the existing calibration, `g=.03` for momentum-.9 regression and `.01` otherwise. This is assumed calibration, not measured parameter displacement. Exposure `E=total_samples_seen/train_rows` counts nominal passes under replacement sampling.

Saved artifacts include source prompts and claims, 48 comparison-set snapshots, reproducible analysis scripts, pair-level results, preregistered predictions and three experiment records. CE and MSE were not combined into a common effect estimate.

## K1040: Local decay evidence, not a universal ranking

The original KB reports plain d2 w48 ReLU controls: AdamW wins 12/12 positive-decay comparisons, with Adam CE around .003 at wd=.001 and .044 at wd=.01 versus AdamW ≤.0002. Original experiment artifacts were unavailable for independent reconstruction. This supports a local observation, not arbitrary architectures, beta2 values, or wider learning-rate, decay and budget intervals.

`q_6a81f1` uses a 1.5-turn spiral, point seed1534072256, 1024/2048 train/test rows, T1024 bs32, default-init plain d1 w256 GELU without LN, lr=.003 and wd=.001. AdamW beta2=.999 is compared with Adam beta2=.95: optimizer and beta2 change together. The third candidate uses Adagrad lr=.0001 wd=.0001. Its split is absent from the measured lab. This prompt cannot isolate decay; `q_682517` supplies no visible evidence.

The pre-experiment lab contains two same-model Adam/AdamW spiral pairs, both plain d2 w48 LeakyReLU with two LN at E4. They change AdamW lr=.0001 wd=.001 to Adam lr=.0003 wd=.0001. Adam has lower CE in both, but these are not matched decay counterexamples.

Before testing transfer, I predicted an AdamW advantage at positive decay on a residual d1 w96 GELU+LN model, with equality at zero decay. Experiment `32c7405bbb55` uses `spiralcls_00f3c5`, instance81196798, 1.5 turns, 1024/2048 rows, default initialization, lr=.003, T1024 bs32, beta1=.9. Only optimizer type, beta2 and decay vary.

The transfer prediction is supported in these four comparisons. It does not establish arbitrary-architecture or arbitrary-beta2 dominance. Absolute CE differences matter more than ratios near zero; these results do not measure corresponding accuracy gains. They identify a decay-treatment difference, not gradient domination, logit-margin dynamics or an inevitable CE floor.

## K1009: Δ does not identify completed learning

`q_b0ae9d` is an 8-feature piecewise boundary, active coordinates0/7, threshold0, point seed1490966472, 1024/2048 rows. All choices use RMSprop lr3e-5 wd0, T256 bs64: **E16 and Δ=.00768**, not Δ≥.1.

Its default-init choices are B d5 w128 residual LeakyReLU with final LN; A d2 w256 residual SiLU without LN; D d3 w192 residual SiLU with middle LN; C d2 w256 plain GELU with first-block LN; E d4 w192 plain SiLU with two middle LN. Its split is absent from measured lab. No ranking was inferred from its evaluation answer. It cannot isolate depth or validate the .1 threshold.

The lab audit finds 42 relevant depth comparisons across smooth-additive, sparse-interaction and piecewise rules. None is a pure depth change. At E4 and Δ≥.1, shallow wins piecewise2/2, smooth0/2 and sparse2/2, each on two sets. Median shallow-minus-deep CE differences are −.05086, +.02547 and −.18054 respectively. These sparse, confounded strata do not estimate a universal threshold.

A high-Δ counterexample is `stabcls_a1889f/set_4096_var_var_fix_f14d2c`: AdamW .003 beta2=.999 wd0, T256 bs16, E4, Δ=.768. `c_b074b6`, d4 w64 plain LeakyReLU with two LN, CE .223616±.027725, loses to `c_873f6b`, d5 w64 residual ReLU with two LN, .191277±.024087. Residual and activation differ, so this challenges the blanket prescription without identifying a depth effect.

K1033 reports reversal below .1 at E32 under limited smooth/sparse controls, confounding depth with width/LN. K1073 reports rule/exposure-dependent optima on one shallow ReLU+LN architecture, with batch/exposure confounding. They limit K1009’s scope; they do not independently establish overconfidence. Completed boundary learning, confident errors and distractor fitting require training loss, accuracy, logits or calibration measurements absent here.

## Narrow Solver-Facing Text

**K1040:** “Matched spiral CE twins only: prefer AdamW over coupled Adam in tested lr=.003, T1024, wd=.001/.01 cells; do not extrapolate to arbitrary architectures or beta2.”

**K1038:** “Deep zero-LN SGD: check family, residual path, activation and raw lr/momentum before ranking by calibrated Delta; pooled endpoints do not establish freezing.”

**K1009:** “1024-row Gaussian tabular CE: compare architectures conditional on rule, exposure and optimizer; Delta≥.1 is not proof the boundary is learned or a universal shallow/wide prescription.”

Submitted revisions retain local observations and replace asserted failure mechanisms with hypotheses. No unrelated claims were merged or deleted.

## Remaining Tests and Boundaries

The preregistered spiral transfer prediction was supported locally; the momentum-SGD improvement prediction failed. The tabular check used existing measurements, not a new prospective experiment.

Exact optimizer/beta2/decay controls distinguish decay effects from beta2 differences locally. Broader spiral claims still require independent splits, architectures, turns and lr/T/wd ranges. Gradient attenuation versus miscalibrated optimization requires layerwise activation/gradient norms, parameter displacement and training trajectories. Tabular overconfidence versus underfitting requires matched architectures, fixed-batch exposure variation, and train/test CE, accuracy and calibration for each rule.

The shared “first 200 characters” failure hypothesis remains a plausible wording risk. Utility causality requires randomized same-question solver replay comparing original wording, scoped wording and noKB retrieval. That was not performed. The evidence supports conservative scope corrections, not manufactured utility causality or a completed mechanistic theory.
