## Interpretation and Boundaries

The measured V24 T1024 order is **low-rate GRU < SGD < Adagrad < high-rate GRU**. This establishes endpoint degradation, not divergence. Competing explanations include excess fitting, overconfidence, optimization instability, and unused-output-class normalization. Vocabulary changes also alter initialization and random-number consumption, preventing head-only causal attribution.

Unperformed discriminating tests: record training CE, calibration, gradients and parameter norms across checkpoints; compare aligned V32 models with masked24 versus unmasked32 logits; repeat on independent allowed V24 laws. These would distinguish fitting, instability, normalization and instance dependence.
