# Condition-first audit of K1018, K1009, K1038 and K1040

Checkpoint 1: frozen load_history/load_kb/load_lab read. Visible history has 833 records, including four completed positive-epoch records (epochs 1-2). Citation exposure is sparse: K1018 two records (q_917f30 and q_a504f7), K1009 one (q_b0ae9d), K1038/K1040 none. No explicit retrieval logs are exposed by load_history; absence of citation is not absence of retrieval. User-provided utility deltas (-0.06/n52, -0.06/n22, -0.03/n68, -0.12/n16) describe usage, not truth calibration.

Initial diagnosis: K1018 explicitly limits feature count but describes a global flip that conflicts with its residual-depth refinement K1065; K1009 bundles width, depth, activation and LN and says XOR/spiral do not reverse, inconsistent with K1018's restricted XOR reversal. K1038 pools across datasets/optimizers and treats deep unnormalized SGD as near-frozen without controlling activation, width or residuals. K1040 has controlled evidence but may be applied to non-twins or unfit spirals. These are hypotheses, not conclusions.

Methods planned: compare ten-seed mean held-out selection loss only within set_id and exact common dataset, budget, init, loss and nuisance settings. Isolate one architecture factor at a time; for optimizer comparisons hold architecture/lr/T constant and explicitly state changed optimizer fields. Stratify XOR features, residual and delta; tabular rule and sample exposure; SGD norm/depth/residual; spiral turns and convergence. Report counts, sets, median ratios and non-independent uncertainty rather than pooled accuracy. Predeclare predictions before controlled experiments. Preserve measured evidence and distinguish reported historical experiments from newly executed measurements. Full reproducible code and analysis will be saved in the research repo.
