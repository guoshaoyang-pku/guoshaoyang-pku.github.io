## Definitions and Matching
Outcome is lower 10-seed mean held-out CE or MSE, not classification accuracy. Delta is the existing proxy: lr*T for Adam/AdamW/RMSprop; 2*lr*sqrt(T) for Adagrad; g*lr*T/(1-m) for SGD, with g=.03 for momentum-.9 MSE regression and .01 otherwise. Neither true parameter displacement nor gradient magnitude is measured. E=total_samples_seen/train_rows under replacement sampling.

Every measured comparison is within one set_id, with dataset/split, loss, default initialization and budget fixed. Architecture-factor audits additionally match the complete optimizer and all other model fields. LN comparisons change the whole layout; count cannot be separated from placement. New depth controls use zero LN, fixing normalization as depth changes. Optimizer comparisons hold architecture/data/budget/decay fixed, with lr or optimizer treatment differences explicitly specified. Different widths are not parameter/FLOP matched.

Saved pre-test pair manifests define the initial audit cohort; they are not pooled with this investigation's controls. All nonfinite means/SDs and failed/excluded candidates are excluded from accuracy denominators. Earlier invalid Adam configurations with inherited SGD momentum were not counted as SGD wins. Counts share candidates and datasets and are descriptive, not independent Bernoulli trials.

## Uncertainty, Explanations and Boundaries
Only aggregate seed means/SDs are visible. For a difference use descriptive halfwidth 2.262*(SD_a+SD_b)/sqrt(10), allowing worst-case unknown covariance. It is approximate, not multiplicity-adjusted or between-dataset uncertainty. Original independent-SE/Wilson columns in the saved live-audit output are not inferential evidence; focused.py implements the conservative bound. No pooled cross-family accuracy or CE/MSE effect estimate is reported.

Competing explanations include underfitting at the nominal threshold, LN-placement/topology interaction, optimizer/gradient-scale miscalibration, and confidence/distractor overfitting after fitting. Held-out endpoints distinguish some ranking prescriptions, not these mechanisms. No train CE, gradients, logits, parameter trajectories or calibration curves are exposed. The literal tests contradict universal thresholds even if a near-converged mechanism remains plausible. K1056's activation tendency and K1073's rule/exposure dependence should remain separate from K1009's depth bundle; E comparisons here also change lr/T and cannot isolate an epoch effect.

Next unmeasured predictions: at the same dim8 XOR architecture and higher AdamW lr=.003/T256, predict the depth advantage weakens relative to .0003, without committing to a reversal. On an independent allowed smooth_additive split, predict d3/d5 versus d1 is not uniformly shallow-favored at Delta=.1536. On a second allowed regression split, SGD's LN interaction may change sign, so predict no universal zero-LN penalty. For 3-turn spiral twins, predict a larger absolute decay penalty only if zero-wd CE actually reaches <.01; convergence is not to be inferred from Delta. Test these with matched widths/topologies, fixed data/init, and separate lr/T/batch grids. Mechanistic tests additionally require currently unavailable training/gradient/logit measurements. Utility improvement requires randomized same-question original/scoped/noKB replay; it was not measured.
