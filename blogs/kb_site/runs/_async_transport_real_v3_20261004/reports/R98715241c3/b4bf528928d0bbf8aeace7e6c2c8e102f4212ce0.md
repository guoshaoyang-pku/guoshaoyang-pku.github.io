# Condition-first repair of K1006, K1015 and K1022

## Scope and Data
This is a solver-utility repair, not a general shallow/plain reversal. Only frozen training records and the allowed lab were used. Initial publication: R98715241c3. Checkpoint refresh pinned e0002 (J17a37d1b0838_research_e0002); history remained 833 records with no new question IDs. Lab contains 12,642 candidates, 1,999 sets and 1,848 datasets; 10,622 MLP candidates have init=default. No new training experiment was necessary or run. q_b57ff5 and q_1e921c were not reinvestigated.

The user-supplied K1006 training utility is -0.07 over 33 uses. Visible explicit citations cover only eight records, mean score .28125; this is not utility or a reconstruction of n=33. The three spiral citations score 0, .25, 0; q_ca0dea scores 0 and q_a504f7 .5. Other cited cases include the two separate investigations. Negative utility therefore remains an attributed aggregate, while the direction of bias is directly visible.

## Definitions and Reproducibility
Depth means repeated hidden blocks after the input projection. A normalized last block is pre-LN before its hidden Linear, not normalization after the activation or on the output head. Residual blocks compute act(x+Linear(norm(x))); plain blocks compute act(Linear(norm(x))). All named questions use PyTorch Linear defaults and ten seeds 0..9. Classification records have 1,024 train/2,048 test rows; q_ca0dea has 256/256 rows. Prior controlled results below are recorded comments, not experiments rerun here; their exact initialization/seed pairing cannot be recovered from those comments alone.

Calibrated delta follows K1001/K1019: Adam/AdamW/RMSprop lr*T; Adagrad 2*lr*sqrt(T); SGD lr*T*g/(1-m), g=.01 except momentum-.9 regression g=.03. It is a proxy, not measured parameter motion. The .03 placement observation is raw lr*T in the quoted Adam-family stratum, not an interchangeable SGD/Adagrad threshold. Equality .05 belongs to the higher band in our reanalysis; q_a504f7 describes its historical split as below/above .05.

research/history_audit.py parses candidates and identifiable ordinal pairs; single-answer records do not order two losing choices. research/additive_audit.py checks the literal LN+residual-minus-tail score, without activation/depth tie-breaks or overrides. research/audit.py matches within one set on model, full optimizer, budget, loss and initialization, varying only placement, LN count, residual or activation. It excludes nonfinite/nonpositive means and failed/excluded candidates. research/xor_lab_audit.py deliberately uses the looser optimizer-type/lr matching reported for XOR; width, activation and LN placement can remain confounded. Pair-level JSON, summaries, five complete named records, frozen KB, compact history and complete family-split lab snapshots are saved. research/reproduce.py reruns the lab and additive audits offline from these snapshots and frozen parsed candidates; history_audit.py requires the pinned KB history to reparse all original questions. Never compare losses across sets.

## Diagnosis and Measured Results
K1006's 74/110 pooled arch-only hits (spiral 12/16, tabular 17/32) are historical predictive associations, not effects of adding LN or residuals. Its first sentence asserts a universal score before its overrides. K1015's NEVER instruction contradicts K1061 and terminal-LN XOR evidence. K1022 treats heterogeneous families as sharing a .1 boundary and subordinates placement to the very additive hierarchy that failed.

Literal additive-score ordinal accuracy in current architecture-only history, at delta<.1 / >=.1 respectively:
| Family | Below .1: correct/ordered pairs (questions) | At least .1 |
|---|---|---|
| Univariate regression | 224/274 (54) | 25/45 (12) |
| Multivariate regression | 294/362 (57) | 60/84 (16) |
| Spiral | 195/244 (46) | 77/101 (21) |
| XOR | 211/260 (40) | 27/96 (14) |
| Tabular | 241/296 (57) | 38/101 (17) |
These are not independent trials, full-ranking hit rates, a replication of 74/110, or utility estimates. They show why deleting all architecture guidance would lose useful signal, and why high-delta XOR/tabular require separate gates. Regression retains LN/depth evidence; spiral has local interaction failures despite reasonable pooled ordinal accuracy.

Strict matched lab, delta<.1: more-LN wins univariate 89/105 (16 sets, 5 datasets; median MSE ratio .313), multivariate 124/141 (22 sets, 4 datasets; .732), XOR 100/167 (16 sets, 6 datasets; CE .947). Splitting residual status, more-LN wins plain/residual uni 67/75 and 22/30; multi 74/81 and 50/60; XOR 42/70 and 58/97. Residual twins win uni 18/26 (9 sets, 3 datasets; .780), multi 31/37 (13 sets, 3 datasets; .958), XOR 44/77 (20 sets, 4 datasets; .971). These unequal effect magnitudes are incompatible with a fixed +1 residual equivalence to an extra LN. At delta>=.1 more-LN wins uni 218/330 (44 sets, 4 datasets; .552), but multi only 0/2 and spiral 0/2: those tiny latter cells cannot define general high-delta reversals. Strict smooth-versus-rectifier uni wins are 18/32 low (.990), 135/440 high (1.339), heavily clustered; no unconditional activation ordering follows.

## Explanations, Predictions and Boundaries
Signal attenuation/terminal scale restoration could explain local plain-network rescues; radial information loss could explain spiral LN-by-residual sign changes; persistence/amplification and held-out overconfidence could explain multivariate/tabular failures. None was directly instrumented here. Width, activation, target difficulty, batch/epochs and selection bias remain alternatives. K1033 already places shallow tabular reversal near delta .03-.1 at 32 epochs, while K1009's opening .1 rule and K1020's opening LN-FIRST still risk overriding their conditions. These external conflicts are flagged, not broadly rewritten.

Prospective, untested predictions: on a new XOR input4 dataset with fixed depth4/width/activation, terminal LN and optimizer, plain should more often beat residual at delta .02 than .10; on a new 1.5-turn GELU spiral at delta .0256, changing one to three LNs may improve residual but not plain (tentative local extrapolation); on a new multivariate target at Adam delta .06, late LN should not have a reliable held-out advantage. Tests would be paired 10-seed factorial cells, holding initialization and training fixed, with seed-level loss differences and multiple independent datasets. No new cells were run: existing counterexamples suffice to remove unconditional instructions, whereas establishing new mechanisms or exact thresholds needs broader replication. Future utility improvement remains unmeasured.

Repeated pairs share seeds, sets and datasets; std values are retained but seed-level covariance is unavailable. No pair count is an independent binomial sample. Tiny median ratios (.99-1.01) do not establish significance. Findings do not extend beyond these synthetic datasets, MLP pre-LN/activation conventions, default initialization, T256-2048 and the observed budgets.

## Submitted Rewrites
K1006 (669 characters): MLPs at comparable calibrated delta (K1001/K1019): select task/budget regime BEFORE LN/residual. Regression: prefer LN-supported depth (K1074); more-LN wins uni 57/69, multi 61/72, but residual is NOT +1 and activation is optimizer/target-dependent (K1039). Spiral: K1020/K1061, with terminal-LN and depth/residual interactions; no universal smooth-activation penalty. XOR: K1015/K1061, then high-dimension K1018/K1065. Tabular: check epochs first (K1033/K1073); shallow reversal can start near delta .03 at 32 epochs, not only .1 (K1009). Use K1022 only for placement-matched ties. Pooled arch-only hit 74/110 is predictive association, not causal LN/residual benefit.
K1015 (617 characters): Classification, same optimizer/lr/budget: inspect the plain stack's FINAL LN before residual/depth. XOR input<8, delta<.05, last-normalized plain with >= rival LN: plain won 18/19 history, 5/6 lab (six sets); above .05 lab 1/5. K1061: deep plain unnormalized-tail loses to residual 26/30; terminal-LN spiral adaptive plain wins 22/23, but SGD residual 3/3 (+2/2 controlled); tabular plain 12/27 lab vs 6/6 corpus. Thus no NEVER-pick-deep-plain rule; outside these cells do not force residual. Regression: use LN/depth/delta gate K1074. Appearance rates (residual 53%, plain 29%) mix architectures, not causal effects.
K1022 (624 characters): MLP pre-LN placement: compare equal LN count/residual, depth/width/activation and optimizer/lr/budget. Multivariate Adam-family: do NOT prefer later LN at lr*T>=.03 (q_ca0dea: 1/6 wins); below .03 only weak evidence (5/7). This is raw lr*T for that optimizer stratum, not a universal calibrated-delta cutoff; strict current Adam/AdamW pairs disagree below (0/2). Tabular delta<.1: weak late-LN tie-break (corpus 6/7, lab 3/5; controlled smooth-additive 2/2, CE ratio .95), after epoch gate K1033/K1073. No generic placement preference for uni/XOR; terminal-LN plain/residual interactions use K1059/K1061, not additive K1006.
