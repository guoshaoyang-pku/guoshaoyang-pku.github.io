## Interpretation, Boundaries And Continuing Predictions
Measured phenomenon: .003 recipes give lower held-out CE than focal AdamW .0003 at256, then deteriorate while AdamW improves by512/1024. Neither “underfit conservative rival” nor “overshooting aggressive optimizer” is established without training CE or parameter/update motion. Competing explanations include finite-data generalization, optimizer-state/noise effects and initialization-dependent trajectories. Declining train CE with rising held-out CE would support generalization deterioration; matched gradient/update norms and controlled clipping/rate interventions would test instability; controlled initialization would test initialization sensitivity.
K1071 already says width/optimizer/rate/budget/loss/init are conditions, not a universal band law. The present .003-versus-.0003 advantage refutes using the old band to discard these exact short-budget rivals, while .001-versus-.003 and budget reversals preserve the danger of systematic maximum-Delta preference elsewhere. No transfer to other widths/layers, residual GRUs, regularized losses, decay/betas, batch sizes, alpha, original benchmark losses or continuous learning-rate thresholds is established.
