## Failures, trajectories and mechanism
No failed training seeds explain the new reversal. Uniform prediction gives ln24=3.178054. Wide RMSprop CE is .064235/.059835 below it; Adagrad is .167407/.075776 below. Narrow RMSprop is .562232/.287076 below. CE proximity alone cannot show uniform logits, missing token conditioning, calibration, or inability to fit.
Archived curves are held-out CE after each update, not training loss. Wide RMSprop mean CE after update1 is3.723659/3.787058; early16-step maxima4.054996/4.144903; minima3.111457 at202 /3.117613 at246; endpoints3.113819/3.118219. These show early high loss and little late mean deterioration, not measured parameter overshoot. Narrow RMSprop has larger late deterioration from minima2.522715/2.736562 to endpoints2.615822/2.890978, yet still wins.
Optimization trajectories, initialization sensitivity, restricted transition fitting, noise/generalization and optimizer state remain competing explanations. Training CE, logits, update norms and actual displacement are unmeasured. Default initialization reproduces the question but is not an initialization intervention.

## Predictions, boundaries and publication
Unmeasured predictions: an additional allowed V24/L16/alpha1.4 split with this exact recipe should reproduce C/B>1 at d128 and<1 at d64; either reliable opposite sign refutes transfer. A width-specific learning failure predicts weak token-conditioned logit information in wide RMSprop; CE near lnV without weak conditioning would refute that interpretation. Overshoot requires measured update/parameter trajectories or a controlled smaller-rate/clipping rescue, not endpoint CE. Generalization requires falling training CE while test CE rises; these curves alone cannot test it.
No universal Adagrad advantage, continuous width threshold, Delta cutoff, optimal rate, alpha law, other initialization/budget/context transfer or original-split numeric reconstruction follows. Two datasets and one seed cohort bound the inference. The recipe fact is publishable while mechanism remains open.
