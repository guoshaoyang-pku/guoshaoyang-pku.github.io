## Counterexamples, Explanations and Boundaries
Retain C wins6/8 comparisons, including both long-budget alpha1.4 cells: neither low-rate nor SGD-first is supported. Retain focal q_3a67db; its adaptive win is not overridden. Nominal calibrated bigram Delta favors C by about90x over B at either budget, yet misses both alpha1.0/T1024 means. Delta is a proxy, not measured parameter motion.
C mean held-out CE minima occur near updates204/215/265/248 on the four long runs, at3.524997/3.575081/3.230259/3.214510. It subsequently deteriorates on every table, yet remains the winner on alpha1.4. This descriptive pattern does not identify noise fitting, overshoot, recurrent-context misuse or calibration. Training CE, logits, gradients and parameter/update norms were not measured.
Competing explanations are recipe-specific optimization trajectories, initialization sensitivity, table/split variation and generalization. Falling training CE with rising held-out CE would support a generalization gap; update-norm spikes and controlled clipping/rate rescue would test instability; controlled head calibration and token-conditioned logits would test calibration/context explanations. None is established by endpoints or held-out curves.
Budget changes updates and exposure together:4096/16384 windows,5.12/20.48 sampled-window epochs,98304/393216 token labels. Width64/depth1 is fixed, not tested causally; wider/deeper GRUs may reverse. Optimizer type, rate and momentum/betas change jointly; A also changes decay. Alpha covaries with table/sequence, so no causal alpha threshold or entropy law follows from two tables per group.
Unmeasured extrapolation: a third metadata-selected table per alpha should reproduce C<B at T256, B<C at T1024 for alpha1.0 and C<B for alpha1.4 under these exact recipes. An opposite mean sign fails that transfer; intervals determine whether it is resolved. Other widths/layers, initializations, batches, data sizes and intermediate/longer budgets remain open.
