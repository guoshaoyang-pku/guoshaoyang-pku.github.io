# High-decay T512 wide-bigram recipe audit
## Finding and scope
The requested full RMSprop recipe beats both tested Adagrad recipes by mean on all four allowed tables and beats AdamW .0003 on three of four. It is not reproducibly superior to every conservative adaptive rival: bg_22dc04 has a finite high-loss seed that reverses its mean against AdamW .0003. Reducing decay or using T256/b32 predicts substantial deterioration on both tested alpha1.4 tables. Four heads does not reproduce K1102's loss when high decay and T512/b16 are retained. These are recipe-local observations, not a regularization mechanism or universal optimizer rule.

## Trajectories, interpretation and boundaries
Archived test-CE curves verify endpoint values. At T512/b16 step128, high-decay R seeds still have CE about3.12 on bg_05e970 and3.04-3.08 on bg_22dc04; by step512 all first-table seeds reach2.47-2.52 and nine second-table seeds reach2.41-2.45. The retained tenth ends3.201166. T256/b32 R endpoints are heterogeneous: first-table range2.4775-3.1232, second2.4308-3.0773. Late CE reductions and seed-sensitive high-loss endpoints are measured; their mechanism is unresolved.
Competing explanations include optimizer/penalty trajectory effects, default initialization and layer-cloning interactions, finite-window generalization, calibration and learning token conditioning. No train CE, true step0 CE, logits/probes, update norms or parameter-motion measurement is available. Near-lnV CE does not establish uniform logits or overshoot. Tests require train/test trajectories and conditioning probes, initialization-controlled repetitions, and a matched underlying-table alpha manipulation.
Intervals assume independent approximately normal paired-seed differences, with t9 critical value2.262157. No multiplicity correction, held-out resampling or between-table interval is applied. The evident heterogeneous seed outcomes make n10 inference particularly fragile on bg_22dc04 and T256. Ratios are ratios of means, not mean seed ratios.
Four distinct independently materialized table/split instances were tested, all previously used for different recipes; there are no newly materialized datasets and only two tables per alpha. Alpha and table/split vary jointly. No transfer is established to other widths, FF, V/L, budgets, batches, losses, pre-LN, initialization or untuned rival rates.
