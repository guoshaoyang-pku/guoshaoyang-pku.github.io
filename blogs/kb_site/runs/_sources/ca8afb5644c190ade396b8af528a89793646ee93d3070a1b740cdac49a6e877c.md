# Scope audit of K1040, K1038 and K1009
## Finding and provenance
All three solver-facing prescriptions exceed their empirical matching scope. This is a scope finding, not proof that their use caused solver errors. The supplied utility associations are K1040 -.12 (n16), K1038 -.03 (n68), K1009 -.06 (n22); retrieval-level noKB outcomes are unavailable, so neither associations nor wording-specific causality can be reconstructed. No evaluation answers, scores, predictions, solution explanations or grading comments were used as outcome evidence.

User-requested q_6a81f1 and q_b0ae9d prompts are saved with provenance. KB historical counts are attributed assertions, not independently recovered measurements. ## Definitions and methods
Loss is held-out 10-seed mean test CE or MSE, lower better. Seeds are 0-9; experiment artifacts include failed-seed counts, full settings and dataset parameters. Standard deviations below describe seed variability, not standard errors. Pairwise confidence intervals cannot be computed from aggregate standard deviations without seed covariance; pair counts share candidates/sets and are not independent trials.
Comparisons are only within set_id. SGD pairs require deep>=3, zero LN, adaptive rival proxy Delta<.1 and SGD proxy Delta>=3 times rival; excluded/failed candidates are omitted. Delta is lr*T for Adam/AdamW/RMSprop, 2*lr*sqrt(T) for Adagrad, and lr*T*g/(1-m) for SGD, with g=.03 for momentum-.9 regression and .01 otherwise. This is an assumed calibration, not measured displacement. E=total_samples_seen/train_rows is nominal exposure under replacement sampling, not unique data passes.
Tabular depth comparisons match set, complete optimizer, loss and budget, but audit remaining architecture differences. Restrict substantive tabular conclusions to smooth_additive, sparse_interaction and piecewise_boundary; lab family labels also include xor/spiral rules, which were not pooled into those conclusions. Analyses are saved in audit/sgd_pairs.json and audit/tabular_depth_pairs.json. No CE/MSE effects are combined into a common effect estimate.

## K1009: Delta does not define learned state
q_b0ae9d is an 8-feature piecewise boundary (active0/7, threshold0, point seed1490966472), 1024/2048 rows, RMSprop lr3e-5 wd0, T256 bs64, E16, Delta=.00768. Five default-init candidates are B d5 w128 residual LeakyReLU LN[0,0,0,0,1]; A d2 w256 residual SiLU no LN; D d3 w192 residual SiLU LN[0,1,0]; C d2 w256 plain GELU LN[1,0]; E d4 w192 plain SiLU LN[0,1,1,0]. Its split is absent from measured lab. No ranking is inferred from the evaluation answer. It cannot isolate depth, normalization or a Delta=.1 threshold.
The lab audit finds 42 relevant depth comparisons across the three stated rules; none is a pure depth change. At E4 and Delta>=.1, shallow wins piecewise2/2 (median CE difference -.05086), smooth0/2 (+.02547), sparse2/2 (-.18054), each two sets. At Delta<.1, E4 results are piecewise6/8, smooth6/10, sparse2/10; E8 results are piecewise0/1, smooth1/4, sparse1/3. These sparse, confounded strata are descriptive, not an estimated universal threshold.
Representative high-Delta counterexample: stabcls_a1889f/set_4096_var_var_fix_f14d2c, AdamW .003 beta2=.999 wd0, T256 bs16 E4, Delta=.768: c_b074b6 d4 w64 plain LeakyReLU two LN CE .223616±.027725 loses to c_873f6b d5 w64 residual ReLU two LN .191277±.024087 (+.032339). By contrast stabcls_0858c7/set_4096_var_var_fix_23573e at the same lr/T but beta2=.95 and piecewise rule gives c_82cdf7 shallow CE .079476 versus c_e14921 deep .136888; width/residual/activation/LN differ.
K1033 reports a reversal below .1 at E32 on limited smooth/sparse controls, with depth confounded by width/LN; K1073 reports rule- and exposure-dependent displacement optima on one shallow ReLU+LN architecture, with batch/exposure confounding. These are relevant limitations on K1009, not independent validation of overconfidence. Completed boundary learning, confident mistakes and distractor fitting require train loss, accuracy, logits or calibration measurements, absent here.
## Narrow solver-facing openings proposed
K1040: “For matched 1-1.5-turn spiral CE twins near lr=.003, T=1024, prefer AdamW over coupled Adam only in tested architectures/decay cells; do not extrapolate to arbitrary beta2 or budgets.”
K1038: “For deep zero-LN SGD, verify family, residual path, activation and raw lr/momentum before using calibrated Delta to rank rivals; pooled endpoints do not establish freezing or an unconditional adaptive advantage.”
K1009: “On 1024-row Gaussian tabular classification, treat shallower/wider architecture as a conditional comparison, not a prescription triggered by Delta>=.1; match rule, exposure, optimizer and residual/LN pattern.”
The revisions retain local observations and replace asserted mechanism fields with hypotheses. No unrelated KB claim is merged/deleted or new general rule added.
## Competing explanations and remaining tests
Pre-registered transfer of the decay penalty was supported locally; pre-registered momentum-SGD improvement failed. No new clean tabular experiment was run; the planned rule/exposure heterogeneity check used existing measurements, so it is descriptive rather than a prospective confirmation.
Coupled decay versus beta2 noise is distinguished by exact optimizer/beta2/decay factorial controls (performed locally). A universal spiral claim still needs additional architectures, independent splits, turns, lr/T and wd ranges, with absolute CE and seed outcomes. Gradient attenuation versus optimization/calibration is distinguished by layerwise activation/gradient norms, parameter displacement and train-loss trajectories in exact SGD twins; endpoint results alone cannot choose between them. Tabular overconfidence versus underfitting needs exact architecture controls at fixed batch while varying T, alongside train/test CE, accuracy and logit calibration across each rule; independent batch changes can separate exposure from noise.
The shared “first 200 characters” failure hypothesis remains a plausible wording risk. Testing utility causality requires a randomized same-question solver replay with identical retrieval except original versus scoped wording and noKB baseline. That experiment was not performed. Findings support conservative scope corrections, not manufactured utility causality or a finished mechanistic theory.
