## Uncertainty and mechanism
Report means, within-table ratios, paired-seed t9 95% intervals, failures, exclusions and cache status. Seed labels do not ensure equal minibatches or logits across widths. Dependent comparisons share configurations and tables. Intervals omit multiplicity, test-set and between-table uncertainty. Initial-logit/functional-step mediation requires supported diagnostics, unavailable in inherited report. A no-update comparison could refute initial-loss-only explanation but cannot identify a mediator.
## Decision
No parent merge now. Merge only if a single numerical law faithfully predicts held-out signs and magnitudes, including inherited high-rate counterexamples; otherwise preserve threshold-rich facts and explicitly falsify candidate theory. No transfer to RMSprop/Adagrad, other loss, vocab/context, initialization, FF/head/batch/budget.
