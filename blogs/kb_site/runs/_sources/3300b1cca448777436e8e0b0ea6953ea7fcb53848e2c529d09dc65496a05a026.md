## Interpretation, Tests and Boundaries
Competing explanations remain rate/decay tuning, activation/depth/width/LN conditioning, target offset/scale and fit-versus-generalization trajectories. No measured gradients, update norms, optimizer states or training-loss curves establish effective lr/3 parity, coherent gradients, momentum causality or overfitting. Nominal calibration places original E/D near proxy parity, but cannot determine their ordering.
Longer-budget convergence is not a sufficient explanation: exact extensions do not restore E<D on either target, and O/D test loss rises. To distinguish overfitting from unstable fitting, measure train/test curves and update/state diagnostics. To distinguish optimizer from tuning, cross algorithms at common lr/decay and retain AdamW decay semantics explicitly. To distinguish step count from exposure, prospectively compareT256/b128 withT512/b64; this matches draws but changes batch noise and update count, so further controls remain necessary.
Before future unmeasured tests, predict the AdamW recipe beats SGD on the exact E network atT512/b64 on a third outcome-blind allowed eight-input target. An opposite robust sign narrows this two-target fact. Predict no reliable full-recipe E advantage from budget doubling alone; a corrected E win on a fresh target would be a genuine transport counterexample.
No original-target numerical replication, universal SGD/adaptive ordering, mechanism, full five-choice reconstruction, plain-SGD conclusion, target-population win rate or transport to other batches/initialization/LN/decay/loss is established.
