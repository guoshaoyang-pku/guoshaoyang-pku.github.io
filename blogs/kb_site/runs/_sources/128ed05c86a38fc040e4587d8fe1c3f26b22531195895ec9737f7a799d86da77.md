## Methods, uncertainty and explanations
Primary outcome mean held-out MSE; report absolute losses,ratio,paired seed margins/95% t9 intervals if available,seedwise wins and failure strata. All-failure/partial-failure cells will not become successful finite evidence. Shared cells,seeds,budgets and cached results are dependent; selected targets are not a population sample.
Faster low-rate fitting predicts declining train and test residuals,especially oscillatory components. Overshoot/noise predicts elevated late train/test fluctuation and rescue after a paired downshift. Generalization predicts better train but worse test fit. Initialization,beta/decay,normalization and optimizer history compete. Held-out endpoints/curves alone do not prove these mechanisms; training residuals,updates and checkpoint forks are currently not exposed. No universal RMSprop rule or pooled family ranking is planned.
