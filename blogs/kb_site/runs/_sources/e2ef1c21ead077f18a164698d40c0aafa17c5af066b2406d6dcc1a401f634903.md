## Interpretation and limits
Same dimension removes the eight-versus-sixteen confound, not target/split or active-feature complexity confounds. Sparse tables are two samples of sign-product geometry; smooth targets differ coefficients/active count. No causal rule-only or universal activation/LN law will follow. Endpoint CE cannot establish calibration or gradient geometry; unsupported instrumentation is known and will not be probed again. Supported interventions here measure architecture/optimizer/budget interactions only. Separate CE/accuracy, paired-seed descriptive uncertainty, provenance hashes/cache/failure counts will be reported.
No merge of K1009/K1056/K1033 without a quantitatively faithful law. Final solver text <=700 characters will be proposed only for independently useful gates. Pending experiments and mechanism questions do not prevent this report being independently reviewable.
