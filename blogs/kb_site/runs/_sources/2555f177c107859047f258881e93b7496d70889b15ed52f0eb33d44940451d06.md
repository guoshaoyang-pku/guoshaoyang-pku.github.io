## Interpretation and Future Tests
On original/alpha1 tables R mean held-out curves reach minima2.690992/3.538577/3.592036 at steps273/264/204, then rise .111813/.213755/.235735 by1024. A reaches its lowest measured mean at1024. At step16 R/A means are2.978394/3.441750,3.763154/3.868613,3.806757/3.872124. Thus earlier held-out fitting and later deterioration are measured, not parameter overshoot or proven overfitting.
Competing explanations include finite-data generalization, optimization noise/state dynamics, initialization sensitivity and table/split structure. Train CE declining while held-out CE rises would support generalization deterioration; matched update norms and controlled rate/clipping rescue would test instability; initialization interventions would test initialization dependence. Available curves are held-out CE only; failed-seed curves are masked/absent, and no motion, logits or training CE was measured.
Historical q_b0cf5f and both alpha.8 analogues refute blanket T512 transport. New alpha1 results preserve q_787657's scoped direction. This does not prove alpha alone determines reversal: vocab/table/splits and budgets remain conditions.
Unmeasured prospective prediction: a third metadata-selected V48/L24/alpha1 table with the exact recipes will favor R at512 and A at1024; at alpha.8 predict A at512 but do not predict failure frequency. Reliable opposite paired signs refute those transports. No new experiment tests these predictions.
No universal optimizer preference, continuous alpha cutoff, .1-.3 ceiling, replacement architecture rule, other-width/depth/loss/batch/default-init transfer or original-question numerical reconstruction follows. Mechanism remains report-only and unfinished.
