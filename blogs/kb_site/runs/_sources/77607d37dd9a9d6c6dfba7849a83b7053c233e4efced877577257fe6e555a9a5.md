## Explanations, Predictions and Boundaries
Composition/capacity is consistent with matched depth gains, but added blocks also change optimization,initialization draws,parameters and sampled minibatches. Propagation through skips cannot explain a universal improvement: matched skip counterexamples and target reversals reject it. Initial-output/baseline fitting,activation-conditioned gradients and LN/feature scaling remain viable; endpoint test MSE and curves do not identify mediation or separate fitting from generalization.
Unmeasured tests: on a third metadata-selected allowed single-cycle target at these exact recipes, predict q7 B<E,C<E and q4 C<A; opposite mean signs reject bundle transfer. Predict fixed-w128 terminal-LN Leaky<GELU at both depths/skips and d3<d1; a reversal rejects the respective transfer. Make no universal skip-sign prediction after P4/P7 failures. These tests remain unrun.
Distinguishing mechanisms requires archived initial outputs,matched initial features/common minibatches,gradient/Jacobian trajectories,linear-extra-block controls and parameter/FLOP matching. A linear-block bridge recovering the gain would undermine necessity of nonlinear composition; equal initial-output matching eliminating the contrast would support initialization/baseline fitting. No undocumented centering/frequency/data intervention was run.
Evidence stops at two fixed targets/splits,default init,the specified widths/LN positions,b32,SGD.001/m.9/decay0 and separate T256/T512 panels. There is no isolated width,initialization,offset,frequency or budget effect and no universal residual/rectifier score. Original-question MSE margins,additional splits,mechanistic mediation and broader transfer remain unresolved. The scoped empirical audit is independently reviewable; the theory is unfinished.
