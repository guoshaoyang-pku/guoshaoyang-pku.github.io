## Evidence, uncertainty and competing explanations
Current evidence is the focal ordinal reversal and prior scoped claims. K1019 pooled adaptive success does not decide this exact setting; K1071 bands are recipe-confounded and not a guarantee. Nominal Delta is not measured motion. Endpoint CE can distinguish recipe rankings but cannot establish noise fitting, overshoot, calibration or recurrent-context misuse. Training/test trajectories, token-conditioned logits and update norms would distinguish those explanations; these quantities are not presumed measured.
