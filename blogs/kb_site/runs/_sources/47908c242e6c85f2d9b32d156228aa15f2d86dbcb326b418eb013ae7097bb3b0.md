# Displacement over-selection: progress, precision and failed metrics
## Evidence And Definitions
Frozen inputs were 863 visible training records, 35 KB claims and 11,215 lab candidates. Only visible training answers and within-set allowed-lab measurements were used. Refresh at the research checkpoint repinned Jbb8de54d2489_research_e0001, with no new history records. Other projects' new experiments were not incorporated.
Nominal displacement D is a proxy, not a measured parameter norm: adaptive optimizers use lr*T; Adagrad uses 2*lr*sqrt(T); SGD uses lr*T*g/(1-m), with assumed g=.01, bigram .003, momentum-MSE .03. Architecture, normalization, target scale and optimizer state can invalidate these gradient-scale approximations. Progress means improved held-out loss relative to insufficiently learned rivals; late precision means a smaller rate beats a larger rate at the SAME budget, not simply that longer training worsens loss.
Training gold answers supply ordinal outcomes only. Lab values are ten-seed mean and seed SD, not confidence intervals. A win is a lower mean; multiple pairs sharing candidates/seeds are dependent. No significance tests, seed-win counts or causal overconfidence claims are inferred. All rate comparisons hold set, data, model, loss, optimizer settings and budget fixed; deliberate optimizer contrasts are labeled separately.

## Explanations, Predictions And Boundaries
Late fitting/noisy minibatch refinement, optimizer-specific trajectories, regularization and numerical instability are competing explanations. Overconfidence/distractor fitting requires train/test CE, error-conditioned confidence and logit norms; none is measured here. MSE reversals cannot be called logit overconfidence. Decay is experimentally separated for the spiral lab, not for its history failure.
Distinguishable next tests, predicted before any new runs: if confident-error inflation explains tabular/XOR late behavior, the losing recipe should lower train CE while raising held-out confidence on errors; if optimization instability explains bigram Infinity, smaller lr or clipping should restore finite metrics before a confidence-only account is needed; if offset memory explains q_09b159, paired allowed large-/small-offset targets should change Adagrad accumulator growth and ordering with architecture fixed. These are unrun predictions, not facts.
Thresholds are tested-grid statements only: tabular long-budget .6144 beats .06144 and2.048; univariate6.144 can beat .2048; bigram .0003 is finite and.003 nonfinite at2048 in one transformer/L1 recipe. No universal continuous cutoff, width law, batch law, data-family optimum or2048 extrapolation from256 is warranted. Five selected failures cannot quantify population-wide solver bias.
