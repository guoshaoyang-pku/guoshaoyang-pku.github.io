## Interpretation, boundaries and next tests
Hard relative benefit strengthens from T256 to2048 on both new splits;this does not prove a universal late-training trend.0717d7's T256 margin is much smaller than C's SD;without paired covariance no confidence or reliable seedwise win rate is claimed. Large easy SDs show possible seed-tail sensitivity. Zero easy A CE requires absolute-margin interpretation.
Faster feature fitting,activation/LN interactions,coupled-decay limits at tiny easy losses,and optimizer-state dynamics all fit parts of the evidence. Endpoint CE cannot identify their mechanisms. Increasing turns also changes scale;causal complexity versus radius is unresolved.
Unmeasured predictions:original bundles on a third allowed three-turn dataset should favor C at both budgets;on a third one-turn dataset,T2048 should favor A. Opposite signs reject those transfers. Separately,rate-only A/C factorials with wd0 and matched optimizer would test whether >=3x rate suffices;decay-only C controls would test the easy-loss account. Those are future tests,not measured claims.
The broader rule remains sparsely supported beyond this exact d3w256/all-LN bundle. No extension to all2turn datasets,all smooth/rectifier activations,other rates,architectures,budgets or seed populations is established.
