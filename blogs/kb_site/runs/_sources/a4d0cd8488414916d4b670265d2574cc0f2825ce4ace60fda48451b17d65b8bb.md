## Uncertainty and Explanations
Intervals assume approximately normal differences across independent seed runs on fixed datasets. Shared seed labels do not match initial weights/logits across widths. Intervals exclude multiplicity,test-window resampling,between-table uncertainty and uncertainty in the original ordinal result. Two independent table/sequence pairs cannot estimate a universal population win rate. Failed/excluded seeds were not silently dropped; none occurred.
No mechanism is required or established. Initialization/cloning,optimization trajectories,capacity/generalization and finite-table variation remain alternatives. Numerical failure is not inferred from high finite CE. Overfitting would require falling train CE with rising test CE; initialization-only explanations require step0 logits/CE and matched batches; optimizer-motion explanations require measured updates/probe-logit motion and a preregistered intervention. Available post-update test curves establish none of these mediators. No train CE,step0,gradients or checkpoints were measured.
