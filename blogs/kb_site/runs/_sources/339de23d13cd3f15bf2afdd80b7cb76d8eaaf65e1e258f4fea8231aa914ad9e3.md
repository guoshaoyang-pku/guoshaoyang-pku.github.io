## Uncertainty, Failed Transfer and Alternatives
Endpoint means do not establish optimization dynamics, representation attenuation or a causal mechanism. SDs are seed spread, not paired intervals; covariance/raw seeds and trajectories are unavailable. A's spread is substantial. Tiny CE ratios look large while absolute margins are below.001.
No primary or full-order transfer failed on these two splits. Failed broader extrapolation is retained: R1dd74ea5c0/K1099 predicted stronger matched deep/shallow benefit on harder data, but fixed-width/SiLU d5res/d3plain favored deep on all four splits and proportionally more on easy (.685/.673 versus.864/.882). Its d5 skip/plain easy ratios1.099/1.230 are much smaller than present d3 skip effects; those recipes must not be pooled.
Historical q_03cc9f and q_56e62b D/A oppose deeper-plain preference; q_0a7f2f supplies high-Delta SGD losses. Neither easy data nor unnormalized tails guarantee plain dominance.
Competing accounts: nonlinear composition depth, skip-conditioned optimization, LN placement/feature scaling, activation-conditioned fitting and seed-tail behavior. Present skip and LN controls distinguish some endpoint effects but not mediation. Dataset selection is two existing easy splits, not random population sampling.
Unmeasured predictions: a third allowed one-turn split at this exact recipe should preserve D<B and D<A; reversal rejects transfer. Changing only rate to3e-5 or budget to1024 is predicted to weaken D/B advantage, but is untested; an opposite trend rejects that budget account. Seed-resolved paired training and trajectories would distinguish systematic versus rare-seed failure effects.
Original q split was not regenerated; lab LeakyReLU slope is null/default in returned schema, not independently certified. Original-question numerical margins, explicit slope control, independently audited failures and paired uncertainty remain unresolved. No causal turns statement: seed and coordinate scale change across existing datasets.
