## Mechanisms and Distinguishing Tests
Endpoint test MSE supports local recipe/rate order,not undertraining,overfitting,sustained adaptive motion or Adagrad accumulator suppression. Rate changes may alter fitting speed,trajectory/basin,update noise and rate-scaled decay; even matched decay coefficients imply unequal cumulative AdamW shrinkage. Beta/decay sensitivity is measured locally,not a mechanistic decomposition of all interactions.
Unexecuted predictions: a fitting-limited low-rate account predicts poorer training MSE at3e-5 as well as poorer test MSE; equal/better low-rate training fit would weaken it. Overfitting predicts improved training loss alongside worsening held-out loss,requiring repeated paired train/test checkpoints rather than separate endpoints. A sustained-update account predicts larger useful measured parameter updates for high-rate AdamW late in training; losses alone cannot test it.
An Adagrad-accumulator account predicts a controlled state-reset intervention changes late progress,with weight/batch/rate controls. A beta-memory account predicts preserved-versus-reset moment or fixed-beta interventions change trajectories beyond early transient effects. These need instrumentation/state forks not exposed by the documented endpoint interface; no unsupported schedules or keys were guessed.
