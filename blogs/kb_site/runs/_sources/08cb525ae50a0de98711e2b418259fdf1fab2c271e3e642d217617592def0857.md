## Mechanisms, Predictions and Boundaries
Coupled decay perturbs both task gradients and accumulated squared gradients; this is a plausible optimization account, not a measured explanation. Useful regularization, numerical sensitivity and task-fitting perturbation remain competing explanations. No update norms, accumulator histories, training CE or measured displacement were collected; endpoint CE cannot establish freezing or overfitting.
Unmeasured prediction: another metadata-selected allowed XOR8 instance at this exact recipe should favor wd0 in mean, potentially with seed reversals; an opposite mean is a transfer counterexample. A genuine trajectory effect should persist under a controlled higher-precision computation; a rounding-dominated effect need not. An accumulator account predicts different decay effects when accumulator state is controlled; overfitting requires decreasing training CE with increasing test CE. These tests remain unrun.
Do not extrapolate to lr.001/.003 XOR16, other architectures/LN masks, decay magnitudes, budgets, batch sizes, initialization or tuned optima. Two fixed metadata-selected instances do not establish a universal probability of transfer. K1002's progress proxy and rate preferences remain observational; K1005's warning against universal decay tie-breaks remains valid.
