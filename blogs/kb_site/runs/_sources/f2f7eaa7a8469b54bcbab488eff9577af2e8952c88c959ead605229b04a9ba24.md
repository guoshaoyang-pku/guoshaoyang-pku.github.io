## Mechanism evidence and discriminating tests
Endpoint test losses identify conditional rankings only. Initial/train MSE,actual per-layer parameter motion,predicted-output changes,gradient RMS and late variance were not collected. The documented runner returns fresh-run metric mean/SD; it exposes no seed pairing,logging,schedules or checkpoint/state forks. Resource/template lists are empty. For an instrumented test, define per-step parameter RMS and cumulative path/net displacement separately per layer. Define function step as RMS[f(t+1,x)-f(t,x)] on fixed train and held-out probes,also divided by train-target SD. Log full-train/test MSE and full-train gradient RMS; measure last128-step loss/output temporal variance separately from across-seed dispersion. Test probes must never tune the controller.
Underfit predicts poorer low-rate train and test fit. Jitter predicts sustained late function/loss variance and improvement of both train/test residuals after downshift. Generalization predicts equal/better high-rate training fit with worse held-out fit. Trajectory/basin dependence can produce persistent differences after downshift; rate-dependent decay and optimizer state remain competitors.
Best discriminating intervention remains **unexecuted**: on O and P,atT1024 fork identical .003 weights,optimizer state and future batches; continue1024 steps at .003 versus anneal to .0001.
