## Mechanisms, uncertainty and fresh predictions
Initialization CE, train endpoints/progress, gradient/update norms and probe-logit motion are unavailable: verified train.py records only post-update test CE; the process API supports CPU MLP/coupled Adam, not this transformer/RMSprop panel. First-update CE or ln24 is not initial CE. No T0 pseudo-baseline was run.
Effective functional-step is an unverified hypothesis: at matched probe-logit motion, its account predicts rate/shape trajectory differences shrink; persistent differences support initialization, conditioning/capacity or generalization accounts. Test this with true step0 train/test CE, fixed probe batches, centered-logit KL/RMS motion and optimizer-update diagnostics under verified sources. Endpoint reversals alone cannot distinguish these mechanisms.
R9d13cfdf1b's sqrt-width/depth rescue did not restore rankings; its joint cubic scored50/72 signs and40/72 effect ranges and failed old-number reproduction. No new fitted law is proposed. A prospective third-table check on unmeasured bg_58d8ba at the exact new T256 recipe predicts TF64/depth1 .0003−.0001 in[−.09,−.03], TF128/depth1 in[.01,.07], and TF128/.0001 depth4−1 in[.005,.035] nats. These are explicit falsification tolerances, not calibrated prediction intervals; opposite signs or effects outside ranges reject transfer.
Intervals condition on fixed tables and approximately normal independent paired-seed differences; they omit multiplicity, test-window resampling and between-table uncertainty. Only two T256 tables and one T1024 table were tested; alpha/table/split are not independently manipulated. No transfer to altered FF, heads, decay, batch, vocabulary/context, loss, initialization, optimizer or budget is established.
