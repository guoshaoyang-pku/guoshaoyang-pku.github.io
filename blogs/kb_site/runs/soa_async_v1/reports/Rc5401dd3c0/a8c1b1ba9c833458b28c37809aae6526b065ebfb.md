Only visible training history and allowed lab measurements were used; no evaluation-answer files were accessed.

The supplied epoch-2 higher/lower-log-Delta counts motivate this investigation but were not independently reconstructed as population-bias statistics. Selected failures and outcome-selected dataflip records cannot establish universal error frequencies.

Nominal Δ is an optimizer-based progress proxy, not measured parameter movement. Ratios below are high-rate mean held-out loss divided by low-rate mean; margins are high minus low. Standard deviations describe ten training seeds, not paired confidence intervals.

## Preregistration and Controls
Predictions were recorded before outcomes: polynomial high-rate wins short/low-rate wins long; deep-GELU Adagrad likewise reverses at 32 exposure epochs; polynomial reversal transfers to another polynomial; historical sparse/smooth AdamW reversal transfers to lab analogues; RMSprop beats AdamW in a full-recipe tabular analogue.

Each rate pair fixes dataset, architecture, width, normalization, initialization convention, loss, optimizer configuration and budget. All runs start fresh. Across budgets, batch remains fixed but T and samples seen change together: their separate effects are unidentified.

A protocol error is preserved: the first polynomial experiment inherited MSE+L2(.001), rather than preregistered plain MSE. A separately identified plain-MSE control corrected this. The inherited-loss results are supplementary, not substituted for the intended test.

Unspecified optimizer knobs use runner defaults; optimizer-state trajectories were not independently measured.

The preregistered reuse of a slow-Adagrad cell from another experiment is inadmissible under within-set comparison rules; no cross-set E/C numerical ratio is used.

Two exact-recipe historical dataset reversals remain relevant:
- **q_a9073c/q_07b812:** dimension8 residual ReLU depth2/width192, LN `[F,T]`, AdamW, rates.001/3e-5, T1024/batch32. Higher wins sparse; lower wins smooth.
- **q_9d6507/q_b9d45e:** dimension8 nonresidual ReLU depth1/width192, no LN, RMSprop wd0, rates.001/.0001, same budget. Higher wins sparse; lower wins smooth.

These are **2 matched recipe groups/4 question sets**, with ordinal outcomes and unavailable numerical margins. Rule family, active features, coefficients and split seeds change jointly. Controlled W analogues do not reproduce the first reversal: both favor higher rate. Historical counterexamples stand, but family labels alone do not transport their ordering.

## Interpretation and Unresolved Tests
Short-budget high-rate wins are compatible with progress limitation. Long-budget reversals remain compatible with late precision, instability, optimizer-specific trajectories or generalization. Endpoint losses alone distinguish none of these mechanisms.

Discriminating tests remain unrun: collect training/test trajectories; compare high-to-low schedules against fixed-rate controls at equal samples; compare continuation with preserved versus reset optimizer state; cross batch sizes under separately matched T and samples; measure finite-loss failures and clipping responses. Falling training loss with rising test loss would favor generalization over failure to fit.

No continuous optimum, universal epoch cutoff, dimension/width law or population-wide bias mechanism follows. Original question splits, alternative initializations, independent seed cohorts and intermediate rates remain untested. Multivariate exclusion from univariate overshoot is not evidence of high-rate safety.
