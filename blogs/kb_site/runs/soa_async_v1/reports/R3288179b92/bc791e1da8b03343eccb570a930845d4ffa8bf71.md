## Explanations and Boundaries
Composition/capacity is consistent with depth gains, but added blocks also change optimization,parameters and RNG consumption. Universal skip-propagation benefit is rejected by matched counterexamples and reversals. Initial-output/baseline fitting,activation-conditioned gradients and LN/feature scaling remain viable. Endpoint MSE does not identify mediation or distinguish fitting from generalization.

Unmeasured transfer predictions: a third metadata-selected allowed single-cycle target should preserve q7 B<E,C<E and q4 C<A; opposite means reject transfer. Predict fixed-w128 terminal-LN Leaky<GELU and depth3<depth1. Make no universal skip-sign prediction after observed failures.

Distinguishing mechanisms requires initial-output measurements,matched features/common minibatches,gradient/Jacobian trajectories,linear-extra-block controls and parameter/FLOP matching. These remain unrun. Evidence stops at the two fixed splits,default initialization,specified widths/LN positions,b32,SGD.001/momentum.9/decay0 and separate T256/T512 panels. Original-target margins and broader transfer remain unresolved. **No universal residual/rectifier score is supported.**
