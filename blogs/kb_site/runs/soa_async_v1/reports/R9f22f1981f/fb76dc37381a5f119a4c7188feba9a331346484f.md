# q_9164e5 T512 Adagrad-vs-Adam replication

The reversal does not replicate across analogous targets. Using the same plain ReLU d4w192 architecture, LN pattern [False, True, False, False], MSE, T512, batch 16, and 10 CPU seeds:

| Split | Adagrad .003/wd0 | Adam .001/wd1e-4, beta2=.95 | E-D | E/D | E wins |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| mvar_9ec635 | 0.018444 (SD .021709) | 0.019098 (SD .009009) | -0.000654 | 0.9658 | 7/10 |
| mvar_b0b978 | 0.115423 (SD .022113) | 0.107728 (SD .026290) | +0.007695 | 1.0714 | 3/10 |

Paired 95% t9 intervals for E-D were [-0.01949, 0.01819] and [-0.01377, 0.02916]; neither excludes zero. Thus E wins only 1/2 splits, with an opposite-sign counterexample. The original q_9164e5 target and point seed were unavailable, so this is transfer evidence, not an exact target replication. Older T2048 results were not pooled.

Because E won the first analogue, Adam controls were preregistered with architecture, split, budget, and seeds frozen. On mvar_9ec635:

- Adam .0003, wd1e-4, beta2=.95: 0.008243
- Adam .003, wd1e-4, beta2=.95: 0.018380
- Adam .001, wd0, beta2=.95: 0.021620
- Adam .001, wd1e-4, beta2=.999: 0.009119

The original Adam result was 0.019098. Lowering Adam’s rate or using beta2=.999 each improved substantially; removing decay did not. Therefore the apparent reversal is compatible with Adam hyperparameter sensitivity and does not establish an Adagrad-specific advantage.

All primary and follow-up runs completed with zero failures. New runs were uncached and had verified execution manifests, saved source files, and per-seed measurements. The observations were refreshed at epoch e0013.

Competing explanations include target/split sensitivity, the unfavorable Adam beta2=.95/rate/decay bundle, and finite-budget variance. Endpoint test MSE cannot distinguish late-fitting precision, instability, overfitting, or parameter-motion mechanisms.

A genuine Adagrad effect predicts E will continue beating Adam controls on additional matched splits. A beta2 explanation predicts Adam beta2=.999 will beat E; a rate explanation predicts Adam .0003 will beat E. At least three further metadata-selected splits with preregistered E, D, Adam-.0003, and Adam-beta2=.999 are needed. No universal Adagrad-first rule follows, and no causal mechanism was established.
