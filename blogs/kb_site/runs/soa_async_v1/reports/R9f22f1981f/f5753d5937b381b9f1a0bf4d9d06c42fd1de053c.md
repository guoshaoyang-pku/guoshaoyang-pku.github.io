q_9164e5 T512 Adagrad-vs-Adam replication report

Question and scope
Does the q_9164e5 reversal (plain ReLU d4w192, one LN at hidden layer 2, MSE; Adagrad lr .003/wd0, E, beating Adam lr .001, betas (.9,.95), wd 1e-4, D) replicate at T512 despite Adam's 3.77x nominal-progress proxy? The original target/split is not present as an executable lab candidate, so this is a report-only transfer test on two metadata-selected allowed 4-input oscillatory/additive symbolic-regression targets. Older T2048 evidence favoring Adam is not pooled with these T512 results.

Definitions and assumptions
A result is a test-MSE mean over CPU seeds 0--9 on one fixed 256-train/256-test split, with lower better. Paired comparisons use identical seed labels within a split. The nominal-progress ratio is a proxy, not measured parameter displacement. The two datasets are independent splits/targets; seed n=10 per split and split n=2. No evaluation gold was used.

Methods and provenance
Both tests used the same architecture: non-residual MLP, depth 4, width 192, ReLU, layer_norm [False, True, False, False], default initialization, MSE, T512, batch 16 (8192 samples). Dataset 1 was mvar_9ec635, expression x0*x2 + x0**2 + sin(2*pi*x1) + tanh(2*x2**2) + x3; dataset 2 was mvar_b0b978, expression cos(2*pi*x2)*sin(2*pi*x0) + cos(2*pi*x3)*x0 + sin(2*pi*x0) + tanh(2*x1) + 2*tanh(2*x2) + x3. Each full recipe was run for 10 seeds, no failures, uncached on first execution, with verified execution manifests and saved model/optimizer/loss/train sources. A refresh pinned observations at epoch e0013.

Paired 95% t9 intervals for E-D are [-0.01949, 0.01819] and [-0.01377, 0.02916], respectively; neither excludes zero. Thus E wins 1/2 splits, not a replicated reversal. The split-level point estimates have opposite signs, and the second split is a counterexample to transfer.

Follow-up controls on mvar_9ec635, same architecture/split/T512/b16/seeds: Adam .0003, wd1e-4, beta2 .95 mean 0.0082426 (SD .0021585); Adam .003, wd1e-4, beta2 .95 mean 0.0183803 (SD .0045461); Adam .001, wd0, beta2 .95 mean 0.0216202 (SD .0152947); Adam .001, wd1e-4, beta2 .999 mean 0.0091187 (SD .0041075). Original D is 0.0190977. These one-factor controls show lower Adam rate and beta2=.999 each outperform original D on split 1; removing decay does not.

Interpretation and competing explanations
Evidence supports a conditional exception on one analogue, not replication of a target-independent optimizer reversal. E's advantage on split 1 is small relative to seed dispersion and disappears across split 2. Plausible explanations are target/split sensitivity, an unfavorable Adam beta2=.95 or rate/decay combination, and finite-budget optimization variance. Controls make an Adagrad-specific mechanism unnecessary: Adam hyperparameters alone recover lower MSE on split 1. Endpoint test MSE cannot distinguish late-fitting precision, instability, overfitting, or parameter-motion mechanisms.

Distinguishable predictions and tests
A beta2 explanation predicts Adam beta2=.999 will beat E on further matched splits; a rate explanation predicts Adam .0003 will do so while beta2/wd stay fixed. A genuine Adagrad-specific effect predicts E continues to beat all Adam controls. Decisive test: at least three additional metadata-selected splits with same architecture, T512/b16 and preregistered E, D, Adam-.0003, and Adam-beta2=.999; inspect paired seed differences and train/test curves.

Boundaries and unresolved questions
The original q_9164e5 target and exact point seed were not reproduced, so no claim about its exact split is changed. Only two analogue splits, one architecture, one budget, and 10 seeds per split were measured. Paired intervals ignore split-selection uncertainty and multiplicity. Failure rate was 0/40 primary runs and 0/50 follow-up runs; cache was false for new runs and true only when re-reading the measured D baseline. No parameter/update traces or training MSE were collected. The result does not justify a universal Adagrad-first rule or pooling with older T2048 studies.

Reproducibility
Primary experiment records and per-seed measurements are retained by the lab with verified manifests; follow-up records retain the same source files and hashes.
