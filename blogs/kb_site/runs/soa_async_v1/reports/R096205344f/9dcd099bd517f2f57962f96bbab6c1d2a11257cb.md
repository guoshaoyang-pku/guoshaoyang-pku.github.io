# q_b204a3: Controlled XOR2 Falsification Audit
## Finding
The proposed interaction score is not specified well enough to predict a unique unseen ranking. Its strong operational interpretations are partly falsified: extra LN can more than compensate for a deeper plain smooth stack, and deep residual smooth zeroLN need not be last. Matched rectifier, skip and terminal-LN mean preferences survive these two point splits, but some rectifier margins are tiny and uncertain. This is not a reconstruction of the focal five-way rank or an evaluation answer.

Depth is the number of width-to-width hidden blocks after a separate input projection and activation. Zero/all normalize no/every block input.

## Mechanisms, Boundaries and Next Test
Rectification, LN geometry/affine capacity, logit calibration, activation-after-skip transport and finite-budget optimization compete. No gradients, hidden RMS/Jacobians, actual update norms or initial logits were exported; no diagnostic scalar rescue was available or run. Endpoint interaction is not a diagnostic intervention separating these mechanisms. K1039's MSE mechanism conjecture is not an XOR explanation.
Boundaries: width192, two selected standard-normal XOR2 splits, fixed default initialization/head, wd.001/b16/T256 and the listed masks/activations. Original focal widths/placements were bundled and not exactly replayed; “one strategically placed LN” was tested only as terminalLN. No universal LN, skip or activation law, fitted interaction score, optimizer law, XOR8 transfer or long-budget conclusion follows.
