## Results and Uncertainty
Per split, mean-sign successes were P1 **24/24**, P2 **18/18**, P3 **8/8**, P4 **0/2**, and P5 **35/44**. These comparisons share anchors, seeds and datasets; they are not independent replications.

Deep plain allLN minus shallow plain terminalLN:
- SiLU: **−.092638**, paired95%CI[−.116386,−.068891]; **−.098824**[−.122559,−.075090].
- GELU: **−.148068**[−.177678,−.118458]; **−.154059**[−.183499,−.124619].

All four contrasts favor the deeper allLN recipe in10/10 seeds, falsifying P4. Depth and LN change jointly in that contrast; the factorial retains the separating controls.

Deep residual GELU zeroLN beats shallow residual GELU zeroLN by **−.020871**[−.035485,−.006256] and **−.019728**[−.033841,−.005614]. It also beats deep plain terminalLN on both splits. Deep smooth zeroLN therefore is not inevitably last.

Tiny margins remain visible: deep plain zeroLN Leaky-minus-GELU is **−.000155**[−.000582,+.000272],7/10 seed wins, and **−.000122**[−.000554,+.000310],3/10 wins. Favorable means do not establish uniformly strong rectifier advantage.

The exploratory depth4-plain activation×LN CE interaction, `(Leaky−GELU at allLN)−(Leaky−GELU at zeroLN)`, is −.037483[−.042283,−.032683] and −.035186[−.039730,−.030642]. This is endpoint nonadditivity, not a mechanistic diagnosis.

Intervals are unadjusted paired t9 intervals conditional on fixed data. They assume approximately normal independent seed differences, lack multiplicity correction, and do not quantify target-population uncertainty.

## Mechanisms and Boundaries
Rectification, LN geometry/affine capacity, initial logit calibration, activation-after-skip composition and finite-budget optimization remain competing explanations. No gradients, hidden RMS/Jacobians, actual updates or scalar-rescue diagnostics were measured. Endpoint interventions do not distinguish these mechanisms.

Conclusions are bounded to the tested width, initialization/head, decay, batch, budget, masks, activations and two XOR2 splits. Original focal mixed-width/placement recipes were not exactly replayed. No universal LN, residual, activation or optimizer law follows.

**Unmeasured-setting prediction:** on allowed XOR2 `xorcls_17bdb0`, width256, otherwise identical SGD0 `.0003`/wd`.001`/T256/b16 settings, plain depth4 allLN will beat plain depth2 terminalLN for both SiLU and GELU in ten-seed mean CE. Either opposite mean sign rejects that transfer prediction.
