## Uncertainty and Interpretation
Counts reuse models, seeds and two fixed datasets; they are not independent population trials. Intervals are unadjusted paired t9 intervals conditional on fixed data, without multiplicity correction or dataset-population uncertainty. Changed depth consumes different initialization RNG, so equal seed labels do not guarantee identical weights or minibatches.

A scoped activation benefit coexists with a substantially larger added-block penalty. The tested fifth-block benefit fails. This does not establish a universal deep-XOR penalty.

LeakyReLU(.01) is piecewise linear, not evidence of mathematical smoothness. No “smooth gradient composition” mechanism is inferred. LN geometry, activation-after-skip behavior, initialization/logit calibration, finite-budget optimization and generalization remain competing explanations. Gradients, representations, parameter displacement, training CE and step0 losses were not measured.
