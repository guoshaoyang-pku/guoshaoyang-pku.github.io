# q_307d17: Equal-width Bundle and Transformer Depth Controls

## Findings
At the original recipe, four metadata-selected allowed V24/L12/alpha1 tables reproduce **C<D<E**: GRU64x2 beats TF64x1, and TF64x1 beats its depth2 twin. These are separate findings: a cross-family bundle comparison and a within-transformer depth intervention. Lowering only transformer learning rate from `.001` to `.0001` reverses the depth ordering on both tested tables. Neither result establishes a broad family law, initialization mechanism, or complete five-choice ranking.

## Predictions and Uncertainty
Preregistered **G<0 and H>0** on every selected table. Conditional preregistration: if H>0 consistently replicates, test only TF1/TF2 at lr.0001 on the first two tables, predicting H<0 with everything else fixed. The condition was met; no GRU rate grid was run.

Every cell has **10 finite seeds, zero failures and zero exclusions**. Pointwise 95% intervals use seed-label differences and t9. Seed labels are repeatability blocks, not matched weights or minibatches. All primary G contrasts have 10/10 negative differences; all primary H contrasts have 10/10 positive differences. Both low-rate H contrasts have 10/10 negative differences.

Intervals assume approximately normal seed differences on fixed tables and omit multiplicity, test-window and between-table uncertainty. Conservative marginal-SD sensitivity intervals retain every reported sign. Counts are descriptive, not independent population success estimates.

## Explanations and Boundaries
No initial CE/logits, current-token ablation, train CE, gradient/update scale or functional motion was measured. Saved test curves begin after updates. No initialization or current-token mechanism is claimed. Competing explanations include depth-conditioned optimization, default layer cloning/RNG trajectories, capacity/generalization and finite-table effects; positions and normalization additionally differ in the cross-family bundle.

Verified no-update baselines and endpoint progress could challenge an initial-loss-only explanation. Controlled minibatches with actual logits/motion could test mediation; train/test trajectories could test overfitting. These tests remain unexecuted.

Prospective prediction for the next eligible table, **bg_3f2142**: original recipe G<0 and H>0; TF-only lr.0001 H<0. Opposite means are sign counterexamples; intervals wholly opposite zero are stronger falsification. No numerical transfer range is asserted.

Do not pool with GRU64x1/TF32x4, V24/L24, V48/L12, other alpha, heads/FF, batch/budget, loss or initialization. A/B were not trained; no full five-choice order follows.
