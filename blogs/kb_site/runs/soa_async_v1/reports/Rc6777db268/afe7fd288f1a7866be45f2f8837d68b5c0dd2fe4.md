# RMSprop versus deep normalized Adam on hard 2-3-turn spirals

## Question and scope
The target is the ArchitectureIQ hard two/three-turn spiral regime at T=256: when can a shallow smooth RMSprop recipe beat a deeper normalized Adam-family recipe, and is RMSprop startup inflation sufficient to explain the reversal? CE is held-out cross-entropy, lower is better. A pair is a within-set comparison; pairs sharing a candidate are correlated. Historical ordinal questions provide winners but not margins. Lab summaries are ten-seed means/SDs; SD is seed spread, not a paired confidence interval.

|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 2 | 1.059 (S .538, D .508) | .717 (S .234, D .326) | 18.33 (S .301, D .0164) |

The reproducible boundary is narrow and non-monotone: RMSprop wins near .001 on both splits, not across the whole high-rate interval. At T=1024, the ratio was .416 on the 2-turn split (RMSprop mean .0412 versus Adam .0992) but 1.56 on the 3-turn split (RMSprop .408 versus Adam .262). At T=2048, the 2-turn RMSprop mean was .0286; the 3-turn RMSprop mean was .311, while corresponding deep-Adam cells were not all available in the same completed call, so no pooled T=2048 ranking is asserted. Seed SDs were often large (RMSprop .003/T1024 SD .0658 on 2 turns and .0909 on 3 turns), so endpoint means do not imply seedwise dominance. Accuracy was recorded per seed by the lab; no failures occurred.

## Startup-inflation test
The experiment API exposes standard RMSprop, Adam/AdamW, model, budget and rate overrides plus CE/accuracy curves. It does not expose bias-corrected RMSprop, an initialized accumulator, displacement-matched schedules, or parameter-norm trajectories. Therefore startup inflation is not isolated. The observed .001 pocket is compatible with transient accumulator effects, but also with architecture, LN, activation, rate, turns and seed/coordinate-scale interactions. No mechanism claim is warranted.

## Competing explanations and predictions
Startup inflation predicts that bias-corrected or preinitialized RMSprop removes the .001 pocket at fixed architecture/rate; this remains untested. Full-recipe optimization/capacity interaction predicts the narrow pocket and turn-dependent T transfer; present data are consistent. Nominal lr*T is not actual displacement for adaptive methods: 2-turn T1024 favors RMSprop while 3-turn T1024 favors Adam, preserving the empirical depth/progress conflict. Activation/LN interaction predicts that swapping activation or LN mask changes the boundary; prior controls support interactions but do not order the present recipes.

A third independent split should be tested before generalizing the .001 boundary. The decisive mechanism experiment is a two-architecture factorial with standard versus bias-corrected/preinitialized RMSprop, displacement-matched Adam, and logged parameter/function motion at T=256/1024/2048. Do not pool turns, inits, losses or budgets.

## Conclusion and boundaries
In the tested hard 2-3-turn regime, high-rate shallow smooth RMSprop beats the deeper normalized Adam-family recipe only in a narrow T256 neighborhood around lr=.001. It is not a general .001-.003 rule, and T transfer is turn-dependent. Startup inflation is plausible but untested, not a sufficient explanation established by these CE endpoints. The supported claim is a scoped recipe comparison; optimizer-only causality, a universal turns cutoff, and a universal RMSprop rate conversion remain unresolved.
