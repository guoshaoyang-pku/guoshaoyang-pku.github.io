## Interpretation and Unresolved Tests
A measured full-recipe mean reversal exists between257c7f and00ccf0, but it is opposite the proposed polynomial advantage and uncertain on00ccf0. It does not reproduce the original direction.

Competing explanations include rate/decay tuning, skip-dependent optimization, LN/width/depth, initial-output offset fitting, target scale, split variation and optimizer trajectories. Exact controls remove residual confounding locally but do not decompose the original width/depth/LN contrast. Finite endpoints do not exclude excursions; held-out loss cannot prove coherent gradients, overshoot, parity or generalization.

A broad gradient-coherence account predicts positive-polynomial SGD advantage under matched controls; these counterexamples weaken it. An offset account predicts centering changes early ordering. Generalization requires better train but worse test fit; fitting lag predicts worse train and test. Train/test trajectories, initial outputs, measured updates and fixed-checkpoint interventions would distinguish them; these tests remain unexecuted.

Future unmeasured prediction: another allowed four-input setting with exact d3/w192/FTT/.01/plain-MSE/T256/batch32 will favor Adam over momentum SGD at shared lr .0003/wd1e-5. A reproducible opposite mean sign narrows this local fact.

No original-split numerical reconstruction, target-only effect, independent-cohort reliability, population win probability, smooth/nonoscillatory extension, budget threshold or universal optimizer recommendation is established.
