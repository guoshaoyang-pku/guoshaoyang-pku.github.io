## Mechanisms, Tests and Boundaries
A local full-recipe mean reversal exists between measured allowed sets257c7f(B wins) and00ccf0(A mean wins),but is opposite the proposed polynomial advantage and uncertain in the latter. It is not a reproduction of the original target-dependent direction.
Competing explanations include rate/decay tuning,skip-dependent optimization,LN/width/depth effects,initial-output offset fitting,target scale,split variation and optimizer trajectory. Exact algorithm controls remove residual confounding locally but cannot decompose the original width/depth/LN contrast. Finite endpoints do not exclude excursions; held-out loss alone cannot prove coherent gradients,overshoot,actual parity or generalization.
A gradient-coherence explanation predicts matched positive-polynomial targets retain SGD advantage after controlling offset/scale and architecture; these polynomial counterexamples weaken its broad form. An offset-fitting explanation predicts centering changes early optimizer ordering. A generalization explanation requires better train but worse test fit; a fitting-lag explanation predicts worse train and test. Train/test trajectories,initial outputs,updates/gradients and fixed-weight/state interventions distinguish these; not executed.
Future unmeasured prediction: on another allowed four-input setting with exact d3/w192/FTT/.01/plain-MSE/T256/bs32,Adam at shared(.0003,1e-5)will beat momentumSGD. Any reproducible opposite mean sign narrows this local fact. This prediction is separate from the failed preregistered target rule.
No original-split numerical reconstruction,same-expression target-only effect,independent-seed-cohort reliability,population win probability,smooth/nonoscillatory extension,budget threshold or universal optimizer recommendation is established.
