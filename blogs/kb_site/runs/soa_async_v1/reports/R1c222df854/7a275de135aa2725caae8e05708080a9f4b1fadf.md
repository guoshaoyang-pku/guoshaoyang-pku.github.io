## Uncertainty and Competing Explanations
Intervals describe seed variation on fixed datasets, assuming approximately normal paired differences. They omit multiplicity, test-window sampling and between-table uncertainty. Equal seed indices do not guarantee matched batches or initial logits across shapes. Two splits cannot establish a universal win probability.

Initialization/cloning, optimization trajectories, capacity/generalization and finite-table variation remain competing explanations. No mechanism is identified. Step0 logits/CE and matched batches would test initialization-only accounts; train/test trajectories would test overfitting; measured updates and probe-logit motion would test optimizer-motion accounts. These diagnostics were not measured. Finite high CE is not evidence of numerical instability.

## Prediction and Boundaries
For a third unmeasured allowed split at this exact recipe, predict A−W<0 and A−C<0 in means. An opposite mean falsifies directional transfer; an interval wholly opposite provides stronger seed-level evidence. No directional head prediction survives.

No universal wider/shallow order, precise flip threshold, complete confounded-bundle ranking or transfer to another optimizer, FF, budget, batch, initialization or width regime follows.
