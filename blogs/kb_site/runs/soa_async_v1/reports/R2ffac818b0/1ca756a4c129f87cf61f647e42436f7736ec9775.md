## Does evidence establish safety at wd1e-4?
**No.** Although the older `q_fb4a7f` comment said lab spirals were all T256, the available lab now contains prior T2048 experiments. In `exp:3dbc82fbb876`, allowed split `spiralcls_0516f4` has 1.5 turns and a fixed residual SiLU depth2 width256 model, LN[true,true], Adam betas(.9,.95), batch16:
| Adam lr | wd1e-5 CE ± SD | wd1e-4 CE ± SD |
|---|---:|---:|
| .0001 | 1.4944e-5 ± 6.6274e-6 | 1.22683e-4 ± 2.30171e-5 |
| .001 | 1.74117e-5 ± 1.98780e-5 | 1.25229e-4 ± 2.06874e-5 |
At fixed rate, wd1e-4 increases mean CE **8.209× and 7.192×**. The B-style/A-style recipe ratio is **8.380×**. Fixed-decay high/low-rate ratios are only 1.165 and 1.021, so this evidence does not establish a strong rate-only penalty.
This is prior evidence on one allowed split, not a new prediction or reproduction on the original question’s point seed1595516977. Absolute losses remain small; “safe” has no declared tolerance. Neither safety nor a universal decay threshold follows. Gradient, norm and margin trajectories were not measured.

Apply twin evidence only with remaining settings matched; do not rank incomparable recipes or infer safety below the tested decay. The later normalized-SiLU T2048 factorial remains separately scoped.

## Uncertainty and next tests
Per-seed paired losses and trajectories are unavailable, so no paired significance claim is made. The strong matched no-LN effect covers one dataset and architecture; normalized near-ties and large SiLU seed SD limit confidence. Historical aggregate sets cover 2–3 turns, but the new intervention covers only two turns. Transfer to residual/deeper nets, other optimizers, budgets or unseen datasets remains unestablished.
Competing explanations include activation-specific optimization, normalization interactions, capacity/residual differences and decay differences. CE interventions distinguish some conditional effects but not their mechanisms. Adam’s coupled-L2 implementation alone does not demonstrate decay-gradient dominance.
Predictions for unmeasured conditions: on a three-turn matched factorial, high-rate no-LN smooth separation should exceed all-LN separation; on another 1.5-turn T2048 matched decay factorial, wd1e-4 should exceed wd1e-5 CE at each rate. Test these predictions before extending either claim. Measure trajectories before asserting gradient/margin or radial-information explanations.
