## Uncertainty, predictions and boundaries
Seed SD is reported; per-seed paired losses and trajectories are unavailable, so no paired significance claim is made. The strong high-rate no-LN effect has only one matched dataset/architecture; normalized near-ties and the large SiLU SD limit confidence. Original aggregate sets cover 2 and 3 turns plus 2.5 turns, but the new intervention covers only 2 turns. No transfer is established to residual/deeper nets, other optimizers, budgets, sampling noise or unseen datasets.
Competing explanations include activation-specific optimization, normalization interactions, capacity/residual differences and decay differences. Measured CE distinguishes some conditional effects but not their underlying mechanisms. Adam’s implementation couples L2 into gradients; that fact alone does not measure dominance of decay gradients or explain these outcomes.
Next unmeasured tests: replicate the activation factorial on a 3-turn split (prediction: no-LN high-rate smooth separation exceeds all-LN separation), and the T2048 rate-by-decay factorial on another 1.5-turn split (prediction: wd1e-4 exceeds wd1e-5 CE at each rate). Test these preregistered predictions with matched cells; measure trajectories before making gradient/margin or radial-information claims.
