## Competing predictions
Early adaptive advantage predicts lower early RMSprop training loss at equal rate, and higher-rate Adam closes or reverses it; late-precision account predicts rate-dependent losses appearing later, possibly train/test both worsening. Test-only deterioration with better train fit favors generalization. Endpoint signs alone identify none of these. Absolute rate, target/split and architecture are gates; ratio is not sufficient causal explanation.
