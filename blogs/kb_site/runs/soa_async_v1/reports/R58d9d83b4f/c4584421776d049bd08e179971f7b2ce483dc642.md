## Methods and uncertainties
Compare only within each returned set. Archive resolved configs, dataset cards, means, SDs, finite/failure counts, margins and ratios. Ten-seed SD is not a paired confidence interval; aggregate output cannot reconstruct paired uncertainty. No prior surviving experiment will be rerun. Audit original lab separately from prior controlled experiments, match dataset/set, budget, model including initialization and loss, and distinguish architecture-confounded pairs.

Endpoint held-out MSE cannot establish overfitting, annealing or accumulator mechanisms. Distinguishing tests would require train/test trajectories, measured updates, state resets or schedules; these are not measured here. No universal low-Delta or Adagrad preference is proposed. This publication is a reviewable preregistration, not a completed theory.
