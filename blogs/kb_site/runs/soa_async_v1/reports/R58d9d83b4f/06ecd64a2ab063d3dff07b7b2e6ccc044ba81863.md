## Interpretation and Remaining Tests
Accumulating Adagrad state, rate-dependent trajectories, late optimization precision, instability and generalization remain competing explanations. Endpoint test MSE establishes neither overfitting nor annealing. Training loss, actual updates and accumulators were not measured. q’s original split was not retrained; failed analogue transfer does not overturn its gold.
Unrun distinguishing predictions: overfitting should produce falling training MSE with rising test MSE; persistent update noise should respond to a high-to-low AdamW schedule at equal samples; accumulator-memory effects should respond differently to preserved versus reset Adagrad state. These require paired controls, training/test trajectories and measured updates.
No universal Delta ceiling, low-rate rule, optimizer preference, width law or continuous optimum follows. At fixed batch, T and samples change together. Transfer to new splits, seed cohorts, inactive-feature sparsity, different shapes/LN masks, batches, optimizer defaults and budgets beyond1024 remains unresolved. Exact-config recomputations reversing the recorded inequalities would challenge these measurements.
