## Competing Explanations, Predictions and Boundaries
Depth, width/parameter count, residual topology, activation, LN placement and their optimizer interactions are bundled. Target-specific approximation/fitting, trajectory differences and confidence/generalization differences remain competing explanations. No measured train CE, logits, boundary representation or actual displacement supports ReLU kink matching, shallow underfitting, SiLU smoothing, or a .1 overtaking mechanism. The target rule switches branches and cannot be reduced to a measured continuous-kink fit.
Distinguishing prediction for an UNRUN activation-only test: swapping B's ReLU to SiLU at fixed d4/w96/plain/LN0001/RMSprop/b16/T1024 may differ by instance; a universal smoothness account predicts lower CE on both, whereas a recipe-interaction account allows loss or negligible gain. Predeclare lower CE on both as the universal account's test, then run paired seeds and retain contrary results. An UNRUN depth-only fixed-width/LN-placement series would test whether B's advantage survives removal of width/residual/activation confounds. Neither result is implied by these bundled transfers.
To separate fitting from confidence, collect train CE, test CE/accuracy and logits over time; fit temperature only on held-out training rows, then lock test evaluation. A confidence explanation predicts differential CE improvement without accuracy change; absence challenges it. For threshold claims, predeclare a fixed architecture activation-only lr-by-T grid around .1, keeping lr and T as separate factors. We predict no evidence warrants a universal abrupt crossover; this remains untested, not a fitted law.
Extrapolation is limited to the named full recipes, optimizer defaults, two allowed four-feature piecewise instances, two budgets and fixed row counts. No global ReLU/depth preference, pooled total ranking, feature-count transfer, different-optimizer claim or exact-original replication follows.
