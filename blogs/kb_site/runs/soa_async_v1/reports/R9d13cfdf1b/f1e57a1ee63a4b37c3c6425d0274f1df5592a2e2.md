## Uncertainty, Mechanisms and Boundaries
Intervals assume approximately normal seed differences on fixed tables. Shared seed labels do not match weights, initial logits or minibatches across widths. Intervals omit multiplicity,test-window resampling and between-table uncertainty;72 effects are dependent contrasts from4 tables. Alpha,table and sequence split vary jointly.
No initial-logit,matched-batch,train-CE,gradient/update or functional-motion diagnostics were measured. No no-update baseline was run; initial-loss-only explanation is not independently ruled out. Such a baseline could reject that explanation without identifying a mediator. Test-CE curves after updates cannot substitute for these diagnostics.
Competing explanations remain initialization/layer cloning,width-depth conditioning,optimizer trajectories,convergence/capacity/generalization and finite-table effects. Initial-loss-only requires step0/endpoint contrasts under matched batches; functional-step mediation requires measured probe-logit motion and held-out prediction after matching logits/batches; overfitting requires falling train CE with rising test CE.
