## Finding

## Competing explanations, distinguishable tests and boundaries
Matching default initialization is not matching initial logits: width alters parameter tensors and RNG consumption before minibatch sampling. No initial CE/logit variance, matched-batch diagnostic, train CE, gradient/update norms or parameter/probe-logit motion was measured. Archived test-CE curves start after updates and cannot supply those mediators. No no-update baseline was run; initial-loss-only explanation is not separately ruled out. Even such a baseline could reject that explanation without identifying the cause.
Candidates remain initialization/layer cloning, width-depth conditioning, optimizer trajectories, convergence/capacity/generalization and finite-table effects. Initial-loss-only predicts trained contrasts stay explained by step0 contrasts under matched batches; a no-update baseline plus step0/endpoint contrasts tests that, but not functional-step causation. Step mediation predicts measured first-step or cumulative probe-logit motion collapses strata and forecasts held-out contrasts after matched initial logits and batch schedules; equal measured motion with unequal outcomes refutes that version. Overfitting predicts declining train CE with rising test CE; held-out CE alone cannot test it. Rate rescue alone identifies none.
Prospective unmeasured third-table prediction at this EXACT recipe: d128 beats64 at .05 in both depths, loses at .3 in both depths, and at .1 wins depth1 but loses depth4 in MEANS; uncertainty for small deep1.4 effects remains. Joint scaling should worsen d128 at .05 and improve it at .3, while both-scaled width order remains wider-at.05/narrower-at.3. Reliable opposite paired signs falsify those transfer predictions. No new exact effect range is claimed outside frozen four-table tests.
Do not extrapolate to RMSprop/Adagrad, other vocab/context, FF/head/batch/budget/loss, alternate initialization or a sharp .1 boundary. The inherited .768 deep reversal remains a counterexample to monotone high-rate rules. Four fresh tables do not establish finished theory, an optimum rate, or a continuous width/depth threshold.
