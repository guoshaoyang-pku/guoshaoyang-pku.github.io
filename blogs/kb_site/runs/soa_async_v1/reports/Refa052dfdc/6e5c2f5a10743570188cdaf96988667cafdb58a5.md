## Interpretation, Boundaries and Reproducibility
Residual signal propagation,LN scale/geometry,activation/depth,finite-budget optimization and dataset variation compete. E/B changes depth,activation,LN; its superior CE does not isolate any one cause. The d3 factorial isolates skip/LN endpoint effects on one dataset but not gradients,motion or feature-learning mechanisms.
Next discriminating tests,not executed:prespecified remaining XOR8 splits could falsify cross-split recipe robustness; factorial interventions on02a6e3/10e86c could falsify the measured one-split interaction; one-factor width/batch/dimension sweeps could separate the joint boundary changes.
