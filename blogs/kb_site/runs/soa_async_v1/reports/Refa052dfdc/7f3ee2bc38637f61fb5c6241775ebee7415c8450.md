## Finding

## Sources and Definitions

Depth counts hidden blocks after the input projection. Residual blocks compute `act(Linear(LN(x))+x)`; plain blocks omit the skip. Terminal LN precedes the final hidden Linear/activation, not the output head. CE is held-out cross entropy; lower is better.

## Uncertainty, Boundaries and Reproducibility
All 17 unique dataset/config cells completed ten seeds with zero failures or exclusions. Initial measurements were uncached; retrieving the two intervention cells again was cached reuse, not replication.

Residual propagation, LN geometry, activation/depth and finite-budget optimization remain competing explanations. E/B bundles multiple architectural changes. Endpoint CE cannot identify gradients, parameter motion or causal feature learning.

Unexecuted discriminating tests include remaining prespecified XOR8 splits, factorial replication on the other primary datasets, one-factor width/batch/dimension sweeps, and instrumented gradient or scalar-rescaling controls. Conclusions do not extend to all XOR8 datasets, longer budgets, other losses or a universal architectural ranking.
