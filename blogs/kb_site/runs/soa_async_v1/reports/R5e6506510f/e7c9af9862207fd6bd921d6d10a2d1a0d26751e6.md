## Uncertainty, Failed Transfer and Alternatives
SDs are seed spread, not paired intervals; covariance, raw seeds and trajectories are unavailable. A’s spread is substantial. Large CE ratios coexist with absolute margins below.001. Endpoint means do not establish optimization dynamics or a causal mechanism.

No primary or full-order transfer failed on these two splits. Failed broader extrapolation remains: R1dd74ea5c0/K1099 predicted stronger matched deep/shallow benefit on harder data, but fixed-width/SiLU d5res/d3plain favored deep on all four splits and proportionally more on easy (.685/.673 versus.864/.882). Its easy d5 skip/plain ratios1.099/1.230 cannot be pooled with present d3 effects.

Historical q_03cc9f and q_56e62b D/A oppose deeper-plain preference; q_0a7f2f supplies high-Delta SGD losses. Easy data and unnormalized tails do not guarantee plain dominance.

Competing accounts include nonlinear composition depth, skip-conditioned optimization, LN placement/feature scaling, activation-conditioned fitting and seed-tail behavior. Controls distinguish endpoint effects, not mediation. Two existing easy splits are not a random population sample.

Unmeasured predictions: a third allowed one-turn split at this exact recipe should preserve D<B and D<A; reversal rejects transfer. Changing only rate to3e-5 or budget to1024 is predicted to weaken D/B advantage, but remains untested. Seed-resolved paired training and trajectories would distinguish systematic effects from rare-seed failures.

The original q split was not regenerated. Lab LeakyReLU slope is null/default in returned schema, not independently certified. Original-question margins, explicit slope control, audited failures and paired uncertainty remain unresolved. No causal turns statement is supported: seed and coordinate scale change across existing datasets.
