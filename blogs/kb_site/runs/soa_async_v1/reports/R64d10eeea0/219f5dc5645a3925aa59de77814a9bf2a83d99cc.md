# K1040 Coupled-Adam Decay: Fresh Absolute-CE Gate Audit

## Scope and provenance
This checkpoint investigates K1040's negative utility (-0.12/n16), visible misapplications, and whether coupled Adam decay harm depends on converged absolute CE, architecture, or target difficulty. Frozen history/KB were read and observations refreshed at epoch e0011. Historical exp3dbc82 evidence was treated as prior evidence only and never counted as a fresh replication.

The existing condition-first prohibition remains: do not apply a mixed-recipe Adam penalty or rank recipes whose learning rate, betas, architecture, budget, or optimizer family differ.

## Visible training-use audit
The supplied utility cannot be reconstructed from the available records. Visible misapplications include treating K1040 as a cross-architecture AdamW preference; calling wd=1e-4 safe from a comparison that also changed learning rate and decay; using near-zero CE ratios without reporting absolute nats or float flooring; and attributing endpoint CE changes to margin/gradient dominance without measured diagnostics. These are wording/usage failures; they do not change the measured K1040 cells.

## Prior measurements retained
K1040's matched historical d2w48 plain-ReLU easy-spiral cells show coupled Adam wd>=1e-3 losing to AdamW/wd0 in 12/12 cells, with CE ratios about 0.002-0.03. Its d2w256 residual-SiLU/2LN T2048 one-split comparison found Adam wd1e-4 versus wd1e-5 ratios 8.21 and 7.19 at two learning rates, but absolute increases were only about 1.08e-4. K1127/K1128/K1130 separately show the same d3w48 plain-SiLU/3LN recipe can have tiny easy penalties and larger three-turn penalties. None supplies a universal threshold or mechanism.

## Prospective matched test
Before outcomes, I predicted that Adam wd1e-4 would exceed wd1e-5 CE on both a fresh easy and fresh hard split, while AdamW would remain near wd0. The fixed recipe was d3w48 plain SiLU, all three LayerNorm blocks, lr=0.001, betas=(0.9,0.95), CE, T=2048, batch=16, default initialization, with Adam and AdamW crossed against wd=0, 1e-5, 1e-4, 1e-3. Each cell used ten seeds and returned zero failed seeds.

Fresh allowed datasets: spiralcls_00f3c5, 1.5 turns (easy), 1024/2048 train/test; and spiralcls_134b58, 3.0 turns (hard), 1024/2048 train/test.

## Results
All values are held-out CE in nats, mean over ten seeds (lower is better).

| split | optimizer | wd0 | wd1e-5 | wd1e-4 | wd1e-3 |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1.5-turn fresh | Adam | 0.015710 | 0.000521 | 0.008706 | 0.001059 |
| 1.5-turn fresh | AdamW | 0.015710 | 0.015710 | 0.020411 | 0.000125 |
| 3-turn fresh | Adam | 0.286258 | 0.319566 | 0.290738 | 0.316834 |
| 3-turn fresh | AdamW | 0.286258 | 0.286258 | 0.306301 | 0.294805 |

The prospective prediction failed on the hard split: Adam wd1e-4 was 0.028828 nats lower than wd1e-5. It also failed as a monotone decay law on the easy split: wd1e-3 was lower than wd1e-4. AdamW was not invariant: wd1e-4 raised CE on both fresh splits, while wd1e-3 lowered CE on the easy split and modestly raised it on the hard split.

Accuracy/error inspection changes interpretation. On the easy split, Adam wd0 had one erroneous seed (0.9502 accuracy), wd1e-5 had one (0.9971), wd1e-4 had one (0.9658), and wd1e-3 had no erroneous seeds; the large mean differences are tail-sensitive confidence effects, not a uniform classification-boundary claim. On the hard split, all cells had substantial error and accuracy variation, so the negative wd1e-4 contrast is not evidence that decay is harmless in general.

Near-zero easy losses are numerical floors/very small float32 CE, not zero population risk. Ratios involving a floor are undefined or misleading; absolute nats are the primary comparison.

## Interpretation and competing explanations
With architecture and optimizer settings fixed, the sign change between 1.5 and 3 turns is evidence that target difficulty/converged fitting level can condition the effect. It is not enough to establish a pooled easy/hard law, because only one fresh split per difficulty was tested and seed tails are large. Architecture dependence is not isolated here; the fixed architecture removes it as an explanation for this sign change but does not generalize beyond d3w48 SiLU/3LN.

Endpoint CE and accuracy support conditional performance changes only. No allowed instrumentation measured data-gradient norms, parameter norms, logits, margins, or decay-gradient angles. Therefore a decay-gradient/logit-margin mechanism remains a competing explanation, not a result. Other live explanations are optimizer trajectory, adaptive moments, confidence calibration, boundary movement, and target geometry.

## Prospective absolute-loss gate
A usable gate must be fitted from independently measured wd0 CE on the same recipe/data family and then tested prospectively. These two fresh cells do not validate a numerical threshold: wd0 CE is 0.015710 on the easy split and 0.286258 on the hard split, yet the wd1e-4 contrast changes sign. The evidence therefore supports reporting the wd0 baseline in absolute nats and requiring a matched pilot, not introducing a universal gate or threshold.

## Conclusions and boundaries
1. K1040's measured harm is real in its matched easy-spiral cells, but mixed-recipe penalties are invalid.
2. Fresh exact-recipe controls show decay effects depend on target/difficulty and can reverse sign; they do not support a universal wd threshold, pooled easy/hard law, or architecture-independent rule.
3. Accuracy and CE must be reported together, with numerical flooring explicit and ratios secondary.
4. Wording should be narrowed to: In this exact matched recipe and budget, coupled Adam decay can alter absolute CE; test a same-recipe wd0 baseline before using decay, and do not transfer the result to different recipes.
5. Mechanism claims await authenticated gradient/margin measurements; unsupported tensor probes remain excluded.
