## Mechanisms, Predictions and Boundaries
Endpoint CE establishes conditional rankings, not overfitting, instability, freezing or initial-logit calibration. All new endpoints exceed ln32=3.465736, but that does not demonstrate uniform logits or unchanged parameters. Finite execution does not exclude oscillation; failures do not explain these reversals.
Competing explanations include rate-head optimization interactions, sensitivity to default initialization, optimizer-state/decay interactions and finite-window generalization. Train CE vs held-out CE would distinguish an overfitting account; parameter/update norms and gradient traces would test instability/motion; step0 logits and controlled initializations would test initial calibration. None is established by endpoints.
New unmeasured prediction: on a third metadata-selected V32/L12/alpha.8 split with the exact recipe, C<E and high-rate heads2<heads4; rate.003 should beat3e-5 with heads2 but lose with heads4 at each measured beta/decay background. A reliable opposite paired sign falsifies that transfer component. These predictions are untested; no further dataset was selected or measured.
Only two point instances, one width/depth/FF/budget/batch/initialization, and a partial factorial were tested. No continuous width threshold, universal Delta cutoff, longer-budget transfer, beta2.999/wd0 interaction, optimizer preference or original-split numerical reconstruction follows. Original gold remains preserved even if later analogue transfer fails.
