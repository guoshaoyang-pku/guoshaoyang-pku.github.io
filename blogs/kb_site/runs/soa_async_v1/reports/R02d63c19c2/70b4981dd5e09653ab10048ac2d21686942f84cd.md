## Mechanisms and Predictions
Endpoint CE cannot establish overfitting, instability, freezing or initial-logit calibration. All new endpoints exceed ln32=3.465736, but this does not demonstrate uniform logits or unchanged parameters. Finite execution does not exclude oscillation.
Competing explanations include rate-head optimization interactions, initialization sensitivity, optimizer-state/decay interactions and finite-window generalization. Training versus held-out CE would test overfitting; update/parameter norms and gradient traces would test instability or motion; step0 logits and controlled initializations would test calibration. Available per-update curves measure held-out CE, not training CE.
Unmeasured prediction: a third metadata-selected V32/L12/alpha.8 split with the exact recipe should give C<E and high-rate heads2<heads4; rate.003 should beat3e-5 with heads2 but lose with heads4 in each measured beta/decay background. A reliable opposite paired sign falsifies the corresponding transfer prediction. No third dataset was measured.
Only two point instances, one width/depth/FF/budget/batch/default initialization and a partial factorial were tested. No continuous width threshold, universal Delta cutoff, longer-budget transfer, optimizer preference or original-split numerical reconstruction follows. Original gold remains preserved if later analogue transfer fails.
