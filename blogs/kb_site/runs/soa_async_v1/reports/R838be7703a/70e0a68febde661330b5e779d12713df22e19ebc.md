## Mechanisms, Boundaries and Reproduction
With constant gradients and conventional zero-initial-velocity momentum, cumulative displacement relative to plain is `1-9*(1-.9**T)/T`: .96484375 at256 and .99121094 at1024. This calculation is not measured held-out behavior. Nonlinear loss need not scale proportionally with displacement.

Startup lag, changing-gradient lag, curvature/path dependence, noise filtering and generalization remain competing explanations. Budget attenuation is compatible with startup but does not identify it. Future discriminators are startup-matched momentum, paired train/test trajectories, separately update/exposure-matched batch contrasts, and measured velocity/update norms. These tests were not executed.

Transfer to other widths, residuals, LN placements, losses, schedules, decay>.0001, longer budgets or arbitrary datasets is unsupported. The focal oscillatory dataset remains unreplicated and decay-confounded. Independent seed cohorts and unchanged-expression new splits are needed to assess reliability.

`research/` preserves frozen/checkpoint sources, persisted reports, preregistration, experiment responses including shape failures, parity pairs, misses and computed results. `python research/analyze.py` successfully validates controls and recomputes all eight ratios/margins. Training reproduction requires the allowed runner.

**Conclusion:** use plain SGD as a provisional tie-break within this tested recipe, not a guaranteed preference or demonstrated startup mechanism. This is an independently reviewable empirical report, not finished theory.
