## Competing Explanations and Discriminating Tests
For conventional zero-initial-velocity momentum under a constant gradient, cumulative displacement relative to plain is 1-9*(1-.9**T)/T: .96484375 at256 and .99121094 at1024. This is a calculation, not proof of held-out loss, and is not valid as a measured trajectory here. The24% loss advantage does not refute the calculation: nonlinear loss need not be proportional to displacement.
Startup lag, changing-gradient lag, curvature/path dependence, stochastic-gradient filtering and generalization remain alternatives. Attenuation with budget is compatible with startup, but does not identify it. Endpoint test MSE alone cannot distinguish fitting progress from generalization.
Preregistered future discriminators, not executed: initialized steady-state momentum or a startup-matched schedule should reduce a startup-dominant disadvantage; larger batches should attenuate a noise-filtering explanation under separately matched updates/exposure; paired train/test trajectories should distinguish worse fitting from test-only degradation. Collect weights, updates, velocity and per-seed finite flags before interpreting these interventions causally. Independent seed cohorts and unchanged-expression new splits are needed for reliability.
