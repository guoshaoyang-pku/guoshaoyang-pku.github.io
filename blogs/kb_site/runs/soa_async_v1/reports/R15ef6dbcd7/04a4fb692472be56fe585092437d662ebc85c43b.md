T256 to T512 doubles updates and sampled draws from 16,384 to 32,768, or 64 to 128 nominal exposures. On `02036a`, all four cells and every seed improve. E changes `-.215431`, CI `[-.293774,-.137088]`; D changes `-.034531`, CI `[-.049327,-.019735]`.

On `071e42`, E changes `-.032874`, CI `[-.099298,.033550]`, with 5/10 improving; D **worsens** by `+.061491`, CI `[.015689,.107293]`, with 2/10 improving. Budget intervals are exploratory pointwise intervals.

All eight T256 curves exactly match their corresponding T512 prefixes. These are trajectory extensions, not independent replications. The full-recipe gap narrows without reversing; on the second target, narrowing partly reflects D worsening. Steps and exposure were not independently manipulated. Target expressions, offsets/scales and point seeds also change jointly across datasets.

## Interpretation and Unresolved Tests
Rate/decay tuning, network conditioning, target offset/scale and fitting/generalization trajectories remain competing explanations. Same-network SiLU controls rule out attributing the entire disadvantage solely to E’s architecture. Longer budget alone does not restore E’s advantage.

No gradient, update-norm, optimizer-state or training-loss measurements establish momentum causality, effective `lr/3` parity, coherent gradients or overfitting. Distinguishing these explanations requires train/test trajectories, optimizer diagnostics, common-rate/decay algorithm controls and separate update/exposure interventions.

For an unmeasured third outcome-blind allowed eight-input target, predict the AdamW recipe beats SGD on the exact E network at T512/b64. A robust opposite sign would narrow this local finding. No universal SGD/adaptive ordering, original-target numeric replication, full five-choice reconstruction or population win probability is established.
