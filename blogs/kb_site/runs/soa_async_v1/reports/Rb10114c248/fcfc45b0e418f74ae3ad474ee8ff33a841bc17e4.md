## Interpretation, Competing Explanations and Tests
C's .00768 nominal Delta does not prevent a measured advantage at alpha.8; B's larger nominal progress does not force a loss at alpha1. Thus both a max-progress law and universal conservative-recipe preference fail as interpretations. Alpha, table law and sequence split change jointly; replicated within-alpha signs are not an alpha intervention or cutoff estimate. No isolating optimizer-type, decay or rate claim is made.
No training failures explain these endpoint differences. A is close to ln32=3.465736, but CE proximity does not establish uniform logits, no conditioning, freezing or overshoot. C/B CE advantages establish endpoint recipe rankings, not their cause.
Competing mechanisms remain report-only: convergence/optimizer-state differences; default-initialization or cloned-layer conditioning; finite-table target difficulty; generalization from finite windows. Measure step0 logits and input-conditioning information to test calibration/conditioning; strong conditioning despite near-ln32 CE refutes a uniform-logit explanation. Measure train CE alongside test CE to test overfitting; absence of falling train CE with rising test CE rejects that specific story. Gradient/update norms and cumulative versus endpoint parameter/probe-logit motion are needed to test instability or overshoot; finite endpoints are not those measurements.
An isolated-rate/optimizer/decay factorial with the exact shared initialization and batch schedule would distinguish recipe components. Controlled alpha resampling on the same underlying Gaussian table and sequence protocol would test alpha causally; current two-table strata cannot. Available archived per-update curves are test CE, not training CE or parameter trajectories.
