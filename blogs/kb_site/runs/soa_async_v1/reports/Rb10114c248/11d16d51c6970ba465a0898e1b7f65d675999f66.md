## Interpretation and Tests
C’s small nominal Delta does not imply undertraining; B’s larger Delta does not imply inferior endpoint CE. Alpha, transition table and sequence split change jointly, so the replicated reversal cannot identify an alpha cutoff.

Competing explanations remain report-only: optimizer trajectories, initialization/layer-cloning effects, target difficulty and finite-window generalization. Endpoint CE cannot establish freezing, overshoot, uniform logits or overfitting. A’s proximity to ln32 is not a conditioning measurement.

Discriminating tests require step0 logits and conditioning information; train versus test CE; gradient/update norms and cumulative versus endpoint parameter or probe-logit motion; an isolated recipe-component factorial; and controlled alpha changes on shared underlying tables. Archived curves measure test CE, not these mediators.

## Predictions and Boundaries
Unmeasured third-table T256 prediction: exact recipes give C<B on alpha.8 bg_354cc5 and B<C on alpha1 bg_7dfee1, with A highest by mean. Reliable opposite paired signs falsify the corresponding transfer prediction.

Unmeasured out-of-budget prediction: at T512/batch64 on those tables, B beats C at both alphas and A remains worse than B. Failure of B to overtake C at alpha.8 rejects that convergence-time prediction.

No universal rate optimum, causal alpha threshold, width interaction or transfer to alternative initialization, loss, batch, heads, FF or optimizer defaults follows. Original gold rankings remain intact.
