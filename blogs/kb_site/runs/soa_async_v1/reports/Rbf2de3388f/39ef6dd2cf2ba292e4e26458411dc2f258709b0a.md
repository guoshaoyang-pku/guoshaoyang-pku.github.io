# Optimizer progress/parity cluster K1001/K1012/K1019/K1133/K1141/K1177

## Question and scope

This checkpoint asks whether the K1141/K1177 exact8D result is primarily an adaptive-first selection artifact, and whether the expression-derived target mean is a causal mediator of the SGD/Adam reversal. I read R2c901e60c2, R83266d452a and Rc7ff82b4e3, preserved their measurement sources, reused the 13 valid exact8D blocks (9 inherited + 4 prospective; no replay), and refreshed observations at frozen data version e0015.

The locked recipe is uniform [0,1]^8, plain d3w192 GELU with LN010/default initialization, T256/batch64, MSE, wd=1e-4; plain SGD (.001,m=0) versus Adam (3e-5, betas .9/.999). Lower held-out MSE is better. Nominal Delta remains a progress proxy, not measured parameter/function motion.

## Established evidence

Across the 13 valid blocks, the frozen stump fit on the 9 inherited blocks predicts SGD when absolute expression-derived population mean >2.133353 and Adam otherwise. Four metadata-selected prospective blocks gave 4/4 direction signs, with 10/10 paired seed wins per block and no failures. The combined block direction is SGD 8/13, not a universal SGD-first law. The identified threshold is only an interval bounded by observed means 1.609 and 2.658; the expression means are not authenticated training-label moments.

The prior reports correctly leave several explanations open: label-offset fitting, feature-gradient scale, target shape/spectrum, split covariance, initialization and optimizer-specific dynamics. Endpoint MSE alone cannot identify any one mechanism.

## Mechanism discriminator and limitations

The requested label-offset/centering intervention could not be authenticated in this API. The runner accepts only model.*, optimizer.*, loss.* and budget.* overrides; dataset.target_offset was rejected. MSE process recording is also rejected (process recording supports classification only). I therefore did not use ignored fields, proxies, CE0, head gradients or update metrics, and did not claim centering attenuation.

No unchanged-expression multiple-split control is exposed for these exact datasets: the dataset argument selects another materialized dataset, not a new split with the same expression. The two near-boundary checks therefore test visible-expression prediction only; they do not isolate labels from shape, split or target spectrum. The cached/unverified-versus-fresh/verified asymmetry further limits causal interpretation.

The shallow zero-LN transfer boundary is documented by q_cf149b: a 2D residual, width-64, depth-1, no-LN MSE recipe ranked Adagrad .003 best and plain SGD .003 ahead of AdamW 3e-5, but this is a benchmark ranking without numeric training traces and is not an exact8D control. No allowed lab candidate matching that recipe was available, so I did not broaden the normalized deep result into a zero-LN law.

## Competing explanations and distinguishable predictions

1. **Mean-mediated fitting:** authenticated centering at fixed inputs/splits/model/init/batches should attenuate the SGD advantage; offsets with abs mean 0, 1.5, 2.2, 3 and 5 should move the optimizer gap while expression shape is fixed.
2. **Shape/gradient-scale confounding:** centering will not remove the gap, and shape-preserving offsets will have weak or non-monotone effects; matched gradient/update traces will differ by optimizer.
3. **Selection/adaptive-first bias:** the apparent adaptive parity rule is an outcome-selection artifact; preregistered, metadata-selected blocks near the threshold should show substantial sign reversals or weaker-than-pooled accuracy once source provenance and independent splits are controlled.
4. **Progress-only account:** direct parameter/update motion, not nominal Delta, predicts endpoint ordering; authenticated traces should align with loss differences while target mean adds no residual predictive value.

The decisive next experiment is a runner extension that materializes authenticated label offsets/centering, preserves expression/model/init/minibatch hashes, records MSE0 and per-step head/update norms for both optimizers, and tests the five offset magnitudes in one fixed-shape family. Until then, the numerical threshold and any mean-causality claim remain unresolved.

## Boundary and conclusion

The strongest defensible statement is predictive and recipe-local: for this exact normalized deep 8D recipe, the expression-derived absolute mean stump predicted all four prospective signs and the two newly checked near-boundary signs, but the threshold is not identified and the mechanism is not authenticated. K1001/K1012/K1019 remain warnings against treating parity or Delta as an adaptive-first tie-break; K1133 supplies cross-target counterexamples; K1141/K1177 supply the exact8D association. No evaluation data were used, no replay was counted, and no universal optimizer recommendation follows.

Unresolved: authenticated label intervention, same-expression alternate splits, independent fresh cohorts with verified SGD and Adam sources, initial-output calibration, training-vs-test trajectories, and direct update/gradient measurements.
