## Definitions and methods
Use q_07bc60's EXACT optimizer/schedule: AdamW lr .003, betas (.9,.999), weight decay .0001, T256, batch16 (4096 examples seen), default initialization and cross entropy. Architecture-only panel fixes width192/ReLU and crosses depth2/4, residual off/on, first-only versus terminal-only LN. All 8 cells have exactly one LN. Depth counts hidden width-to-width blocks, not input/head projections. Residual contrast fixes depth,width,activation,LNmask; depth changes parameter count necessarily. Placement moves a single preLN between first and last branches, not adding head/stream LN.
Easy splits 05d021/06bcc2 have 1 turn; hard 016ff9/01793b have 2 turns. Each materialized split has 1024 train/2048 test points. These are four independent split instances, not 32 independent architecture contrasts or independent causal turn interventions.
Ten seeds0–9/cell. Numerical differences are CE(A)-CE(B) in nats; ratios are meanCE(A)/meanCE(B), not mean seed ratios. analyze.py computes paired seed differences, Student t intervals (df9,2.262), and wins; same numeric seeds do not guarantee identical random draws across different architectures. Intervals characterize training-seed uncertainty conditional on a split, not population split uncertainty, and are unadjusted for multiple comparisons. Full contrasts and measurement hashes/curves are preserved.

## Mechanisms and boundaries
Terminal preLN normalizes branch input only, not residual stream/head input. Supported dotted architecture overrides do not supply stream/head normalization, scalar-tail intervention or scale hooks. Existing per-seed curves allow learning trajectories, not feature/gradient instrumentation. No scale intervention is executed; endpoints cannot identify radius information, head conditioning or causal branch-vs-stream scale.
Competing accounts include optimization stability/tail failures, effective nonlinear capacity, altered feature geometry and residual stream accumulation. Their endpoint predictions overlap; discrimination requires actual supported feature-scale and gradient measurements or stream/head interventions. Matched signs establish architectural effects conditional on recipes, not a unique mechanism.
No export to Adam low rate, RMSprop, more-LN, Leaky .01, other widths, longer T, original full rankings or arbitrary turn thresholds. No cluster replacement/merge is justified yet. Report remains usable, independently reviewable unfinished science.
