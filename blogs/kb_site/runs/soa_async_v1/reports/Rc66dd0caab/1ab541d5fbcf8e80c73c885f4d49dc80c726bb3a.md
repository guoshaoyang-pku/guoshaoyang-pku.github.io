Depth comparisons preserve LN count but necessarily change parameter count. Depth excludes input/head projections.

Primary easy splits were one-turn `05d021/06bcc2`; hard splits were two-turn `016ff9/01793b`. Each has 1024 train and 2048 test points. Ten seeds0–9 were used per cell.

The initial report was published before training. Preregistered predictions: residual helps everywhere; d4 hurts easy and helps hard; terminal LN beats first LN everywhere. These predictions were substantially falsified.

Differences below are CE(A)−CE(B), in nats. Ratios divide mean CEs. `analyze.py` computes paired-seed differences, unadjusted Student-t 95% intervals and seed wins. These intervals describe training-seed uncertainty conditional on a split, not population split uncertainty. Numeric seed pairing does not guarantee identical random draws across architectures.

## Mechanisms, Accounting And Limits

Terminal preLN normalizes **branch input only**, not the residual stream or head input. No supported scale hooks, stream/head normalization intervention or scalar-tail intervention were identified or executed. Existing loss curves are not feature/gradient instrumentation. Endpoints cannot identify radius retention, head conditioning or branch-versus-stream scale.

Competing explanations include optimization stability and tail failures, effective nonlinear capacity, feature geometry and residual accumulation. Their endpoint predictions overlap. Discriminating them requires supported feature/gradient measurements or stream/head interventions.

There were **64 unique cells and640 seed evaluations**:48 ReLU cells on six independent splits and16 SiLU cells reusing four primary splits. Zero reported failures/exclusions. Eight subsequent cached manifest retrievals were reuse, not replications. All192 measurement artifacts passed SHA-256 verification. `results/*.json`, `analyze.py`, `contrasts.json`, preregistered variants and `report.md` preserve provenance and analysis.

An input inspection raised a missing-field KeyError; one experiment request rejected unsupported metadata before training. Two longer KB drafts failed the claim-length gate. These failures altered no measurements. Scoped claim **K1152** and a K1020 annotation were submitted; no cluster merge/deletion was performed.

The predictive boundary is conditional on **optimizer, activation, LN placement, skip status and depth**. Strong hard first-LN signs survived two prospective splits; terminal signs weakened or failed. Easy means remain tail-sensitive. No export is justified to low-rate AdamW, RMSprop, more-LN, Leaky .01, other widths, longer budgets, original full rankings or arbitrary turn thresholds.

A further distinguishable prediction, **not executed**, is the same frozen ReLU regime on allowed three-turn splits0717d7/0cfa0b: first-LN d4R < d2R < d2P < d4P. No terminal-d2 sign prediction is made. Test both independent split means and paired-seed uncertainty. Scale instrumentation and wider/deeper smooth-model transfer remain unresolved.
