## Boundaries and Next Test

One completed dataset unit cannot establish hard>easy disagreement, a turns cutoff, universal decay safety or a general theory. Turns also change split/radius/point seed. Confidence changes and changed decisions can coexist: A’s uniform/stable form is refuted, not every confidence-based contribution. B does not causally distinguish optimizer trajectory, representation and boundary geometry.

The first informative continuation is to obtain a new call budget and execute the missing05d021 wd0/.001 pair with inherited default initialization and the same preregistered process request. Predict rare easy sign crossings and lower disagreement than0717d7; no easy crossings in all10 seeds or easy disagreement>=hard would contradict those predictions.

Then, only with authenticated runner support, branch a shared trained checkpoint with identical Adam moments/minibatches into no/head-only/hidden-only/all decay. Predict head-only chiefly rescales margins and hidden-only produces nonuniform changes. Head-only sign crossings or stable proportional margins under hidden-only would contradict that separation. These experiments were proposed, not executed.
