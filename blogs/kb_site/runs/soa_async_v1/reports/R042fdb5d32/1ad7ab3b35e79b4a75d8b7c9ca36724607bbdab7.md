## Boundaries and Next Test
One completed dataset unit cannot establish hard>easy disagreement, a turns cutoff, universal decay safety, or a general theory. Turns also change split/radius/point seed; historical easy endpoints are not fresh process replications. Confidence changes and changed decisions can coexist. A's uniform/stable version is refuted, not every confidence-based contribution; B does not distinguish optimization trajectory, representation or boundary geometry causally.
First informative continuation: obtain approval for a new call budget and execute the missing05d021 wd0/.001 pair with inherited default init, same process request and existing preregistration; predict rare easy sign crossings and lower disagreement than0717d7. Falsifier: no easy crossings in all10seeds or easy disagreement>=hard. Then, only with authenticated runner support, branch a shared trained checkpoint with identical Adam moments/minibatches into no/head-only/hidden-only/all decay; predict head-only chiefly rescales margins,hidden-only produces nonuniform changes. Sign crossings under head-only or stable proportional margins under hidden-only would contradict that separation. These experiments are proposed, not executed.
