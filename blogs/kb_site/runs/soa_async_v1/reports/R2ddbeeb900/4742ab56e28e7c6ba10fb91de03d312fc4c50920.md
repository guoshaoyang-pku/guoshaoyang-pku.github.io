## Interpretation And Continuation
Measured phenomenon: .003 recipes beat focal AdamW .0003 at256, then their held-out CE deteriorates while AdamW improves by512/1024. Neither conservative underfitting nor aggressive overshoot is established without training CE or parameter/update motion.
Competing explanations include finite-data generalization, optimizer-state/noise effects and initialization-dependent trajectories. Declining train CE alongside rising held-out CE would support generalization deterioration; gradient/update measurements and controlled clipping/rate interventions would test instability; controlled initialization would test initialization sensitivity.
K1071 already conditions its evidence on width/optimizer/rate/budget/loss/init. These results refute discarding the exact short-budget rivals because of the old band, while the .001-versus-.003 and budget reversals preserve the danger of systematic maximum-Delta preference elsewhere.
No transfer is established to other widths/layers, residual GRUs, regularized losses, decay/betas, batches, alpha, original benchmark numerical losses or continuous rate thresholds.
