## Interpretation, Predictions and Boundaries
Alpha/table construction is not matched: different underlying Gaussian tables and sequence seeds vary jointly. The study cannot causally assign the reversal to alpha, a difficulty threshold or table identity alone. Only the same-table rate/FF interventions isolate their recipe changes.
Optimization trajectory, default initialization/architecture interaction, target fit and finite-split generalization remain competing explanations. CE near ln24 does not measure uniform logits, token conditioning, representation disruption, parameter overshoot or actual displacement. Train CE, logits, gradients and update norms remain unmeasured.
Unmeasured prediction: a third allowed exact V24/L16 alpha.8 table bg_3dfb54 with FF64/b32/T256 should retain RMS.003<Adagrad.0001 and reverse against Adagrad.0003; FF256 should shift RMS−Adagrad positively. Reliable opposite paired effects reject these transfers. No experiment was run on this table.
Mechanism tests require actual step0 evaluation and token-conditioning probes; train/test CE separates fitting from generalization; update norms and isolated initialization controls test optimization hypotheses. A shared underlying-table alpha intervention would be required for any causal alpha assertion and is not an allowed intervention here.
No transfer is established to altered heads,batch,V,L,budget,loss,initialization or Adam recipes; no universal optimizer,rate,alpha or displacement law is proposed.
