## Failures, Provenance and Uncertainty
Forty fresh replay/rate cells plus four bridge cells yielded 440 finite outcomes, cached=false. Six original d128 cells supplied 60 reused outcomes. The audited evidence comprises 50 distinct valid cells/500 seed outcomes across six distinct tables.
A bg_9a3dce audit request inherited unused betas metadata, causing three cache misses and 30 numerically identical rematerialized outcomes. Original-base retrieval returned cached=true; seed vectors and curve hashes match. These are not independent evidence. bg_87ba9c retrieval was cached=true.
The T0 request returned ten nonfinite/excluded outcomes. Audited `train.py` never evaluates initial CE: with no updates, its empty metric list produces Infinity. Direct torch import was sandbox-blocked. Genuine initial CE and CE change from initialization therefore remain unavailable; neither first-update CE nor ln24 was substituted.
An initial full-history snapshot exceeded the file-size limit and was replaced with focused frozen sources. Invalid base/name requests produced no measurements. No valid T256 cell failed or was excluded.
Intervals assume independent, approximately normal paired-seed differences. They omit multiplicity correction, test-window resampling and between-table uncertainty. Shared candidates and seeds are not independent table replications.

## Interpretation and Remaining Tests
Alpha and table construction were not matched: underlying Gaussian tables and sequence seeds vary jointly. No causal alpha effect follows. Same-table rate and FF interventions isolate their recipe changes, but not their mediating mechanisms.
Optimization trajectories, default initialization/architecture interactions, target fitting and finite-split generalization remain competing explanations. CE near ln24 does not establish uniform logits, representation disruption, overshoot or measured displacement.
Unmeasured prediction: exact FF64/b32/T256 recipes on alpha.8 bg_3dfb54 retain RMS.003<Adagrad.0001 but reverse against Adagrad.0003; FF256 shifts RMS−Adagrad positively. Opposite paired effects reject those transfers. This table was not tested.
Discriminating measurements require genuine step0 CE and token-conditioning probes, train/test CE, update norms and controlled initialization comparisons. Causal alpha inference requires explicitly matched underlying-table construction.
No transfer is established to altered heads, batch, V, L, budget, loss, initialization or Adam recipes. No universal rate, optimizer, alpha or displacement law is proposed.
