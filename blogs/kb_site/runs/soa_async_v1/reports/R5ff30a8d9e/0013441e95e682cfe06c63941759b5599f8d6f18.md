## Interpretation, Predictions and Boundaries
A scoped activation benefit coexists with a much larger added-block penalty. This refutes the tested "fifth residual block helps" transfer, not all possible deep XOR networks. LeakyReLU(.01) is piecewise linear and is not evidence of mathematical smoothness; no "smooth gradient composition" mechanism is inferred.
Competing explanations include activation-after-skip behavior,LN geometry,initial logit calibration,finite-budget optimization and generalization. Gradient norms/Jacobians,hidden representations,parameter displacement,train CE and step0 losses were not measured. Held-out curves start after update1; endpoint interventions cannot identify the cause.
Unmeasured prediction: on metadata-next XOR16 xorcls_19c4ba with these exact eight recipes, retain E<C and each matched Leaky<ReLU and added-block d4<d5 mean sign; E<A is tentative given the two crossing intervals. Opposite signs reject the respective transfers. A third split, longer-budget replay and instrumented train/gradient/logit traces distinguish split sensitivity,finite-budget recovery and calibration explanations. They were not run.
Conclusions do not transfer to another width,batch,optimizer/betas/decay,initialization,mask,dimension or training budget without evidence. No generic >=8-feature/Delta.05-.3 rule is used, and no universal activation/depth/LN scoring law follows.
