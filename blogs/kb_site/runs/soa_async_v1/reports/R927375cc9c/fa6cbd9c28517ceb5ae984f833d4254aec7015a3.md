## Mechanism and Boundaries
Zero-initialized, uncorrected RMSprop accumulation is a plausible startup explanation, not established causal mediation. Baselines show initialized-output calibration and substantial fitting gains; they cannot distinguish transient state from steady optimizer geometry, beta-dependent progress or target/LN interaction. Gradient moments, updates and full-training residuals were not measured.

A common startup-plus-late-Adam law does not explain four-cycle near-ties, weak withheld margins and target-dependent rate effects. Endpoint loss cannot establish jitter, overshoot, generalization or oscillatory-component acquisition. Prior temporal **test**-curve statistics remain separate observations.

Distinguishing tests remain open: paired accumulator warm start/state fork with unchanged weights and batches; preregistered early-step attenuation; paired late downshift with update norms and train/test residuals. Frequency-only targets and new same-expression splits would separate frequency, scale and split effects. None is identified by these endpoints.

Extrapolation excludes other architectures, normalization, batch/init scales, Adam betas/decay, continuous optimal rates, original focal splits and multivariate tasks.
