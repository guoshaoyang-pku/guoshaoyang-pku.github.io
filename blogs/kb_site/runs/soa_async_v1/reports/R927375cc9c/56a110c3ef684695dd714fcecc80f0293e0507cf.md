## Mechanisms, Counterexamples and Boundaries
RMSprop's zero-initialized uncorrected square accumulator is a plausible early amplification source; early fitting advantage is compatible with it,not evidence of causal mediation. No-update controls show calibrated initialized-output errors and large fitting gains,but cannot distinguish transient state from steady optimizer geometry,beta-dependent progress or target/LN interaction. No parameter updates,gradient moments or full-training residuals were measured.
A common startup-plus-late-Adam story does not reproduce four-cycle late near-ties,withheld weak margin,or target-dependent higher-rate effects. Endpoint losses do not establish jitter,overshoot,generalization or oscillatory-component acquisition. Prior report's temporal TEST-curve statistics remain separate observations,not training curves.
Distinguishing future test: paired state fork or calibrated accumulator warm start with unchanged weights/batches,then compare early update norms and train/test residuals; schedule intervention should predeclare whether attenuating early RMS amplification removes its lead. A paired late downshift tests reversible overshoot; train-better/test-worse residuals test generalization. Neither is available here. Frequency-only targets and same-expression new splits would separate frequency,scale and split effects.
Extrapolation excludes other widths/depths/LN,batches,initialization scales,Adam betas/decay,continuous rate optimum,unmeasured budgets,original focal point splits and multivariate tasks. No optimizer default or universal lower-rate rule is restored.
