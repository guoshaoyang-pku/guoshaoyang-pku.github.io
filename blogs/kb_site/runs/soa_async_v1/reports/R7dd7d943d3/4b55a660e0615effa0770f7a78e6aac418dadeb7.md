# Displacement Over-Selection: Progress, Precision and Failed Metrics

## Evidence and Definitions
Frozen inputs: 863 visible training records, 35 KB claims and 11,215 lab candidates. Only visible training answers and within-set allowed-lab measurements were used. Checkpoint refresh repinned `Jbb8de54d2489_research_e0001`; no new history records appeared.

Nominal displacement Δ is a progress proxy, not measured parameter movement: adaptive optimizers use `lr·T`; Adagrad uses `2·lr·√T`; SGD uses `lr·T·g/(1−m)` with assumed gradient scales. Architecture, target scale and optimizer state can invalidate those approximations. Late precision means a smaller rate beats a larger rate at the **same budget**, not merely that longer training worsens loss.

Training answers provide ordinal outcomes, not loss ratios. Lab results are ten-seed means and seed standard deviations, not confidence intervals. Pair counts share candidates and are dependent. No significance, seed-win frequency or causal overconfidence claim is inferred.

## Frozen Audit
Exact matching of dataset/set, model, loss, budget and all optimizer settings except learning rate found only **3 pairs across 3 sets**, all bigram LM. Higher rate won all three:
- Width-64/depth-2 GRU, AdamW, `.0003→.003`, 256 steps/batch32: loss ratio **0.92163**.
- Width-32/depth-3 transformer, RMSprop, `.0001→.0003`, 256/batch16: **0.98532**.
- Width-64/depth-1 GRU, plain SGD, `.001→.003`, 256/batch32: **0.99909**.

## Controlled Results
All comparisons below are within their named experiment sets. Batch16 is fixed; 256/2048 steps correspond to 4096/32768 sampled rows. Each experiment uses an allowed lab split, **not the original failure-question split**. Exact data cards, candidate IDs, resolved recipes and seed SDs are archived.

**Univariate, `sym_0c2620`, `exp:34aa6df219ff`.** Target `cos(2π·cos(2πx))−1`; width128/depth2, nonresidual LeakyReLU(.01), no LayerNorm, Adam(.9,.95), decay1e-5. At 2048 steps, rates `.00003/.0001/.0003/.003` give MSE **.52273/.35314/.27677/.01578**, SD **.00624/.02743/.01039/.01092**. Higher rates win **6/6** grid pairs. Rate `.003`, Δ=6.144, beats all three lower rates with ratios **.0302/.0447/.0570**. The preregistered overshoot-penalty prediction was falsified.

**Multivariate, `mvar_000e80`, `exp:46dafe43cf05`.** Three-input oscillatory target; width32/depth5 residual SiLU, LN `[F,F,T,F,T]`, Adam(.9,.999), decay1e-4. Rates `.0003/.003` give MSE **.77893±.20585 versus .69733±.06546** at256, then **.65152±.02806 versus .70482±.04937** at2048. Higher rate wins **1/2**; ratios **.8952 then 1.0818**. The prediction that high rate wins both budgets failed.

**Smooth tabular, `stabcls_021b60`, `exp:e3b7925d9c02`.** Eight features, 1024 train rows; width256/depth1 nonresidual ReLU+LN, Adam(.9,.999), decay1e-4. Rates `.00003/.0003/.001` give CE **.41170/.22133/.18960** at256 and **.22041/.14817/.16338** at2048. Higher rates win **3/3 short, 2/3 long**. The `.001/.0003` ratio reverses **.8566→1.1026**, but `.001` still beats `.00003`. Best tested long-budget Δ is **.6144**, not .05–.15. This is a grid minimum, not a continuous optimum.

**Bigram, `bg_013a4e`, `exp:88b5f9940c28`.** V32/context16, transformer width32/depth4/heads4/FF128, Adam(.9,.999), decay0, CE+L1(.01). Rates `.0003/.003` give CE **3.07942±.01672 versus 2.90496±.01020** at256, ratio **.9433**. At2048, `.0003` gives **2.96067±.01045**; `.003` returns mean/std **Infinity**, with `error=null`. This is a failed metric, not measured overconfidence or a known 10/10 seed failure. The failure boundary between rates is unmeasured.

**Spiral, `spiralcls_0516f4`, `exp:3dbc82fbb876`.** 1.5 turns; width256/depth2 residual SiLU, two LN, Adam(.9,.95). At decay1e-5, rates `.0001/.001` give CE **.38286±.02921 versus .002943±.002527** at256, then **1.4944e-5±6.6274e-6 versus 1.7412e-5±1.9878e-5** at2048. Ratios reverse **.00769→1.1651**. At2048/decay1e-4, the lower rate again wins by mean, ratio **1.0207**. Higher rate wins **1/1 short, 0/2 long**, but tiny losses and overlapping SDs preclude a confident general ceiling. Higher decay raises mean CE **8.209×/7.192×** at the two rates.

An exploratory AdamW spiral grid, `exp:b7eb3f31d314`, favored `.003` over `.0003` at256 but reached **0 versus 2.4267e-8** at2048. The ratio is undefined at this numerical floor. It is not persuasive late-precision evidence and is not merged with Adam.

**XOR rate-only, `xorcls_0dd7d2`, `exp:d82786f1e971`.** Sixteen features; width24/depth3 nonresidual GELU, LN `[T,F,T]`, AdamW(.9,.95), decay0. ## Claims and Falsifiers
Six conditional factual claims, each ≤700 characters, were submitted as **K1076–K1081**, plus annotations to K1072/K1073. Exact texts are in `proposed_claims.json`.

They record the univariate high-Δ counterexample; tabular and multivariate budget reversals; bigram nonfinite output; weak spiral mean reversal; and XOR’s separation of optimizer reversal from rate-only progress.

An exact same-split/recipe/seeds0–9 recomputation that fails the stated inequalities falsifies each measured claim. A finite recomputation of bigram `.003/2048`, or a demonstrated reporting artifact, falsifies its failure statement. New splits or seed cohorts can falsify predictive extensions, but are not contradictions of recorded measurements. No family-wide threshold is proposed.

## Explanations and Boundaries
Late refinement, optimizer-specific trajectories, regularization and numerical instability compete with overconfidence/distractor fitting. The latter requires train/test CE, logit norms and confidence on errors, none measured here. MSE reversals cannot be called logit overconfidence.

Unrun, distinguishable predictions: confident-error inflation should lower train CE while raising held-out confidence on errors; numerical instability should respond to smaller rates or clipping; an offset-memory explanation should alter Adagrad accumulators and ordering on architecture-matched offset interventions. These are tests to perform, not established mechanisms.

Thresholds remain grid-local: tabular Δ=.6144 beats .06144 and2.048; univariate6.144 can beat .2048; one bigram transformer/L1 recipe is finite at `.0003` and nonfinite at `.003` after2048 steps. No universal cutoff, width law, batch law or extrapolation from256 to2048 follows. Five selected failures cannot quantify population-wide solver bias.
