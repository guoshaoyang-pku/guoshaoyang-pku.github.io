## Uncertainty, alternatives and boundaries
Recipe generalization across targets is testable here; slower unnormalized smooth feature learning and distractor overfit are competing unmeasured explanations, not established mechanisms. A reversal falsifies the predicted recipe transfer. Train/test trajectories and factorial width/depth/LN tests could distinguish alternatives, but are not part of this single-contrast experiment. Seed covariance is unavailable, so aggregate SDs cannot yield paired significance. Two datasets are not a population-wide generalization result.
