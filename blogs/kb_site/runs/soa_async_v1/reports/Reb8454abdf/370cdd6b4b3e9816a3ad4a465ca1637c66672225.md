## Predictions before Measurement
On two allowed metadata-matched splits per family, terminalLN will beat one-earlyLN more consistently for deep plain SGD than Adam. Residuals should reduce the plain deep zeroLN deficit; no prediction that residual always wins terminalLN or MSE. Deep plain zeroLN should have lower input-dependent head feature scale and gradient RMS than d2; raw RMS alone may include bias and is insufficient. A fixed nonnormalizing scalar calibrated on training inputs to restore terminalLN head RMS should recover much of the SGD terminalLN advantage if scalar shrinkage is sufficient. If it restores RMS but not sensitivity, gradients, updates or loss, pure scalar shrinkage is rejected. LN centers and projects its Jacobian; normalization geometry can remain decisive. Adam may be less sensitive to scalar attenuation, but epsilon, head logits and nonlinear trajectories prevent exact scale invariance.
