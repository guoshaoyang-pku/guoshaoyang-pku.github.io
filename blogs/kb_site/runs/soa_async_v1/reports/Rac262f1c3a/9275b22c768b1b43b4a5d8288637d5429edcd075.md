## Mechanisms, Missing Measurements and Falsifiers
A width-dependent effective step is compatible with rate rescue, but rate changes also alter convergence and generalization. No measured mediator establishes that mechanism. Initial CE/logit variance, train CE, true cumulative parameter/output motion and endpoint displacement remain unavailable: accessible lab files have no training tensors or checkpoints, and sandbox PyTorch import fails with blocked ctypes.dlopen. No logit-scale calibration experiment was run. Test-CE curves cannot substitute for these measurements.
The proposed functional quantity must distinguish cumulative path length sum_t||theta_t-theta_(t-1)|| from endpoint displacement, and probe-logit path length sum_t RMS(z_t-z_(t-1)) from initial/final logit distance. A useful next test measures first-step output RMS before fitting a width scaling, predicts a held-out split's boundary, then tests it with an explicit batch schedule. Failure to collapse depth/alpha strata rejects a width-only mediator. Calibration predicts a boundary shift after matching initial logits while preserving optimizer/batches; equal calibration without a shift rejects that version.
Competing explanations remain default initialization/layer cloning, width-depth conditioning, optimizer trajectories, capacity/generalization and finite-table noise. Overfitting requires falling train CE with rising test CE; numerical instability requires finite-state/gradient/motion traces, not high endpoint CE. Uniform logits cannot be inferred from CE near ln24. Smaller-width preference at high Delta and wider preference at lower Delta are observations, not proof of overshoot.
Prospective unmeasured transfer: another allowed V24/L16 split at each alpha with this exact recipe should favor d128 over d64 at.05 in both depths and favor d64 at.3. At.1 predict wider wins at depth1; depth4 remains uncertain at alpha1.4. Reliable opposite paired signs falsify transfer. No precise continuous flip, universal initialization, SGD/Adagrad/RMSprop, CE+L1, head, FF, batch, budget or original-question numerical reconstruction follows.
