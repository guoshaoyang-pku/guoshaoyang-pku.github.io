## Finding
A narrowly gated bundle predicted all four new table means correctly: at Adam .0001/T256/b32/residual d3w256, SiLU010 beats Leaky(.01)011 on two sparse4 sign-product tables, while Leaky011 wins on two smooth4 tables. This reproduces K1151’s direction beyond its original splits without the sparse8/smooth16 dimension confound. It is not a general activation law: **same-mask010 SiLU wins on all four**. LN count, placement, residual and rate cannot be scored as independent bonuses.
Of 36 preregistered CE mean-sign predictions,32 succeeded and4 failed. Failures are the two smooth4 same-mask activation predictions and two sparse4 residual predictions. The neighboring-rate smooth bundle reversal occurs in the mean but is uncertain. No quantitatively faithful global law or solver-utility improvement is established; K1009/K1056/K1033 are not merged.

## Uncertainty, Competing Explanations and Boundaries
Bootstrap uses10,000 paired seed resamples,RNG913;intervals are descriptive,not multiplicity-corrected,population confidence or independent test-table uncertainty. Seeds pair by ID;different LN modules can change downstream initialization/random-stream alignment. Two tables/rule are independent allowed target/splits,not matched-rule interventions on identical points. Sparse coefficients differ but the sign-product boundary form is the same;active-count,target complexity,coefficients and split remain potential explanations. Dimension is held at4,not causally manipulated.
Supported interventions isolate activation,LN placement/count,residual,lr,and batch/exposure. Competing mechanisms include representation fitting,initialization/optimizer trajectories and confidence changes. No supported calibration or gradient-geometry measurement was available;known unsupported temperature/logit instrumentation was not reprobed. Endpoint CE/accuracy do not identify confidence,overfitting,gradient noise,feature multiplication or measured function motion.
Distinguishable UNRUN predictions:at the same Adam/b32 recipe,a third sparse4 sign-product table will have SiLU011−010 CE>0 and a third smooth4 table<0;forecast+.005/−.015. Failure rejects this narrow count-effect transfer. A fixed-point irrelevant-coordinate intervention could separate distractor dimension from rule;it requires allowed tensors and is not supplied. A held-out-training-fitted temperature intervention predicts differential CE reduction without accuracy change under a confidence account;train/test logits and calibration split are unavailable,so this account is unresolved. Gradient geometry requires supported traces and a preregistered intervention,not endpoint interpretation.
No transfer is claimed to GELU,other widths/depths,optimizers,decay,initialization,row counts,multi-term sparse rules,or a continuous Delta cutoff. No new depth experiment corrects underselection of depth,and no solver backtest measures improved utility. The harmful default is transferring pooled shallow/smooth/LN scores past their recipe gates;simply reversing those defaults is also unsupported.
