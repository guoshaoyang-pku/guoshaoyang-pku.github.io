## Competing Explanations, Target Limits and Next Tests
q_42b34c is smooth_additive,T256/b32 and goldC for the same B/C recipes; K1151 already preserves that focal recipe reversal. q_9e5a60 is piecewise_boundary,T1024/b16,RMSprop and heterogeneous architecture,goldB: it is not an Adam/T256 control. The new piecewise Adam test supports B, so piecewise labels alone do not force a rectifier win. Changing rules also changes targets and splits; no causal rule-only attribution follows.
Possible explanations include activation-dependent target approximation,LN placement/representation effects,initialization and optimizer trajectories,or prediction-confidence differences. Endpoint test CE does not distinguish them; no multiplicative-feature learning,undertraining,confidence or measured motion mechanism is claimed.
Preregistered predictions succeeded for sparse B/C and normalized activation contrasts, and piecewise had the smaller B/C margin. Zero-LN smooth losses and the existing q_42b34c reversal are retained counterexamples. A future matched b16 smooth-additive factorial tests whether the historical b32 reversal transfers; a b16/b32 crossover on identical allowed sparse/smooth targets tests batch dependence. Neither has been run here. A placement crossover(001/100 versus010 at fixed LN count) tests whether count alone predicts preference. Train CE,logit/margin trajectories and train-validation-fitted temperature interventions would distinguish fit from confidence accounts, but are not supplied by these endpoints.
Transfer limits: two sparse sign-product instances,one piecewise target,one optimizer/rate/batch/depth/width/residual recipe. No claims across multi-term interactions,AdamW/RMSprop,plain networks,GELU,widths,depths,batches or progress bands. No universal Delta=.1 cutoff follows from a single .0256 experiment.
