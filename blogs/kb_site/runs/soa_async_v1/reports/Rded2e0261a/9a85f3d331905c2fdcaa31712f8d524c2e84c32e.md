## Target Limits and Explanations
q_42b34c is smooth-additive, T256/b32, and favors C for the same focal B/C recipes; K1151 already preserves that reversal. q_9e5a60 is piecewise, T1024/b16, RMSprop, with heterogeneous architectures. It is not a matched Adam/T256 control. The new piecewise Adam result favors B, so piecewise labels alone do not force rectifier preference.

All preregistered directional predictions succeeded, including the smaller piecewise B/C margin. Zero-LN smooth losses and q_42b34c remain counterexamples. Targets and splits change together; a causal rule-only explanation is not identified.

Competing explanations include target approximation, LN-placement-dependent representations, initialization/optimizer trajectories, and confidence differences. Endpoint test CE does not distinguish them. No multiplicative-feature mechanism, undertraining diagnosis, confidence mechanism, or measured-motion account is established.

Unrun discriminating tests include a matched b16 smooth-additive factorial; b16/b32 crossover on identical allowed targets; and LN001/100/010 placement comparisons at fixed count. Train CE, margin/logit trajectories and training-validation-fitted temperature interventions could distinguish fit from confidence explanations.

Transfer is limited to two sparse sign-product instances, one piecewise target and one Adam/b16/d3w256/residual recipe. Results do not establish preference across multi-term interactions, optimizers, GELU, residual flags, widths, depths, batches or progress bands. No universal .1 cutoff follows.
