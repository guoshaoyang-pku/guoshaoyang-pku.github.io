# High-decay T512 wide-bigram recipe audit

## Finding and scope
The requested RMSprop recipe beats both tested Adagrad recipes by mean on all four allowed tables and beats AdamW .0003 on three of four. It is not reproducibly superior to every conservative adaptive rival: bg_22dc04 has a finite high-loss seed that reverses its mean against AdamW .0003.

Reducing decay or using T256/b32 predicts substantial deterioration on both tested alpha1.4 tables. Four heads does not reproduce K1102’s loss when high decay and T512/b16 are retained. These are recipe-local observations, not a regularization mechanism or universal optimizer rule.

## Trajectories and interpretation
Archived test-CE curves verify endpoints. At T512/b16 step128, high-decay R seeds remain around3.12 on bg_05e970 and3.04–3.08 on bg_22dc04. By step512, all first-table seeds reach2.47–2.52 and nine second-table seeds reach2.41–2.45; the retained tenth ends3.201166. T256/b32 R endpoints are heterogeneous: first-table range2.4775–3.1232, second2.4308–3.0773. Late CE reductions and seed-sensitive high-loss endpoints are measured; their mechanism remains unresolved.

Competing explanations include optimizer/penalty trajectory effects, default initialization and layer-cloning interactions, finite-window generalization, calibration and token-conditioning learning. No train CE, true step0 CE, logits/probes, update norms or parameter-motion measurement is available. Near-lnV CE does not establish uniform logits or overshoot. Discriminating tests require train/test trajectories, conditioning probes, initialization-controlled repetitions and matched underlying-table alpha manipulation.

Intervals assume independent, approximately normal paired-seed differences, using t9 critical value2.262157. They omit multiplicity correction, held-out resampling and between-table uncertainty. Heterogeneous seed outcomes make n10 inference particularly fragile on bg_22dc04 and T256. Ratios divide means rather than averaging seed ratios.

Four independently materialized table/split instances were tested, all previously used for different recipes; no new datasets were materialized. There are only two tables per alpha. Alpha and table/split vary jointly. No transfer is established to other widths, FF, V/L, budgets, batches, losses, pre-LN, initialization or untuned rival rates.

Unmeasured prediction for alpha1.4 bg_96d120/bg_e30aa2: low-decay heads2/T512/b16 R loses to matched G3/W3; high-decay heads4/T512/b16 R beats G3/W3; high-decay heads2/T256/b32 R loses to W3. Reliable opposite paired signs reject these transfers. No universal heads2 high-decay victory is predicted.
