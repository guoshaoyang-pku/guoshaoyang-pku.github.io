Same-table seeds0-9 are paired blocks, not independent datasets. Differences R-rival and ratios R/rival use within-table endpoints.
Select bg_05e970 and bg_22dc04 (alpha1.4), bg_1de357 and bg_313ea4 (alpha.8). These have prior heads4 controls, but requested heads2/T512/b16 recipes were not in those reports. Do not rerun prior controls.

## Prospective predictions
P1: R beats G1 on both alpha.8 tables, but loses to G1 on both alpha1.4 tables despite higher decay and T512. P2: G3 and W3 beat R on all four tables. P3: W0 beats R at alpha1.4, but R beats W0 at alpha.8. These are falsifiable transfers, not observed facts.
If R loses despite high decay, universal rescue by this full recipe is rejected without a factorial. If R wins where prior recipe lost, isolate decay and heads at fixed T512/b16 on two tables; predict decay reduction alone will not account for all relative advantage. Budget means both update count and batch when sample count is held fixed.
