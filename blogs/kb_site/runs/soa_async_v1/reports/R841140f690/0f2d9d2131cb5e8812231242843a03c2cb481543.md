## Mechanism tests and definitions
Data gradient means gradient of training CE alone, before adding wd*w, with batch and full-train norms distinguished. Log per-layer norms, parameter norms, signed logit-margin quantiles z_y-max(z_other), train/test CE and error, finite/failure counts. r is a raw gradient norm ratio, not Adam-preconditioned update dominance; cosine, moment state and cancellation can matter. At wd0, r=0 except undefined zero/zero norms. A causal test should apply decay after convergence, exclude head/hidden decay, or fit positive scalar temperature on training logits and evaluate held-out logits. Margin-only suppression predicts lower margins and recoverable CE with unchanged decisions; persistent changed errors/boundaries oppose that account. Calibration on held-out labels is not permitted as evidence of generalization.
