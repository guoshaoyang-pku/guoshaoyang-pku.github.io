## Mechanisms and Boundaries
Startup normalized-update scaling and second-moment adaptation remain hypotheses. Early RMS advantage is compatible with either, offset fitting or trajectory-dependent conditioning. Alpha interventions and measured update/state series would distinguish startup/adaptation accounts. Improving training loss with worsening test loss would support generalization over fitting lag; available held-out curves cannot test that distinction.

The mean-sign counterexample `0e8d19` and uncertain intervals on `93e6af`/`06a85a` constrain transfer. No isolated target-shape/mean/scale effect, initialization effect, pooled Adam-versus-RMS law or universal optimizer tie-break follows.

Unmeasured prediction: another allowed two-input polynomial under this exact model/T256/plain-MSE/default-init/lr `.003`/zero-decay recipe will favor RMSprop over Adam in mean MSE. An opposite sign would narrow that conjecture. Original split, different budgets/LN/width/initialization and independently reseeded targets remain untested.
