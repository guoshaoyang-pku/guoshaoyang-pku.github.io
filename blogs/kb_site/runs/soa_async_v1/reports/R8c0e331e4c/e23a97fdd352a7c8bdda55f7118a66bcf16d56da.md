q_e285d5 supports B on its observed target/split, not a general preference for conservative normalized momentum-SGD. New exact full-recipe controls give B wins **0/3 target means at T256 and 1/3 at T1024**. One mixed target reverses toward B; another mixed target and a polynomial retain C. ## Recipe Verification

Depth counts blocks between input projection and output head: B has six Linear layers total, C seven. Both are deep; “shallower generic B” obscures simultaneous width, residual, normalization-position, optimizer, beta and decay changes. Plain blocks apply GELU(Linear(LN(x))); residual blocks apply GELU(x+Linear(LN(x))).
Both q recipes use plain MSE, T1024/b32 and 32,768 sampled rows. Controls additionally use T256/b32 and 8,192 rows. Updates and exposure co-vary. Lower held-out mean MSE wins; reported ratios are B/C and differences B−C.

## Uncertainty and Scope
Seed-ID-paired t95 intervals for long-budget B−C are `05883d [−.222505,−.106119]`, `06497c [.021825,.113098]`, and `0f2210 [.002227,.003990]`. These use independent-seed and approximate t assumptions, df9, and no multiple-comparison adjustment. Raw differences and short-budget intervals are archived. Matching seed labels does not guarantee shared initialization or identical minibatch streams across architectures.
Three selected targets with one split each are not population sampling or independent split replication. Budget contrasts share datasets; 120 runs are not 120 target replications. Endpoint SD is not temporal noise. Raw losses should not be pooled across target variances.
The controls establish dataset-instance dependence while full architecture recipes remain fixed. They do not isolate function shape, offset, variance or split, and do not separately establish architecture dependence. q remains additional ordinal local evidence.

## Mechanisms and Further Tests
Endpoints do not establish precision, oscillatory fitting, overshoot or jitter. Representation, undertraining, trajectory dependence, generalization, decay and optimizer state remain competing explanations. Curve files are archived, but their schema was not verified as train/test/function-motion instrumentation; no curve-based mechanism claim is made.
A discriminating test crosses both architectures with both optimizers, records paired data streams, and logs full train/test MSE, function-update RMS, gradients and late temporal variance. Undertraining predicts worse low-rate train and test fit; generalization predicts better high-rate train but worse test fit; reversible jitter predicts same-state downshift improves both losses and reduces late motion. Forks must preserve weights, optimizer state and future batches.
Untested forecast: at T512/b32, C retains lower full-recipe mean MSE on `06497c` and `0f2210`; `05883d` remains uncertain. No further training was performed.
Only a measured local B preference is justified. No universal multivariate maximum-Delta or conservative-Delta rule, optimizer-only recommendation, or transfer to new architecture, batch or initialization follows.
