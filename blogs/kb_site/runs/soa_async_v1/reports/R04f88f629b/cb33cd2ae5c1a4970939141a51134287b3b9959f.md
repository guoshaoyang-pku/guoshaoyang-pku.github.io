# Negative-Utility Architecture Claims: Scope Repair and LN-versus-Residual Backtest

## Question and Measurement Sources

This investigation asks whether K1006's additive residual=one-LN scoring, K1018's XOR Delta~0.05 reversal, K1009's pooled high-Delta tabular reversal, and K1038's zero-LN SGD "near-frozen" wording encourage overrides outside measured regimes. It also validates current hypothesis q_1f04dc. No causal mechanism is required for the scoped comparison.

Frozen research inputs were read with load_history(), load_kb(), and load_lab(): 863 history records and 11,215 lab candidates, including visible prior controlled experiments. The supplied utility estimates (-.07 n33, -.06 n52, -.06 n22, -.03 n68) are retrieval/use diagnostics, not measured architecture effects. History exposes question, revealed answer, prediction, score, citations, explanation, comment, hypothesis, epoch, source and provenance. It does not expose all retrievals or counterfactual claim utilities. Actual citation counts are 16/3/1/1 for K1006/K1018/K1009/K1038. Their utility denominators therefore cannot be independently reproduced from these fields.

The lab source contains within-set candidate mean held-out losses and standard deviations across ten intended seeds. Means with partial seed failures must be flagged. Candidates marked excluded are not comparable. All comparisons here use only the allowed lab and same set_id; no cross-dataset loss ranking is performed.

## Definitions and Methods

Depth is the number of hidden MLP blocks between input projection and head, not all Linear layers. n_ln is the count of normalized hidden blocks. Residual means the benchmark block-level identity addition; it does not imply one LN-equivalent optimization unit.

Core-rule Delta is a proxy, not a measured parameter trajectory: Adam/AdamW/RMSprop lr*T; Adagrad 2*lr*sqrt(T); SGD lr*T*g/(1-m), with g=.03 for momentum MSE regression and .01 otherwise, following K1001/K1019. Width, actual training steps, sample budget, dataset, optimizer configuration, LN placement, activation, regularization and residual status remain potential effect modifiers.

The q_1f04dc lab comparison is exactly: plain MSE MLPs, nonresidual candidate A and residual candidate B, A.n_ln=B.n_ln+1, .5<=Delta_A/Delta_B<=2, same set_id, excluded=false. Low-Delta requires BOTH Delta<.1. High group means at least one Delta>=.1. Neither optimizer equality nor equal width/depth/activation is required by this reported comparison. The recorded effect is MSE_A/MSE_B, lower favors the one-extra-LN plain candidate.

History parsing uses natural-language model definitions and revealed ordinal gold, equal optimizer TYPE and learning rate, and low Delta. Ranked answers supply pair ordering; single-winner answers supply ordering only when one eligible member is the revealed winner. The focal question is excluded from its historical backtest. This reconstructs the reported denominator but does not imply matched optimizer hyperparameters or causal identification.

## q_1f04dc Results

The reported lab result reproduces: low Delta 23/31 across 25 sets and 25 datasets; median MSE ratio .906336. High Delta 5/13 across 12 sets/datasets; median 1.054061. Low-Delta univariate comparisons are 17/34 across 25 sets, median .988537. Thus the observation is specific to the multivariate pooled sample and should not be exported to univariate regression or higher Delta.

The historical claim also reproduces: excluding q_1f04dc, low Delta and equal optimizer type/lr yield 40/52 wins across 31 questions. The Adagrad subset is 12/17 across nine questions. Including the focal case adds one win, one pair and one question. These are pair counts, not independent trials, and they incorporate ordinal rather than numeric losses.

In q_1f04dc itself, A is plain depth2 width192 SiLU with two LN; B is residual depth1 width192 LeakyReLU with one LN. Both use Adagrad .001, 256 steps, 16,384 samples, weight decay .001, so Delta=.032. Gold A contradicts the solver's residual=one-LN tie followed by rectifier preference. This is a use failure of additive scoring, not proof that residual connections never help.

Sensitivity and stratification:
- Removing ALL failed-seed candidates changes the low group to 22/30, 24 sets, median .897740, and high to 3/10, median 1.075673. Exact 23/31 requires retaining nonexcluded partial-failure candidates.
- 4,096 samples: 22/30, median .908131; 8,192: 1/1, median .087072. There is no 16,384-sample eligible pooled lab pair reproducing the focal budget.
- Equal widths: 14/19, median .889144; plain narrower: 9/12, .924152; no plain-wider comparisons.
- Plain depth minus residual depth -1: 5/6, ratio .701240; 0: 7/8, .899030; +1: 4/9, 1.166100; +2: 2/2, .718782; +3: 5/6, .830532. Notably, the focal +1 depth gap is not favored in the pooled lab subset.
- Input dimensions 2/3/5/8: wins 3/3, 9/13, 8/11, 3/4.
- Equal optimizer type counts are AdamW 0/2, RMSprop 2/2, SGD 3/3. No eligible low pair matches the full optimizer dictionary, so no pair isolates LN/residual while controlling optimizer settings.
- Adagrad plain vs SGD residual: 1/4, median 1.118695, contrasting with some mixed adaptive-optimizer strata. A calibrated Delta ratio is not optimizer exchangeability.

Eight low-Delta losses are retained in pairs.json. Examples: mvar_086f22 plain five-block SiLU three-LN versus residual four-block LeakyReLU two-LN, equal width256 and AdamW lr .0001 but different decay, ratio 1.193; mvar_cf5f29 analogous contrast ratio 1.379; mvar_e57563 plain one-LN depth1 width96 GELU Adagrad versus residual zero-LN GELU SGD ratio 1.071. These counterexamples are not explained away.

## Visible Use Records: Factual Failure Versus Mis-Scope

K1006: q_1f04dc directly identifies false LN/residual equivalence; q_ca0dea reports a plain net beating its residual counterpart and confounded LN-placement failures at 2048 Adam steps. Spiral q_c64c90 and q_df3845 challenge additive normalization/residual scoring; q_fa7235 reports smooth activations outperforming rectifiers at shared Delta=.768. q_1e921c and q_bb084b show normalization/depth can lose to shallow or rectifier candidates. Their post-hoc mechanisms are not treated as established measurements.

K1018: q_09e52b is a successful in-scope use: sixteen features, shared Adam Delta=.0768, GELU/fewer-LN option wins. q_917f30 has only .5 ranking score, but its comment says the claim helped choose the winner; a prior controlled eight-feature XOR at Adam Delta=.0512 gave D-like CE .2196, A-like .2210, B-like .2244, contrary to a proposed fewer/delayed-LN ordering. The new sharp threshold is not warranted by that counterexample. q_a504f7 at Delta=.00768 and input_dim<8 concerns overcrediting residuals, not factual failure of the high-dimensional high-Delta condition.

K1009: its only visible citation, q_b0ae9d, is low-Delta tabular (.75 ranking score). The missed C/D contrast confounds width, GELU/SiLU, LN location and depth; the record explicitly says evidence was too sparse for a new rule. It does not falsify the high-Delta aggregate. The claim's pooled high-Delta default still needs condition-first wording by rule family, sample exposure and optimizer; "boundary already learned" and "XOR/spiral do NOT reverse" are not supported as universal facts.

K1038: its only visible citation, q_0e3752, says it helped remaining ordering; error was using SGD activation preference over regression depth in normalized residual stacks. That is not a zero-LN factual failure. The measured 21/60 deep zero-LN larger-Delta win count, even if correct under its original filter, measures relative held-out loss, not frozen parameters or absence of learning. Its own shallow-zero-LN figures are much higher. Full dynamics or loss-before-training would be required to call any network frozen.

Tentative prediction: plain one-LN beats residual zero-LN at Delta=.032; a losing result is a counterexample to universality, not a reason to change the comparison after seeing the result.

## Proposed Solver-Facing Repair

K1006 should lead with regime and comparable optimizer/budget, prefer measured normalization tendencies, and remove universal residual=one-LN scoring and forced activation tie-breaking. Add the multivariate exact-one-extra-LN pooled comparison with effect size and denominator, then state mixed-optimizer confounding, low budget concentration, +1-depth counterexamples, and univariate/high-Delta boundaries.

K1018 should lead with synthetic XOR, >=8 features, 1024 training rows, shared optimizer/calibrated Delta and tested budgets; call .05 an empirical bin boundary, not a sharp switch. Keep smooth/fewer-LN counts as measured tendencies, distinguish depth with/without residuals and large depth gaps, retain the .0512 counterexample, and do not override width/budget/optimizer conditions.

K1009 should separate activation, LN and depth evidence rather than combine them into a guaranteed shallow-wide-smooth winner. Stratify smooth_additive/piecewise/sparse_interaction and sample exposure. Do not say the boundary is known to be learned or that XOR never reverses. Refer to budget-specific evidence rather than universal Delta>=.1 precedence.

K1038 should lead with the actual comparison (SGD >=3x calibrated Delta, rival Delta<.1, same set), stratify zero-LN depth/residual/family/activation, and replace near-frozen with reduced reliability of Delta ranking. A held-out win rate cannot identify parameter motion. No automatic ranking below all adaptive or normalized rivals follows.

## Uncertainty, Boundaries and Reproducibility

Counts are descriptive. Multiple pairs share sets, models and targets; ten seeds do not make dataset effects independent. Tiny optimizer/depth strata cannot justify a new deterministic rule. No individual-seed covariance was available for paired standard errors, and no causal explanation was required or demonstrated.

backtest.py and pairs.json reproduce the lab scope and counterexamples; history_backtest.py and history_pairs.json reproduce historical comparisons; visible_records.json and claims_snapshot.json preserve retrieval/use source records; predictions.json preserves preregistered test directions. Scope reanalysis and controlled results will be added before the final publication. Unresolved questions include transfer to the focal 16,384-sample Adagrad budget, exact width/depth/activation matching, dataset-specific reversals, and whether displacement calibration should vary beyond current proxies.
