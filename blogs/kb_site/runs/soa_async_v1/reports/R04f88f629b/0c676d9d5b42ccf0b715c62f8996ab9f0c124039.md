# Condition-First Repair of Architecture Claims

## Question and Sources

Investigated negative-utility K1006 (-0.07 n33), K1018 (-0.06 n52), K1009 (-0.06 n22), and K1038 (-0.03 n68), distinguishing retrieval/use mis-scope from factual failure. Independently validated `q_1f04dc` without requiring a causal mechanism.

Frozen inputs: 863 history records and 11,215 base-lab candidates. A checkpoint refresh retained epoch-1 history and exposed current claim repairs plus experimental results. Base backtests exclude experiments; controlled results are reported separately.

The supplied utility estimates cannot be reconstructed from visible citations: K1006/K1018/K1009/K1038 appear in 16/3/1/1 cited records, respectively. Complete retrieval exposure and counterfactual utilities are unavailable. Negative utility therefore signals use risk, not necessarily false architecture facts.

Lab comparisons use held-out mean losses within one `set_id`. Excluded candidates are removed; nonexcluded partial-seed failures are retained for denominator reconstruction and separately tested. History supplies ordinal gold, not candidate loss magnitudes.

## Definitions and Methods

Depth counts hidden MLP blocks between projection and head. LN count refers to normalized hidden blocks. Residual addition is not defined as an LN-equivalent optimization unit.

Calibrated Δ follows K1001/K1019: Adam/AdamW/RMSprop `lr*T`; Adagrad `2*lr*sqrt(T)`; SGD `lr*T*g/(1-m)`, using `g=.03` for momentum MSE regression and `.01` otherwise. This is a proxy, not measured parameter motion.

The scoped lab comparison requires plain MSE, a nonresidual candidate with exactly one additional LN versus a residual rival, Δ ratio 0.5–2, and the same set. Both Δ must be below 0.1 for the low regime. Equal width, depth, activation, or optimizer configuration is not required by the reported pooled fact.

History backtesting requires equal optimizer type/lr and low Δ. Ranked gold supplies eligible pair order; single-winner gold supplies order only when the winner belongs to the pair. The focal question is excluded from the historical backtest.

## Validated LN-versus-Residual Fact

The reported lab result reproduces exactly:

| Comparison | Plain higher-LN wins | Sets | Median MSE ratio |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Multivariate, both Δ<0.1 | 23/31 | 25 | 0.906336 |
| Multivariate, at least one Δ≥0.1 | 5/13 | 12 | 1.054061 |
| Univariate, both Δ<0.1 | 17/34 | 25 | 0.988537 |

The historical result also reproduces: excluding `q_1f04dc`, 40/52 wins across 31 questions; Adagrad 12/17 across nine questions. Including the focal case gives 41/53 and Adagrad 13/18. Thus the focal exclusion explains the denominator difference.

In `q_1f04dc`, A is plain depth2 width192 SiLU with two LN; B is residual depth1 width192 LeakyReLU with one LN. Both use Adagrad .001, decay .001, 256 steps, batch64, 16,384 samples, Δ=.032. Gold A contradicts the solver’s residual=one-LN tie followed by a rectifier tie-break. This directly demonstrates scoring misuse, not universal residual inferiority.

## Stratification and Counterexamples

Low multivariate lab evidence is concentrated at 4,096 samples: 22/30 wins, ratio .908. The remaining 8,192-sample pair wins. No eligible pair directly matches the focal 16,384-sample budget.

Equal widths give 14/19 wins, ratio .889; plain-narrower comparisons 9/12, .924. No plain-wider comparisons exist. Input dimensions 2/3/5/8 give 3/3, 9/13, 8/11, and 3/4 wins.

Plain-minus-residual depth gaps −1/0/+1/+2/+3 give 5/6, 7/8, 4/9, 2/2, and 5/6 wins. Crucially, the focal +1-depth subset has median ratio 1.166, opposing a depth-specific universal preference.

Equal optimizer type/lr yields only 4/6 wins, ratio .913. No pair matches the full optimizer configuration. Equal-type AdamW gives 0/2, RMSprop 2/2, SGD 3/3. Mixed Adagrad-plain versus SGD-residual gives 1/4, ratio 1.119. These small, confounded strata do not establish optimizer-specific laws.

Removing every candidate with any failed seed changes low evidence to 22/30, ratio .898, and higher-Δ evidence to 3/10, ratio 1.076. Exact 23/31 requires retaining a nonexcluded partial-failure comparison.

A 10,000-resample set-cluster bootstrap gives descriptive 95% ranges of .567–.903 for win fraction and .721–.959 for median ratio. These are sampling-sensitivity intervals, not causal guarantees.

All eight low-Δ losses remain recorded. Examples include `mvar_086f22` and `mvar_cf5f29`: plain depth5 SiLU three-LN loses to residual depth4 LeakyReLU two-LN at equal width256, with ratios 1.193 and 1.379. Optimizer decay differs. These counterexamples are not explained away.

## Preregistered Controlled Tests

Predictions were saved before experiments. Only allowed lab datasets were used.

On `mvar_e57563`, fixed width96 depth1 GELU, Adagrad .001, decay0, 256 steps, batch16, Δ=.032, residual and LN were crossed:

| Architecture | Mean MSE | Seed SD |
|---|---:|---:|
| Plain, LN0 | .459344 | .007793 |
| Residual, LN0 | .451954 | .011002 |
| Plain, LN1 | .420702 | .010103 |
| Residual, LN1 | .419177 | .007300 |

The predicted plain-LN1 versus residual-LN0 preference holds: ratio .931, approximately 6.9% lower MSE. Equal-LN residual benefits are small. This supports one scoped comparison, not transfer to the focal target, depth, activation, or budget.

On sixteen-feature `xorcls_0dd7d2`, fixed width24 depth2 plain GELU AdamW, 256 steps and batch16, compare one versus two LN:

| Δ | One-LN CE | Two-LN CE |
|---|---:|---:|
| .0256 | .692235 | .692118 |
| .0512 | .687533 | .684777 |
| .0768 | .681533 | .676190 |

More-LN wins all three means. The preregistered high-Δ fewer-LN prediction fails. The high-Δ ratio is .992; differences are small relative to seed SDs, and paired significance cannot be computed without seed-level covariance. No tested condition shows a sharp switch.

Competing explanations include architecture/optimizer selection, target-specific effects, and imperfect Δ calibration. The fixed XOR threshold prediction was tested and failed here. SGD freezing requires trajectories or initial-to-final measurements, which these rankings do not provide.

## Repairs and Boundaries

Four short, condition-first revisions were submitted for K1006/K1018/K1009/K1038. They remove residual=one-LN scoring, an automatic XOR LN switch, pooled tabular precedence, and “near-frozen” SGD wording while retaining scoped counts, effects, and counterexamples.

Refresh exposed K1076, but targeting it was rejected by the proposal snapshot. Its validated definitions and limitations were instead incorporated into K1006 without creating a duplicate claim.

Solver precedence should first check family/data, loss, optimizer/calibrated Δ, actual steps/sample budget, and comparable architecture. Scoped preferences must not override stronger evidence outside these gates. A counterexample limits a rule; it need not establish the opposite universal rule.

Unresolved questions include exact replication of the focal 16,384-sample Adagrad configuration, matched LN-placement comparisons, transfer across targets, and SGD parameter trajectories. No causal theory is claimed.
