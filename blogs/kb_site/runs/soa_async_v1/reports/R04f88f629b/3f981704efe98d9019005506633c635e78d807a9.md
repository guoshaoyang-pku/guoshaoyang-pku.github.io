# Condition-First Repair of Architecture Claims

## Question, Sources and Definitions
Investigate whether negative-utility K1006 (-0.07 n33), K1018 (-0.06 n52), K1009 (-0.06 n22), and K1038 (-0.03 n68) encourage out-of-scope defaults; independently validate q_1f04dc's multivariate one-extra-LN/plain versus residual comparison. No causal mechanism is required.

Frozen inputs were read through load_history(), load_kb(), and load_lab(): 863 history records and 11,215 base measured candidates. refresh_observations() at the research checkpoint kept history at e0001, exposed already-repaired current claims and K1076, and exposed 34 experimental candidates, ten from this investigation. Base-corpus backtests explicitly use load_lab(experiments=False); controlled results are separate. Earlier and current claim snapshots preserve the difference. Reports were published before testing and after initial count validation.

The supplied utility numbers cannot be independently reconstructed: visible citations number 16, 3, 1, and 1, respectively, not the supplied retrieval denominators. History contains predictions, gold answers, citations and post-hoc comments but not complete retrieval exposure or counterfactual utilities. A negative utility estimate is therefore a use-risk diagnostic, not proof that an architecture fact is false. Comments are evidence of solver reasoning, not experimentally established mechanisms.

Lab losses are held-out mean MSE/CE across ten intended seeds. Excluded candidates are removed; partially failed but nonexcluded candidates are retained for the reported denominator and separately tested in sensitivity analysis. All loss ratios compare candidates within one set_id, never different datasets. Depth counts hidden MLP blocks between projection/head. n_ln counts their LayerNorms. A residual path is block identity addition, not one equivalent LN point.

Delta is the KB displacement PROXY: Adam/AdamW/RMSprop lr*T; Adagrad 2*lr*sqrt(T); SGD lr*T*g/(1-m), with g=.03 for momentum MSE regression and .01 otherwise (K1001/K1019). It is not measured parameter motion. Optimizer type equality does not imply equal momentum, decay, betas, or actual displacement. Width, depth, LN location, activation, target, steps, batch size and samples remain relevant.

Competing explanations remain selection/confounding of depth, activation and optimizer; target-dependent architecture effects; or a displacement proxy that does not index an identical training stage across models. Distinguishable predictions include sign reversal under fixed architecture near .05 versus continuous or target-specific differences, and freezing versus meaningful parameter motion under SGD. The former was tested here and did not show the predicted reversal; the latter needs trajectory or initial-to-final measurements and is not identified by held-out rankings.

Mechanistic fields explicitly distinguish untested explanations from measurements.

Condition-first solver precedence: check family/data, plain loss, optimizer/calibrated Delta, actual sample/step budget and comparable architecture first. Apply a scoped preference only after these gates. Do not let residual=one-LN, sharp .05 XOR, pooled tabular reversal or "near-frozen" SGD wording override evidence outside those gates. A counterexample limits the rule; it need not imply the opposite universal rule.

Residual uncertainty: pair dependence, small strata, selected benchmark targets, partial seed failures, proxy Delta and missing retrieval counterfactuals. One fixed multivariate dataset does not replicate q_1f04dc's exact target/depth/activation/budget; one narrow XOR dataset does not refute every high-dimensional smooth-activation advantage. Wider matched-budget tests, LN-placement controls, and SGD trajectories remain unresolved. No causal theory is claimed.
