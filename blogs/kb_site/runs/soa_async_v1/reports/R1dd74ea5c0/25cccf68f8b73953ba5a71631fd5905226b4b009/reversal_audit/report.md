## Explanations, Extrapolations and Tests
Late precision is consistent with tiny easy losses and low-rate easy budget reversals, but its universal high-rate budget prediction failed2/4. A small CE alone does not prove learned boundaries followed by noisy refinement; no gradients,margins,confidence or train-CE trajectories were measured.
Boundary complexity is consistent with the replicated ORIGINAL architecture reversal and hard-split depth5 residual benefit. However fixed-SiLU deep/shallow relative benefit is larger on easy splits, and depth3 residual loses everywhere. Coordinate scale, LN placement, width, activation, optimizer state, regularization and seed-tail behavior compete; endpoint losses do not identify a mechanism or causal turns effect.
Unmeasured predictions: on additional allowed1/2turn datasets at these exact recipes, predict the history-decay optimizer and original architecture sign patterns persist; an opposite mean ordering rejects transfer, not the recorded fact. For stronger late-precision tests, preregister T4096 and lower-rate controls: Adam should gain relative to RMS after both attain comparably small CE; persistent RMS superiority would reject that version. A complexity account predicts hard-data depth5 skip benefit persists across rates/budgets after controlling activation/width; disappearance or depth3 reversal limits it. Same-seed/scale-controlled turns interventions and training trajectories would distinguish explanations, but are not exposed by the allowed endpoint dataset interface.
