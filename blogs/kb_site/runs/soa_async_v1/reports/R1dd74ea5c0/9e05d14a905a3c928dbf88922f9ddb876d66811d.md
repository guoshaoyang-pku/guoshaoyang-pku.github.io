## Explanations and boundaries
Late precision predicts Adam's relative improvement as CE becomes small or budgets lengthen; boundary complexity predicts persistent deep-residual benefit on harder datasets even at comparable endpoint loss. Seed/scale, decay, beta and activation effects compete. Endpoints alone cannot establish boundary-learning trajectories or refinement mechanisms. No blanket optimizer or architecture switch follows from gold. Full controlled results and falsifiers remain pending.
