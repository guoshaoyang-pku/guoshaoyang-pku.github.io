## Explanations and Falsifiers
Late precision is consistent with tiny easy losses and the low-rate easy budget reversals. Its universal high-rate budget prediction nevertheless failed2/4. Endpoint CE does not demonstrate a boundary-learning phase followed by noisy refinement.

Boundary complexity is consistent with the original architecture reversal and hard-data depth5 residual benefit. But fixed-SiLU deep/shallow relative benefit is larger on easy data, and depth3 residual loses everywhere. Coordinate scale, normalization placement, activation, width, optimizer state, regularization and seed-tail behavior remain competing explanations.

Unmeasured predictions: additional allowed datasets at the exact historical recipes should preserve the optimizer and original architecture sign patterns. Opposite means would reject transfer. A stronger late-precision test would preregister T4096 and lower-rate controls, predicting Adam gains after both optimizers attain comparably small CE. A complexity account predicts hard-data depth5 skip benefit persists across rates and budgets after width/activation control. These tests remain unrun; causal turns tests require seed/scale-controlled data interventions unavailable here.
