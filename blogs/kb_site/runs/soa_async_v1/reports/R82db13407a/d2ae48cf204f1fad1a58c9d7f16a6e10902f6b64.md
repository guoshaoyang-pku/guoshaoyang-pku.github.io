## Bias and Countercondition
Controlled recipe preferences can prevent an adaptive-first mistake locally: P/Leaky supports SGD, both SiLU targets support RMSprop, and O/Leaky is uncertain. Applying these observed results retrospectively does **not** demonstrate systematic prospective bias reduction. An activation-conditioned policy needs fresh-target validation.

## Mechanisms, Predictions and Limits
Rate exposure, activation-dependent conditioning, LN/residual geometry, target offset/scale and gradient trajectories remain competing explanations. Matched controls identify endpoint recipe differences, not accumulated-progress causality. No gradient RMS, velocity, optimizer-state, update-norm or train-loss measurements establish the mechanism.

Future discriminators are paired train/test trajectories, measured gradients/updates, authorized centering/scaling interventions and independent seed/split cohorts. Before fresh-target experiments, predict RMSprop beats SGD .001 under this exact SiLU background atT256; Leaky may attenuate or reverse that sign. An opposite SiLU result refutes that extension. A bias-reduction audit must freeze the policy before observing new gold and retain all eligible contrasts and failures.

No transport is established toT1024, other architecture/LN/residual settings, batch16/64, nonzero decay, arbitrary target scales, other activations, classification, Adam or plain SGD. The original focal positive polynomial is not an allowed exact dataset here. Deliberate target selection does not support population win probabilities.
