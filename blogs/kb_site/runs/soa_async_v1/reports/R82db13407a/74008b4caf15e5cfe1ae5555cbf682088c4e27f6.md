## Interpretation, bias and countercondition
Controlled recipe preferences can prevent a blanket adaptive-first choice locally: on P/Leaky, SGD is the supported preference; on both SiLU targets, RMSprop is supported; on O/Leaky the mean SGD preference is uncertain. Choosing by these already observed cells is retrospective, not evidence of prospective systematic bias reduction. Neither unconditional adaptive-first nor unconditional boundary-SGD-first captures all four mean signs. An activation-conditioned rule fits this grid but needs fresh-target validation; no population bias rate or general optimizer optimum is established.

q_235706 E<B is an out-of-scope near-parity momentum-SGD/Adam reversal: SGD .0001,m.9,wd.001,plain d3w24 GELU,LN[TFT] versus Adam .00003,wd1e-5,residual d3w24 GELU,LN[FTT],T512,batch64,proxy~1.111. Its RMSprop D uses .003,lr*T1.536 and a different Leaky architecture. It cautions against adaptive-family priors but validates neither this SGD/RMSprop boundary nor an SGD/Adam rule. No plain/momentum inference follows.

Rate exposure, activation-dependent conditioning, LN/residual geometry, target offset/scale and gradient trajectory remain competing explanations. The local factorial controls identify recipe-dependent endpoint differences, not accumulated-progress causality. Endpoint MSE and saved held-out curves do not measure gradient RMS, velocity, optimizer state, update norms or train loss. Generalization versus fitting lag is unresolved.

Distinguishable future tests: measured train/test trajectories distinguish poor fitting from better train/worse test generalization; layerwise gradients/updates test the lr/3 calibration; authorized paired centering/scaling tests offset dependence; fresh expressions/splits and independent seed cohorts test ranking reliability. Before such measurements, predict on a fresh allowed target with this exact background atT256: RMSprop beats SGD .001 under SiLU, while Leaky can attenuate or reverse that sign. A SiLU opposite sign refutes that extension. A prospective ranking audit must freeze this condition-first policy before new question gold and compare pairwise errors against adaptive-first, with all eligible contrasts and failures retained.
