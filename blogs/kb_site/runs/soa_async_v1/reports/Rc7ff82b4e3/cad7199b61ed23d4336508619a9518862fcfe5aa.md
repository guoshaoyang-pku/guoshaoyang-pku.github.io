For the exact q_90a22b model and optimizer assignments, plain SGD wins six of nine completed allowed target/split blocks; Adam wins three. All ten seed pairs agree with their block's mean order. The user's preregistered prediction, SGD lower mean MSE in >=7/10 new blocks, is **not established**: only nine valid comparisons completed, with six wins. ## Mechanisms and Falsifiable Intervention
Offset fitting, feature-gradient scale, optimizer-specific dynamics, target complexity and finite-budget fitting remain competing explanations. No authenticated target-mean, initial-feature/gradient, parameter-motion or optimizer-state diagnostics were supplied. Target expressions contain different constants, but constants alone are not measured target means. Endpoint effects identify no unique cause.
Separate preregistered mean-centering intervention: subtract training-label mean from both train and test labels (or restore it to predictions), hold all model/rate/decay/budget assignments fixed, and never use test mean. Offset-mediated advantage predicts the centered margin shifts positive, reducing SGD advantage; persistence or enlargement would count against that directional prediction. The exposed experiment interface allows model/optimizer/loss/budget overrides, not an authenticated target-centering transform. Therefore centering was not run; adding a nominal unsupported key or modifying allowed datasets would not authenticate it.
No broad optimizer pooling, evaluation-answer access, assumed useful-gradient-motion explanation, or short-claim operations were used.
