## Fixed Recipe
Two-input, nonresidual MLP: width256/depth3, LeakyReLU(.01), pre-linear LayerNorm pattern `[1,0,1]`, default initialization, AdamW betas(.9,.999), wd1e-5, batch16. Primary objective is **plain MSE**, comparing lr3e-5 with .001.

Both datasets have 256 train/256 test rows and domain `[0,1]`. Targets are `x0**3+x0*x1+x1-2` on `mvar_5a829e` and `x0*x1**2+x1**3+x0` on `mvar_93e6af`. Target and split change jointly between datasets.

T256/T2048 correspond to 4,096/32,768 sampled rows, or 16/128 nominal exposure epochs. Update count and exposure therefore co-vary. Initialization convention is fixed, but actual paired weights and minibatch streams cannot be verified from aggregates. Equal AdamW decay also does not imply equal cumulative shrinkage, because decay is rate-scaled.

## Tool Limitations

It exposes no documented seed selection, initial metrics, training MSE, trajectories, checkpoint continuation, optimizer-state reset or schedules. Resource/template listings are empty.

Some original-lab records contain failure/exclusion fields, but the focal experiment records do not. Their absence is not evidence that every seed remained stable. Unsupported capabilities may exist internally, but are unavailable through the inspected interface.

No new training was run. Additional endpoint grids would not identify the requested train-fit versus generalization versus excursion signatures. Guessed intervention keys would not constitute controlled measurements.

## Preregistered Discriminators
1. **Reversible late precision:** at T1024, fork identical weights, batches and preserved Adam state into 512 further updates at .001 versus3e-5. Log full-train and fixed-test MSE every32 steps. Predict downshift reduces both residuals in stable seeds. Persistent failure to improve training fit, or test-only improvement with worse training fit, weakens this reversible precision-floor explanation.

2. **Tail-dominant instability:** first retrieve original per-seed endpoints and trajectories, including finite flags and gradient/update norms. Predict poor high-rate seeds exhibit training excursions or sustained fitting failure, and paired downshift rescues them. Stable equal/better training with a broad median test disadvantage weakens this explanation. Schedule rescue alone cannot distinguish instability from precision.

3. **Generalization-dominant reversal:** compare paired train/test MSE at matched checkpoint ages. Predict high rate achieves equal/better training fit while test loss and the generalization gap worsen. Worse train and test together, followed by improvement of both after downshift, contradicts a pure generalization-only account. Mixed mechanisms remain possible.

4. **Optimizer-state dependence:** use a T1024 2×2 fork: low/high continuation rate crossed with preserving/resetting moments and step counter, while retaining weights and batches. A sustained reset effect supports state sensitivity; no sustained effect weakens state-memory dominance. Reset is not a pure beta2 intervention.

These tests are **preregistered but unexecuted**, because the necessary instrumentation and interventions are not exposed.

## Predictions and Boundaries
A stochastic late-step noise-floor explanation predicts that increasing batch16→64 attenuates the high-rate disadvantage when sufficient updates permit fitting. Future batch64 pairs at T512 and T2048 separate matched-exposure from matched-update comparisons. Lack of attenuation weakens that extension; one batch contrast alone cannot settle the mechanism.

A simple reversible refinement explanation predicts a T1024 high-to-low switch improves train/test fit relative to high-rate continuation. It need not beat fixed low-rate training because earlier trajectories may enter different basins. Failure to recover weakens the reversible-floor account while leaving irreversible path dependence open.

Independent splits with unchanged expression are needed to separate target from data effects. Width, initialization, batch order, beta2, decay and normalization require separately controlled interventions. No direction of width transfer is established here.

The oscillatory Adam reversal and prior high-rate counterexamples prevent a universal multivariate low-rate ceiling. Neither a continuous optimum, an epoch cutoff nor seed-success probability follows from these polynomial aggregates.

**Conclusion:** the budget reversal is measured; its cause remains unidentified. Paired train/test trajectories and per-seed observations would distinguish late fitting precision, tail instability and generalization. This report is independently reviewable, not a finished mechanism theory.
