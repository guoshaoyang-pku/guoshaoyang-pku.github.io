## Definitions and Fixed Recipe
Primary outcome is endpoint held-out test MSE, reported as ten-seed mean and seed SD. SD is not a confidence interval. “Late precision” as an observed label means lower rate wins at equal long budget; a reversible optimization precision floor is a separate, unproven causal explanation. Instability includes excursions or numerical failures, neither established by a large finite endpoint. Generalization concerns train-versus-held-out performance, not test loss alone.

The fixed model is a nonresidual MLP: input Linear2→256 plus LeakyReLU(.01), three width256 hidden blocks with pre-linear LayerNorm pattern[1,0,1], then scalar head; five Linear layers total. Initialization uses PyTorch Linear defaults. AdamW has betas(.9,.999), wd1e-5; batch16; primary objective plain MSE(lambda0). Rate is3e-5 versus.001. Initialization convention is fixed, but pairing of actual initial weights and minibatch streams cannot be verified from aggregates.

Both allowed datasets have256 train/256 test rows and domain[0,1]. mvar_5a829e targets x0**3+x0*x1+x1-2; instance seed100081164975 and documented uniform point seed100081165975. mvar_93e6af targets x0*x1**2+x1**3+x0; instance seed100035922047; the prior report describes uniform sampling, but this experiment data card does not expose a point seed. Target, coefficients and split change jointly between datasets.

T256/T2048 give4096/32768 sampled rows, or16/128 nominal exposure epochs on256 training rows. These are not guaranteed complete passes. T and exposure co-vary at fixed batch; no separate update-count or exposure effect is identified. lr*T=.06144/2.048 atT2048 is nominal, not measured parameter displacement. Equal AdamW wd does not equal equal cumulative shrinkage because decay is rate-scaled; equal betas also do not imply equal moment trajectories.

## Capability Audit and Experiment Decision
The exposed run_experiment schema offers base candidate, optional other allowed dataset, dotted-key variants and why; its documented output is ten-seed selection-metric mean/SD. It supports fresh configuration comparisons, but contains no documented seed selection, checkpoint export, continuation, optimizer-state reset, schedule, clipping control, or trajectory-return option. Those capabilities are unavailable through the inspected interface, not proven absent from the underlying trainer.

Observed focal rows expose model/data/loss/optimizer/budget plus test_mse mean/std. They do not expose individual seed endpoints, initial/final training MSE, initial test MSE, trajectories, actual updates, Adam moments, per-seed finite flags or exclusions. Some original-lab rows have failed_seeds/excluded fields, but all ten focal experiment rows lack them. read_record provides question/answer context, not these measurements; resource/template listings are empty.

No new training was run and no completed identical cell was repeated. Extra fresh endpoint grids are feasible, but do not identify the requested train-fit versus generalization versus excursion signatures. Guessed schedule/reset keys would not be a valid intervention. The preregistration below is therefore explicitly unexecuted; Python aggregate audits are analysis, not new training observations.

## Preregistered Discriminators
1. Reversible late precision: on each existing allowed polynomial split, atT1024 fork identical weights, batches and preserved Adam state into512 more updates at.001 versus3e-5; log full-train/fixed-test MSE every32 steps. Predict downshift reduces both residuals in otherwise stable seeds. Persistent non-improvement in train MSE, or test-only improvement with worse train fit, weakens this reversible precision-floor account. Schedule rescue alone is not diagnostic of precision rather than instability.

2. Tail-dominant instability: obtain original ten seed endpoints and trajectories before choosing poor seeds; log finite flags and gradient/update norms. Predict high-loss seeds show training excursions or sustained fit failure, and paired downshift rescues them. A broad median test disadvantage with stable equal/better high-rate training weakens this account. Predefine dropping only the single largest high-rate endpoint as a sensitivity check: a remaining median disadvantage rejects a sole-one-outlier explanation, not all instability explanations. No endpoint threshold is retrofitted as a “failure.”

3. Generalization-dominant reversal: compare paired train and test MSE at the same checkpoint ages. Predict high rate attains equal/better train fit while test loss and the generalization gap worsen. Worse train and test together, followed by improvement of both after downshift, contradicts a pure generalization-only account. Mixed signatures permit coexistence; absence of injected noise does not guarantee absence of finite-sample generalization error.

4. Optimizer-state dependence: atT1024 use a2×2 fork, continuation rate low/high crossed with preserving/resetting moments and step counter; preserve weights and batches. Predict a state-dominant explanation produces a sustained reset-versus-preserve difference beyond transient adaptation. No sustained difference weakens that explanation. Reset is not a pure beta2 change, and fresh low-rate endpoints are not state-reset continuations.

## Falsifiable Next-Setting Predictions and Boundaries
These are hypotheses for future supported instrumentation, not measured transfers: a stochastic late-step noise-floor account predicts that increasing batch16→64 attenuates the high/low residual disadvantage when enough updates permit fitting. Test new batch64 rate pairs atT512 (32768 exposures) andT2048 (131072 exposures); update-age and exposure-only accounts predict invariance along different matching axes. No attenuation weakens the batch-noise extension. Batch simultaneously alters sampling noise, exposure and optimizer history, so a single batch contrast does not settle the mechanism.

A progress-then-refinement account predicts a high-to-low switch atT1024 can improve final train/test fit relative to the high continuation, but it need not beat a fixed low trajectory because basins and state differ. Failure to recover despite adequate continuation falsifies the simple reversible-floor version; it leaves irreversible path dependence open. Intermediate rates orT1024 rankings alone could bracket a reversal but would not establish its cause.

Data and target effects require independent allowed splits with unchanged expression; the two observed datasets do not isolate either. Width effects require width128/256/512 rate pairs with the same target, split, normalization and initialization policy; no direction is justified here. Actual initialization versus minibatch-order effects need separate seed controls. beta2, decay, LN, depth or activation interventions must remain individually scoped rather than bundled into a “low-rate mechanism.”

No universal multivariate low-rate ceiling, continuous optimum, width law, epoch cutoff or seed success probability follows. The first dataset supports a budget reversal; the second supports a long-budget mean ordering with marked heterogeneity. Neither distinguishes late fit, outlier instability and generalization. Original question splits and independently chosen seeds remain outside the controlled evidence.
