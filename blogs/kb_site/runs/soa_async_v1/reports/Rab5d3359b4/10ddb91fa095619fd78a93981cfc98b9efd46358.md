# Exact Low-Progress XOR8 Adagrad Decay: Transfer Audit

## Finding
For the exact tested q_f03f18-style recipe, wd0 beats wd1e-4 on both preregistered allowed splits, but only by **2.06 and 2.58 millionths of a CE nat**. Each split has 9/10 paired seed wins, one reversal and no failures. This supports a narrow mean-order tie-break, not a practically substantial advantage or a universal low-progress decay law. The mechanism remains unresolved.

## Mechanisms, Predictions and Boundaries
Coupled decay perturbs task gradients and accumulated squared gradients, but that plausible optimization account is not identified. Generalization, numerical sensitivity and task-fitting perturbation remain competing explanations. Update norms, accumulators, training CE and actual displacement were not collected; endpoint CE cannot establish freezing or overfitting.

Unmeasured prediction: another metadata-selected allowed XOR8 instance at this exact recipe should favor wd0 in mean, potentially with seed reversals; an opposite mean is a transfer counterexample. A genuine trajectory effect should persist under controlled higher precision; a rounding-dominated effect need not. An accumulator account requires controlled optimizer-state measurements or interventions. Overfitting requires decreasing training CE with increasing test CE. These tests remain unrun.

Do not extrapolate to lr.001/.003 XOR16, other architectures/LN masks, decay magnitudes, budgets, batches, initialization or tuned optima. Two fixed metadata-selected instances do not establish universal transfer. K1002’s proxy/rate observations and K1005’s warning against universal decay tie-breaks remain intact.
