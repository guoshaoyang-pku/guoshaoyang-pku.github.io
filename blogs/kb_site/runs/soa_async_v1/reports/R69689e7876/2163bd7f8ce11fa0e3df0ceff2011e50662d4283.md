## Interpretation, Alternatives and Next Tests
The two focal recipe pairs can exhibit preregistered target/split-dependent numerical reversals under identical executable recipes. The smaller oscillatory Adagrad margin should not be presented as comparable to its much larger additive margin. LN count alone cannot account for these conditional orders; neither can a universal residual override.
Baseline fitting, feature propagation, activation/normalization interactions, target complexity and split-specific approximation remain competing explanations. No centering mechanism was measured. Successful directions do not identify which target feature caused a reversal, and a higher oscillation count is not itself an established predictor.
Distinguishable next tests: documented train-only centering with identical points would test the baseline hypothesis; train-only scaling would separate scale effects; multiple new point seeds per fixed expression would test split sensitivity; crossed residual/LN/activation/depth interventions would identify bundle components. These are proposed tests, not measured results or preregistered directions for unavailable interventions.
Boundary: evidence applies to these fixed targets, CPU splits, optimizer configurations and short budgets only. No guarantee for centered targets, other widths/depths, longer training, other optimizers or unseen target distributions. The failed setting needs diagnosis. An independent preregistered target panel is needed before calling this a usable predictive rule.
