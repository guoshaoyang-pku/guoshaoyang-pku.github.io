## Interpretation and Competing Explanations
Preregistered numerical reversals occurred under identical executable recipes across completed target settings. The oscillatory Adagrad difference is substantially smaller than the additive difference. Neither LN count nor a universal residual override explains the conditional orders.

Baseline fitting, feature propagation, activation/normalization interactions, complexity and split-specific approximation remain competing explanations. Centering causality was not measured. Successful directions do not identify the responsible target feature.

Distinguishing tests require documented train-only centering/scaling on identical points, multiple splits per expression, and crossed architecture interventions. These remain proposed tests. Evidence is limited to the measured targets, fixed splits, focal optimizers and short budgets; no pooled scoring law is established.
