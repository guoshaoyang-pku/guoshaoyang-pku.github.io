# Adagrad Depth Versus FF: Matched Replication of q_5c5e48
## Finding
At the exact low-rate recipe, depth3 beats depth2 at fixed FF128 and FF256 on both allowed splits: four negative mean contrasts, each with a negative pointwise paired 95% interval and 10/10 seed wins. However, depth3/FF128 versus depth2/FF256 wins only one of two split means; both intervals include zero. Thus the pure-depth result replicates, but “depth outweighs smaller FF” does not transfer reliably. Mechanism remains unresolved.

Depth advantages grow substantially after step1, compatible with different early optimization rather than an endpoint advantage already established before training; this is not causal mediation. Cloned-layer representations, optimization/conditioning, capacity and finite-table effects remain alternatives. No initial logits, train CE, gradients, parameter motion or checkpoints were measured; no overfitting, overshoot or uniform-logit mechanism is established by CE alone.
A distinguishable next test records step0 and matched-batch train/test curves while retaining cloned initialization. Independently reinitializing layers would test dependence on cloning, but is not the present recipe.
