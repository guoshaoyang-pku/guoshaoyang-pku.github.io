## Methods and uncertainty
Within-set contrasts only, endpoint CE in nats. Retain seed IDs, failures, exclusions, caches and measurement hashes. Paired-seed t intervals will describe initialization/training-seed variability, not independent dataset/test-sampling uncertainty; shared seed does not guarantee shared batches across FF shapes. Inspect available curves for step0 CE before claiming an initialization advantage.
## Mechanisms and boundaries
Cloning/initial representation, early optimization and capacity are competing explanations; endpoint ordering alone identifies none. The factorial separates depth from FF at fixed heads/width but does not equate model weights across FF. Only two independent allowed splits at one alpha, budget and optimizer recipe are planned. Prior AdamW width reversals and depth-gap results are separate evidence and forbid universal shape sorting.
