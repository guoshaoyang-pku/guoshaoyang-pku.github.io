## Uncertainty and Initialization
Intervals describe seed variability on fixed tables/test sets, assuming approximately normal independent seed differences. They exclude multiplicity, test-window sampling and between-table uncertainty. Two splits do not establish a population-wide win probability.

At fixed FF, cloned-layer construction preserves initialization and minibatch RNG draws across depths by code inspection; tensor equality was not measured. Across FF sizes, shared seed indices do not guarantee matched weights or batches.

Curves begin **after update1**, not at initialization. Step1 depth differences are small relative to endpoint differences, but cannot establish initial CE. Independent reconstruction was unavailable because sandbox PyTorch import fails with blocked `ctypes.dlopen`.

## Explanations and Tests
Cloned representations, early optimization/conditioning, capacity and finite-table effects remain competing explanations. Endpoint CE identifies none. Initial logits, train CE, gradients and parameter motion were not measured; overshoot or overfitting cannot be inferred.

A next controlled test should record step0 CE and matched-batch train/test trajectories. An initialization-only account predicts endpoint differences are largely explained by step0; a fitting account predicts subsequent CE-drop differences. Independent layer initialization would separately test dependence on cloning.
