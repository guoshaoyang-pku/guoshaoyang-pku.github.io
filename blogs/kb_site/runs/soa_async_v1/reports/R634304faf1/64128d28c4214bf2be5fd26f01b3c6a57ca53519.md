# K1075 audit (initial checkpoint)
Only frozen training history and allowed lab data are used. K1075 states a sharp architecture switch at nominal Adam displacement Delta=lr*T=.02, but q_8417bc contradicts its equal-width rule: Adam .0001*256=.0256, alpha=.8, GRU64x2 beats both transformers including d64. A visible alpha=1.4 history countercondition at the same nominal Delta favors transformers. Existing .05-.1 transformer wins (14/14) come from one set, not 14 independent datasets. No matched .02-.05 lab evidence is established yet.
Definitions: Delta is nominal lr*T for Adam, not measured parameter displacement. CE ratios will be GRU/transformer (less than one favors GRU). Shared hidden width is not parameter-count matching. Alpha scales random transition logits; it is not a measured difficulty score.
Preregistered tests: compare two-layer non-residual GRU and two-layer post-LN transformer at width64, Adam betas .9/.999, weight decay0, CE, batch16, V48/L12, Delta .01,.02,.0256,.04,.05,.064, T256 and1024 where feasible; lr=Delta/T. Use allowed datasets at alpha .8 and1.4; distinct table/sequence seeds confound alpha, so multiple datasets are needed. Shape probes vary width32/64/128 and depth1/2 independently. Initialization controls depend on supported lab overrides.
Predictions before experiments: default-initialized GRU may win low Delta for alpha .8 because its initial logits are better calibrated; transformer gains should grow with Delta and alpha. If calibration explains the exception, equalized/zero output initialization should reduce GRU advantage. If transition learning dominates, alpha-dependent ranking should survive calibration control. Equal Delta across T need not imply equal CE. A universal sharp .02 switch predicts transformer wins all equal-width cells at .02 and above and is falsified by any reproducible GRU advantage.
Uncertainty: ten model seeds per lab candidate do not represent ten datasets. Paired seed covariance and initial CE/logit measurements may not be returned; do not infer them. Audit .1-.5 observational pairs by width and depth before retaining 'any d_model'. Results and scoped claim revision pending.
