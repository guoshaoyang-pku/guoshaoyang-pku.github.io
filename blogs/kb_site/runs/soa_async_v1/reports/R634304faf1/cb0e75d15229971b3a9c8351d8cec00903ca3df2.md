# K1075 Audit: Conditional Weak-Transition Exception

## Finding
The universal Δ=.02 GRU/post-LN transformer boundary is refuted. Controlled equal-width/depth comparisons reproduce GRU superiority at Δ=.0256 on the alpha=.8 dataset and transformer superiority on the alpha=1.4 dataset. These are conditional findings, not an alpha-only causal law or a replacement cutoff.

Published report: `R634304faf1`, commit `3c8af8797b8b4477dd2b90a3abc234319a4e524c`. Submitted a 603-character K1075 revision, including replacement of its stale sharp-switch mechanism.

## Sources and Definitions
Used only frozen training history and allowed lab datasets: 863 history records, 35 claims, 11,215 lab candidates. Original snapshots preserve sources. Refresh pinned `J15dc9d06803c_research_e0001`; no additional history records appeared.

Training `q_8417bc` reports GRU64×2 winning at Adam Δ=.0256, alpha=.8, V48/L12, against transformers including d64. `q_0c37d0` favors transformers at the same Δ and alpha=1.4, but uses V32 and different shapes. History supplies rankings, not numerical CE.

Δ=lr×T is nominal Adam exposure, not measured parameter displacement. R=GRU mean test CE / transformer mean test CE; R<1 favors GRU. Alpha scales transition logits. Equal hidden width is not parameter matching: d64×2 has 56,112 GRU versus 73,904 transformer parameters.

## Methods and Counts
Compared non-residual GRU64×2 against causal post-LN transformer64×2, heads4/d_ff128, learned positions, default family initialization. Shared Adam betas(.9,.999), weight decay0, cross-entropy, batch16; T256/1024; lr=Δ/T. Both datasets have V48/L12, train800/test200 windows and a fixed transition law shared across splits.

Datasets: alpha=.8 `bg_5ea2a7`, sequence/table seeds 148064170/148074170; alpha=1.4 `bg_424569`, seeds 100098864176/100098874176. There are **two datasets, one per alpha**. Alpha and table/sequence seeds remain confounded.

Main grid: 48 configurations, 24 comparisons, 478 finite seed metrics. Ten seeds were scheduled per configuration; all have n=10 except alpha=.8 transformer Δ=.01 at both budgets, n=9. Numeric seed identities and per-seed losses are absent from experiment artifacts; paired covariance is not assumed.

Overall: 11 saved experiment artifacts; 81 distinct evaluated configurations, 810 scheduled seed evaluations, 71 failures, 739 finite seed metrics and 75 finite configuration summaries. Two cached repeats are not replications. Another 24 configurations failed during vocabulary-mismatched dataset transfer and supplied no seed counts or CE.

Predictions were published before experiments: dataset-dependent crossover, possible GRU calibration advantage, imperfect equal-Δ budget equivalence, and shape confounding. Initialization interventions were unavailable through exposed lab fields. Near-initial probes used lr=1e-12.

## Uncertainty
Intervals combine marginal bounds mean±2.8×SD/√n and divide their endpoints. For n≥9, these are conservative ≥95% pointwise intervals under approximately normal independent model-seed losses, without paired covariance. They exclude between-dataset uncertainty, test-window resampling and simultaneous correction. Failed-seed cells describe survivors only.

Alpha=.8 R intervals, T256/T1024: Δ=.02 [.983761,.990488]/[.984040,.990676]; .0256 [.988251,.994480]/[.988642,.994701]; .04 [.995666,1.000556]/[.996223,1.000792]; .05 [.998416,1.002899]/[.999029,1.003175]; .064 [1.000660,1.005290]/[1.001383,1.005720]. ## Explanations and Predictions
Calibration remains unverified. Near-initial GRU64×2 CE is 3.876151±.002637 SD at alpha=.8 and 3.874870±.004239 at alpha=1.4, n10 each, close to ln48=3.871201. All transformer near-initial probes failed10/10, including the T256 retry. No valid initial CE gap, logit variance or equal-head intervention was measured.

Transition fitting is consistent with the growing transformer advantage on the stronger-law dataset, but table/sequence variation, sample information, parameter counts, positions and failure selection are competing explanations. No mechanism is established.

Falsifiable extrapolations: on additional allowed V48/L12 datasets at d64×2, Adam Δ=.03/T256, predict R<1 for alpha=.8 and R>1 for alpha=1.4; reliable opposite signs refute transfer. At alpha=1.4/.256, predict transformer superiority at equal d32 but not uniformly at d128. Approved equal/zero-head initialization should substantially reduce the low-Δ GRU edge if calibration dominates; persistence of the alpha-dependent edge would support transition fitting. These conditions remain unmeasured.

## Conditional Claim
Bigram LM: no universal Delta=.02 GRU/post-LN switch (Adam Delta=lr*T). Default-init V48/L12, Adam, batch16, d64x2, T256/1024: alpha.8 GRU has lower mean CE at .01/.02/.0256/.04 (8/12), transformer at .05/.064; .04/.05 uncertain. Alpha1.4: GRU only at .01 (2/12). Only 2 datasets, one per alpha; alpha/table effects confounded. At Delta=.256, T256, alpha1.4, equal d32 favors transformer, d64 is uncertain, d128 favors GRU. Width/depth matter; older .1-.5 GRU8/9 is not width-independent. Initialization calibration is untested; tiny-lr transformer probes failed10/10. Do not infer a replacement cutoff.
