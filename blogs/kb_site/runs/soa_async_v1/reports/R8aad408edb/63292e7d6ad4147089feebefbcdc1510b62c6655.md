# Exact GRU optimizer recipes reverse across allowed bigram datasets
## Conclusion
At V48/L24,T512/b32, the exact focal recipe ranking reverses across independent allowed tables/splits: AdamW wins the mean on four alpha.8 settings; RMSprop wins on four alpha1.0 settings. Six development signs match the preregistration, and two held-back mean-sign predictions succeed. Three alpha.8 margins are small, including two with seed intervals crossing zero. This supports dataset-associated recipe reversal, not optimizer-class or causal alpha superiority.

## Competing Explanations and Boundaries
The exact recipes may have dataset-dependent finite-budget trajectories; the observed budget reversal distinguishes this from a budget-invariant recipe ranking. Table/split-specific sampling remains an alternative to causal alpha, and within-alpha replication does not remove that confounding. LR and optimizer defaults are bundled, so optimizer identity is not isolated.
No claim of overfitting, update noise, parameter displacement or convergence follows from ordinal gold. Even the measured RMSprop CE increase does not distinguish those mechanisms without training-loss, gradient or motion diagnostics. No extrapolation to other architectures,losses,LRs,batches,budgets,vocabularies or alpha values is warranted.
Next discriminating tests: authenticated same-table alpha intervention; independent sequence splits on one table; additional allowed tables near entropy3.495 with predictions frozen beforehand. No unsupported dataset mutations were used. Reliable sign at small alpha.8 margins and the causal role of alpha remain unresolved.
