# Slow Plain SGD: Final Loss, Progress and Transfer
## Finding
The proposed GRU64x1/x2-over-TF32/64 rule for every `0<lr*T<=.8` is not validated: fresh equal-width cells produce two mean-sign counterexamples, both statistically unresolved. Duration preserves the signs; context/dataset transfer does not preserve all of them. Lower final CE must not be confused with greater progress.

G1/G2 are nonresidual GRU64x1/x2; TF1/TF4 are causal post-LN GELU TF64x1/x4, heads2/FF128, learned positions, dropout0. Default family initialization, biased linear heads and plain token-mean CE were retained. Equal width is not parameter matching. Plain SGD uses momentum0/wd0, b16, T512/2048, `Delta=lr*T` .2/.6/.8. T512 rates are .000390625/.001171875/.0015625; T2048 rates are one quarter. Budgets see 8,192/32,768 windows, not equal duration or token exposure. Delta is nominal exposure, not measured displacement or optimizer equivalence.
Define **M=CE_TF−CE_GRU**, positive favoring GRU, and **P=CE0−CE_final**. Then **M_final=M0−(P_TF−P_GRU)**. No-update SGD lr0/wd0 supplies initialization-equivalent CE. Seeds0–9 are repeatability blocks, not identical weights/minibatches. Pointwise 95% CIs use paired differences and t9=2.26216; conservative marginal-SD sensitivity is saved. ## Tests, Failures and Boundaries
**46/48 final means favor GRU; 38/48 CIs wholly favor GRU; none wholly favor TF.** At L12/.8, G2/TF4 margins are −.00502565/T512 and −.00507865/T2048, both CIs crossing zero. The all-positive point prediction fails without establishing a decisive TF phase. At .2/.6 all 32 means favor GRU, but some .6 intervals cross zero; no continuous .6 cutoff is inferred.
All 24 duration-paired contrast signs persist; maximum mean margin shift is .000195694 nats. This is not a formal equivalence test. Retention `M_final/M0` ranges [−.03357,.76131] on L12 versus [.19600,.85260] on L24. Tables and sequence seeds cochange with context: transport heterogeneity, not a causal context effect.
**TF extra actual test progress is positive in all 48 means and paired95%CIs.** Thus progress and duration-sign predictions succeed even where final-rank predictions fail. Initial-gap decomposition is an accounting identity, not proof that initialization caused the rankings.
Matched Adam(.9,.999), wd0, Delta.0256/T512 on bg_04502b is a boundary only: G1/G2/TF1/TF4 CE=3.032721/3.086577/3.007964/2.940509. G1/TF4 M=−.092212, CI[−.110188,−.074235]; G1/TF1=−.024757[−.044638,−.004876]; G2/TF4=−.146068[−.162228,−.129908]. All four cross-family means/CIs favor TF. The preregistered TF4-over-G1 prediction succeeds. Momentum was not tested.
There are 60 valid configurations, 600 finite seed evaluations and zero reported valid-run seed failures. Fourteen invalid L24 TF requests inherited L12 positional tables and raised IndexError; no finite outcomes or seed-failure counts were returned. TF-only corrections explicitly set context24 without repeating valid GRU training. One name-key rejection supplied no measurements.
One L24 G1 no-update baseline was inadvertently recomputed because its unused GRU context attribute differed from the surviving specification; it receives **no independent replication credit**. This deviates from the no-repeat instruction. Old numerical grids were recovered from frozen artifacts, not rerun. Remaining valid new cells are uncached; cache repeats are never independent evidence.
## Interpretation and Unresolved Tests
The tested phase is conditional final-gap retention under exact recipes, not GRU-first or a universal `lr*T` law. No widened ≤.8 law is promoted. Old68/68 supports old cells, not unseen heads, FFs, decay or contexts. K1075 adaptive counterexamples remain separate; consolidation does not erase incompatible optimizer scopes.
q_43b634 uses L12/T2048/b16, SGD.0003/wd.001, Delta.6144, GRU64x2 versus TF64 depths1–4, heads2/4, FF64/256. This wd0/heads2/FF128 audit is **not its exact recipe or a complete-order replay**. The final-loss-versus-progress reasoning error is corrected; no focal numerical ranking or mechanism is inferred.
Competing explanations include initial predictive dispersion/bias, gradient conditioning, depth-dependent optimization, positions, gating, capacity and table/split variation. Initial CE and ignored head-scale/position fields do not establish causal calibration. Verified calibration interventions, train endpoints, actual updates and same-table context controls remain unavailable and unresolved.
Prospective unmeasured test: next eligible L12/alpha1 table bg_0915b4, identical heads2/FF128, SGD wd0/b16/Delta.2/T2048: predict all four M>0 and TF extra test progress>0. At .8 no universal sign is predicted. Opposite means refute the narrow point prediction; opposite CIs provide stronger falsification. This is not a newly validated law.
