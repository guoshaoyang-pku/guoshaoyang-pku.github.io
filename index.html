Why Do Conventional World Models Fail to Learn Cellular Automata?

Shaoyang Guo*, Ziming Liu†

Can conventional world models learn exact world dynamics from observed history? Using cellular automata as a testbed, the answer is no in many cases: a CNN predicts 96.3% of cells but completes only 18.9% of rollouts; a joint diffusion model completes none.

arXiv Code
arXiv Preprint · 2026

Forking: Sudden Overfitting Under Replay

Shanbin Yu*, Shaoyang Guo*, Haoran Zhao, Danni Yu, Ziming Liu†

Forking is a generalization failure discovered in NanoGPT autoresearch: under data replay, models with an over-encoding representation suddenly overfit along a different branch instead of continuing to generalize.

arXiv Code
arXiv Preprint · 2026

ArchitectureIQ: On the Measure of Training Intuition

Zirui Ren, Shaoyang Guo, Chencheng Tang, et al.

A benchmark for model intuition: each question presents a synthetic dataset and several training recipes, and the test-taker predicts which recipe wins. Frontier models reach ~76% (random 33%) against a best human 66% — good, but far from perfect.
